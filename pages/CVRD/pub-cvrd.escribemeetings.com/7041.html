R3 Report from the Arts & Culture Division Re: 2023 Budget Amendment for Function 426 – CPAC Platform Lift Replacement It was moved and seconded that it be recommended to the Board on November 8, 2023 that the 2023 budget for Function 426 – Cowichan Performing Arts Centre be amended for the inclusion of the $40,000 emergency capital purchase of a vertical platform lift, funded by the Capital Improvement Fund. MOTION CARRIED R4 Report from the General Manager, Land Use Services Department Re: Regional Growth Management COMMITTEE OF THE WHOLE MINUTES – WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 8, 2023 PAGE 4 It was moved and seconded that it be recommended to the Board on November 8, 2023: 1. 2. 3. That staff organize a workshop for the CVRD Board on the topic of regional planning and growth management, including a presentation by the Province of BC on regional growth strategies; That municipal councillors be invited to participate; and That any costs incurred be expensed to Function 100 General Government. MOTION CARRIED R5 Report from the Recycling & Waste Management Division Re: CVRD Bylaw No. 4526 – Solid Waste Fees and Regulations Amendment Bylaw, 2023 It was moved and seconded that "CVRD Bylaw No. 4526 - Solid Waste Fees and Regulations Amendment Bylaw, 2023", be considered for three readings and adoption. It was moved and seconded that the motion be amended to add the wording “That the use of the weigh scales at the CVRD recycling facilities stay at the current tipping fee of $7.00”. MOTION CARRIED Voting resumed on the main motion as amended It was moved and seconded that it be recommended to the Board on November 8, 2023 that "CVRD Bylaw No. 4526 - Solid Waste Fees and Regulations Amendment Bylaw, 2023", be considered for three readings and adoption and that the use of the weigh scales at the CVRD recycling facilities stay at the current tipping fee of $7.00. MOTION DEFEATED It was moved and seconded that it be recommended to the Board on November 8, 2023 that "CVRD Bylaw No. 4526 - Solid Waste Fees and Regulations Amendment Bylaw, 2023", be considered for three readings and adoption.
