QUESTION PERIOD The Question Period is an opportunity to offer the public to ask a question regarding the business of CVRD. The Question Period allows for an allotment of 15 minutes and is comprised of two parts. The first is reserved for questions directly related to agenda items. If there is time remaining, questions may be on a matter of public interest related to and in the CVRD jurisdiction. Each questioner is permitted to ask one question, plus one follow-up question related to the answer. The Question must truly be a question, and not a statement of opinion, or speech. Questioners must avoid personal references, insinuations, violent, offensive, or disrespectful remarks about another person; and unparliamentary language. 20. The sale of monthly passes would be discontinued to coincide with the launch of UMO to ensure no duplication. No dayPASS rate is available for the CVC or NCX and discounted rates are only currently available through monthly passes (to be replaced by a 30-day pass) or tickets. Project and Solution Overview In 2023, BC Transit will enter the next phase of its Electronic Fare Collection System project by introducing the Umo fare technology platform in 30 transit systems across the province. Importantly, cash will remain as a payment option for those riders that choose or need to use it. On the bus, new fare validators will be installed, and riders will present their mobile app or reloadable smart card for fare verification. In the near future, riders will also be able to tap their credit or debit card onboard to pay their fare when boarding. For BC Transit and its local government partners, Umo will be a new source of data on fare usage and transit ridership that will be used to inform future recommendations on fare policy and service delivery.
