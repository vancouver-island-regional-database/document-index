COUNCIL STRATEGIC PRIORITIES 2016 to 2019 January 26, 2016 City Manager Ruth Malli The Process (How We Got Here)  Review of existing plans  Current local, regional and broader economics  Emerging issues, current and future needs  Legislated responsibilities and requirements The Strategic Priorities The 2016 Priorities  Support Investment in the Downtown Core. Continue to provide tax incentives for building investment. Complete sidewalk and crosswalk projects and install amenities.  Welcome Visitors. Continue to direct business licence revenue to fund the Visitor Information Centre at the Ladysmith Chamber of Commerce location. Enhance facilities at Transfer Beach. Complete improvements to expand Aggie Hall parking. The 2016 Priorities  Ladysmith is Open for Business and Development Investment. Engage with the development community to streamline application processes and implement best practices for community growth and investment.  Invest in the Waterfront. Engage with the community to update the Waterfront Area Plan. The 2016 Priorities (cont’d)  Protection of the Watershed. Establish a Watershed Roundtable with community partners. Complete terms of reference and focus areas, such as signage and security.  Invest in Infrastructure to Protect the Environment, and Provide for Sustainable Services in the Future. Complete Waste Water Treatment Plant to secondary treatment, evaluate and implement options for recycling bio-solids, upgrade the fueling system at Public Works, and initiate water system improvements to provide for adequate and safe drinking water to meet the present and future needs of the Town and its neighbours. The 2016 Priorities (cont’d)  Water Conservation. Continue to provide incentives to decrease water consumption and participate in regional water conservation education campaign.  Working Together with Stz’uminus First Nation. Continue Naut’sa Mawt Steering Committee Meetings and co-host Council Community to Community event to prepare update to Memorandum of Understanding with SFN. Commence next phase of service agreements (building inspection, water and sewer). The 2016 Priorities (cont’d)  Leverage Infrastructure Funding. Prepare project design plans to leverage infrastructure grants from senior governments for infrastructure renewal and expansion. Develop asset management strategy and necessary I.T. related infrastructure to protect investment and enhance infrastructure funding success.  Working Together to Address Community Concerns. Resolve nuisance property concerns through a multi-agency approach. Establish a Bar Watch Program, with Good Neighbour Agreements. The 2016 Priorities (cont’d)  Sustainable West Coast Town. Continue to encourage development of green industries. Continue to implement the Sustainability Action Plan.  Prompt Communication. Expand use of technology to deliver timely information while continuing to utilize traditional communication methods.  Working Together with our Neighbours. Renegotiate water service agreement with Diamond Water District.  Transparency. Install technology for live- streaming and archiving of Council meetings. What Is Next?  Council approves funding, which determines the work  Work plans are prepared by staff  Council continues to use the Strategic Priorities as a decision making guide  Results, progress through quarterly reports and Annual Report Financial Plan Overview  Minimal operational change 2.17%  Service level considerations: any changes?  Selection of capital projects?
