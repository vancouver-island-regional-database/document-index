DELEGATION Bryan Henderson of the Ladysmith and District Arts Council made a presentation to Council regarding preparations for the Islands Celtic Festival 2011, to be hosted by the Ladysmith and District Arts Council on April 29 and 30. He requested that Council consider approving the following requests: 1. To be an event partner, and offer partner pricing on Town facility rentals and the use of the Town vehicle for the event 2. To permit parking of recreational vehicles in the vicinity of the Machine Shop. 3. To provide Public Works staff and a truck to move a stage from Nanaimo to Ladysmith and back. Council agreed to consider these requests under New Business. MINUTES 2010-439 It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes of the October 4, 2010 Council Meeting be adopted as circulated.
