REPORTS 2010-218 2010 – 2014 FINANCIAL PLAN The City Manager, R. Malli, circulated two tax rate options for Council’s consideration. Tax Increase s Industrial Commercial Residential Option 1 4.65% 3.75% 5.99% Option 2 0% 2.96% 5.99% (Over all increase of 4.45%) Option 2 would require the use of $50,000 from the Town’s allocated surplus. Discussion ensued regarding the options as presented. It was moved, seconded and carried that Council approve Option 2 of the 2010-2014 Financial Plan which includes the transfer of $50,000 from allocated surplus.
