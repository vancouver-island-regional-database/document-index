OFFICIAL COMMUNITY PLAN/ZONING BYLAW APPLICATIONS a. Zoning Bylaw Amendment Application 3360-09-01 - 11 & 17 Warren Street (Pabla) Planner Brinkman introduced the rezoning application for 11 and 17 Warren Street. The applicant’s designer Mich ael Ker answered questions from APC members and presented information rega rding the proposed design of the mixed use building. It was moved, seconded and carried that th e Advisory Planning Commission recommends to Council approval of rezoning 3360-09-01 (P abla) on the condition that the developer achieve the following: • Develop an environmentally friendly parking area; • Manage all stormwater on-site; • Every effort shall be made to re-use the existing dwelling on-site by moving it to another location and/or to re-use the building’s wood; and • Create a separate strata corporation for the commercial and residential uses; The APC members generally stated that they did not support the design of the building as presented. The APC members commented that the information provided by the designer did not clearly demonstrate the proposed building and they would have liked a better illustrated rendering.
