http://www.ladysmith.ca/docs/adp-apc-documents/2012-10-10-apc-minutes.pdf October 27, 2016 MINUTES Advisory Planning Commission (APC) Wednesday, October 10, 2012 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall PRESENT: Chair – John Wilson; Members – Katherine Turner, Todd Hancock, Tina Donovan, Pamela Fraser; Council Liaison – Gord Horth; Staff Liaison – Lisa Brinkman, Planner and Recorder. ABSENT/REGRETS: Evelyn Kaatz GUESTS: Bob Wall (R.W. (Bob) Wall Ltd.), Project Manager; Garry and Carol Wagenaar, Property Owners The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda of October 10, 2012 be approved. 2. NOMINATION/ELECTION OF CHAIR K. Turner nominated J. Wilson for the position of chair of the APC. J. Wilson accepted the nomination. There were no further nominations. J. Wilson was acclaimed to the position of APC Chair. 3. ADOPTION OF MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the APC minutes of February 13, 2012 and the Joint Commissions minutes of August 9, 2012 be adopted. 4. COUNCIL REFERRALS See 5a. 5. OFFICIAL COMMUNITY PLAN (OCP)/ZONING BYLAW PROPOSED AMENDMENTS a. Zoning Amendment Application 3360-12-02 1030 Oyster Bay Drive (R.W. (Bob Wall) Ltd.) Project Manager, B. Wall, presented the rezoning proposal to add uses to the light industrial zone for the subject property. Planner L. Brinkman provided a summary of Official Community Plan policy that relates to the proposal. APC members discussed the proposed rezoning and asked questions of the applicant. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Planning Commission recommends approval of adding the additional uses listed for this site and this site only with the following concerns: Industrial zoning should be protected Parking shortages Diminishing downtown Road access; and Drive thru fast food is not supported.
