AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of March 24, 2014 be approved. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of December 16, 2013 be approved. 3. ZONING BYLAW PROJECT – NEW DEVELOPMENT PERMIT GUIDELINES a. Review of Draft Development Permit Area Guidelines for DPA-3 – Commercial, DPA-4 – Multi-Unit Residential and DPA-5 - Industrial L. Brinkman provided an introduction to the Draft Development Permit Area guidelines for DPA-3 – Commercial, DPA-4 – Multi-Unit Residential and DPA -5 – Industrial. R. Kinney explained an approach for review and analysis of the draft document. The members reviewed the draft document and the following themes emerged to improve the DPA 3, 4, and 5:  Ensure the DPA objectives and guidelines are tailored specifically for Ladysmith;  Ensure the guidelines are realistic from an economic perspective;  Include guidelines for hillside and sloping sites;  Improve the clarity of several of the guidelines and explain what is meant by the term ‘should’;  Avoid the use of specific numbers and measurements as they are not appropriate for a guideline; and  Improve several of the images/photos and consider that buildings have 5 sides (including the roof); It was moved, seconded, and carried that the following amendments be made to the Draft DPA 3, DPA 4, and DPA 5 Development Permit guideline document: a) Amend DPA 3 – Commercial  Delete s ection 4(b) Mansard roofs are discourage d, and should not be permitted on buildings of less than four storeys in height. 2  Delete s ection 8(c) Strongly contrasting, inten se, bright, and/or day -glow colours should be used for accent only, and should not exceed a maximum of 10% of the façade area. b) Amend DPA 4 – Multi-Unit Residential  Delete portions of section 5(a) so that is states: Townhouses should have separate, street-oriented entrances that express strong unit identity.  Delete section 8(d) Stucco, vinyl siding, and aluminum siding are not supported as acceptable building cladding materials. c) Amend DPA 5 – Industrial  Delete section 2(c) Buildings should orient frontages towards the street.  Delete section 2(f) Buildings on a corner parcel should orient frontages towards both streets. S. Arnett left the meeting at 2:55 pm. 4. NEW BUSINESS None. 5. MONTHLY BRIEFING None. 6. NEXT MEETING – TBD 7.
