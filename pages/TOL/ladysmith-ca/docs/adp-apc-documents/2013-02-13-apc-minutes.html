ADOPTION OF MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the APC minutes of December 12 , 2012 be adopted. 3. COUNCIL REFERRALS See 4a. 4. OFFICIAL COMMUNITY PLAN (OCP)/ZONING BYLAW PROPOSED AMENDMENTS a. OCP Amendment and Rezoning Application 3360-13-01 Lots 7 and 8, District Lot 147, Plan VIP85271 (1201 & 1251 Christie Road) APC members discussed the proposed rezoning. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Planning Commission recommends support for rezoning 1201 and 1252 Christie Road to permit light industrial use. However, the Advisory Planning Commission has the following concerns that they wish to see addressed:  Noise and odour emissions;  Improve the safety for cyclists and pedestrians (i.e. sidewalk or protected path) on Christie Road and the Trans Canada Highway;  Carefully consider retaining walls and slope stability;  Carefully consider how drainage is managed;  Concern regarding the potential view of a large flat asphalt roof from adjacent residential lots; and  This is th e gateway to Ladysmith, thus site and architectural design should be carefully considered. 5. CORRESPONDENCE None. 6. NEW BUSINESS Advisory Planning Commission February 13, 2013 2 None. 7. MONTHLY BRIEFING Planner L.
