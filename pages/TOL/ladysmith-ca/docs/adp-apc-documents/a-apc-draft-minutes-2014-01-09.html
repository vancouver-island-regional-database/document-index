ABSENT/REGRETS: Council Liaison – Gord Horth GUESTS: Applicant – Luke Kolk The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda of January 9, 2014 be approved. 2. WELCOME, INTRODUCTION AND ORIENTATION Meeting members introduced themselves. Planner L. Brinkman welcomed APC members and provided an orientation of the information provided in the Advisory Planning Commission binders to the new members. 3. NOMINATION/ELECTION OF CHAIR T. Hancock nominated P. Fraser for the position of chair of the APC. P. Fraser accepted the nomination. There were no further nominations. P. Fraser was acclaimed to the position of APC Chair. 4. ADOPTION OF MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the APC minutes of August 27, 2013 be adopted. 5. COUNCIL REFERRALS See 6a. 6. OFFICIAL COMMUNITY PLAN (OCP)/ZONING BYLAW PROPOSED AMENDMENTS a. OCP Amendment and Rezoning Application 3360-13-05 Lot A, District Lot 43, Oyster District, Plan EPP 12594 As Shown on Plan EPP12595 (Holland Creek Place), Thomas Tremble L. Brinkman presented the OCP and rezoning amendment application 3360 -13-05 for a proposed 8 bare -land strata lots on Holland Creek Place. APC members asked questions and discussed the proposed application. The Advisory Planning Commission (APC) recommends support for the application 3360-13-05 to permit eight single family bare-land strata lots at Lot A, District Lot 43, Oyster District, Plan EPP12594 subject to the following conditions:  That the site plan include an area for a minimum of four visitor parking stalls;  That signage restrict parking to allow for emergency vehicle turn-around in the area designated; and  The APC would like to ensure the Town has the authority to enforce covenants as registered in connection with the development. 7. CORRESPONDENCE None. 8. NEW BUSINESS None. 9. MONTHLY BRIEFING Planner L. Brinkman provided information regarding the boundary expansion application from Couverdon and information regarding the next steps for the new zoning bylaw. 10. NEXT MEETING The next meeting is scheduled for Thursday, February 13, 2014 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall. 11. ADJOURNMENT It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 8:50 p.m. ____________________________________ Chair (P.
