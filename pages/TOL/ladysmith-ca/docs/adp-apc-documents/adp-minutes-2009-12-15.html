http://www.ladysmith.ca/docs/adp-apc-documents/adp-minutes-2009-12-15.pdf October 27, 2016 MINUTES Advisory Design Panel (ADP) Tuesday, December 15, 2009 at 1:00 p.m. Boardroom, Development Services Office LADYSMITH PRESENT: Chair – Brian Bancroft; Members – Ron Kinney, Brian Childs, Kaien Shimizu; Council Liaison – Bruce Whittington and Alternate Council Liaison Jillian Dashwood; Planner and Recorder – Lisa Brinkman. GUESTS: Consultant - Maureen Pilcher; Designer - Will Melville; Owner - Curt Kerns. The meeting was called to order at 1:04 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of December 15, 2009 be approved. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of the October 13 2009 meeting be adopted. 3. COUNCIL REFERRALS See 4a. 4. DEVELOPMENT APPLICATION REFERRALS a. OCP / Zoning Amendment Application 3360-09-08 1237 – 4th Avenue – Lot 1, District Lot 97, Oyster District, Plan VIP84065 – Maureen Pilcher Planner, L. Brinkman provided a summary of the development proposal for 48 residential units at 1237 4th Ave. Advisory Design Panel members requested copies of the arborist report, preliminary landscape statement, and riparian area assessment report. Designer, W. Melville and Owner, C. Kerns provided information regarding the proposed design of the site. Consultant, M. Pilcher presented a ‘tree assessment map’. Items discussed by ADP members are as follows: • Desire to see a site plan with actual protected trees identified; • Ensure fire chief has reviewed the plans to review fire protection strategies onsite; • Importance of providing access to lands beyond to maximize flexibility for future land use opportunities; • Discussed protection of riparian area on lot 2. Owner, C. Kerns indicated that he is committed to permeable paving onsite. Advisory Design Panel December 15, 2009 2 It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Design Panel advises Council that it supports the proposal (3360-09-08) with the proviso that the future use of Lot 2, Plan VIP84065 (owner’s parcel containing Rocky Creek riparian area) is consistent with the overall plan as presented (delinea design consultants Ltd.-Site Plan-September 10, 2009). 5. NEW BUSINESS None. 6. MONTHLY BRIEFING None. 7. NEXT MEETING – Tuesday, January 19, 2010 at 1:00 p.m. – Council Chambers, City Hall. 8. ADJOURNMENT It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 2:20 p.m. __________________________________ Chair (B.
