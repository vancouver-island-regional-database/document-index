http://www.ladysmith.ca/docs/adp-apc-documents/adp-minutes-2011-02-16.pdf October 27, 2016 MINUTES Advisory Design Panel (ADP) Wednesday, February 16, 2011 at 1:00 p.m. Boardroom, Development Services Office LADYSMITH PRESENT: Chair – Brian Bancroft; Members – Kaien Shimizu, Brian Childs; Council Liaison – Bruce Whittington; HRAC Member – Peter Blumel; Planner and Recorder– Lisa Brinkman ABSENT: Ron Kinney GUESTS: Kevin Lamont, Project Facilitator; Kelti Chamberlain, Landscape Architect; Jerry Ellins, Architect The meeting was called to order at 1:05 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of February 16, 2011 be approved. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of August 24, 2010 be adopted. 3 . C O U N C I L R E F E R R A L S None. 4. DEVELOPMENT APPLICATION REFERRALS a. Development Permit Application #3060-11-01 Oak Development Ltd. (Russ Jackson) 19 Gatacre Street Planner, L. Brinkman, provided an introduction of the proposed mixed use building at 19 Gatacre Street. It was noted that the commercial area of the building is 177.35 square metres and that eight parking stalls are required onsite (clarifying the staff report). Project Facilitator, K. Lamont presented information regarding the proposed building and site plan. A review and discussion was held examining the proposal. The following comments were provided: • Balcony should be constructed of cedar and should not contain glass panels. • Quality workmanship and quality of materials is essential to ensure an attractive structure that the Town can be proud of for the next 100 years. • It is unclear how the grade will be treated at the rear. • Align the windows and doors (or increase door height) at the rear elevation. • A good-neighbour fence may be appropriate (at side lot lines) in the rear. • Cornice at front and rear require improved materials and design. • Rear parking area should be improved to provide better flow and connection between the lane and the business at the rear – (consider brick pavers for permeability, heritage look, and more appealing for pedestrian access). • Location needed for recyclable materials in rear area. • Consider a gabled roof – no false front. • The fancy wood door does not fit well with vinyl windows. • Two washrooms may be required at main level for two businesses. Advisory Design Panel February 16, 2011 2 • Building style is suitable for a main commercial street – perhaps not Gatacre Street. • The texture of the façade and windows requires improvement. • Canopies at front should be included for rain protection. • Siding should be a wood based product. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advi sory Design Panel (ADP) advises Council that it supports the concept of the commercial/residential building at 19 Gatacre Street subject to the applicant addressing the general issues identified at the February 16, 2011 ADP meeting relating to front and rear façade appearance as well as the design and function of the rear parking area. b. OCP & ZBL Amendment application #3360-09-01 Satpal Pabla – 11 & 17 Warren Street (Jerry Ellins – Architect) Planner, L. Brinkman, provided an introduction of the proposed rezoning at 11 and 17 Warren Street. Architect, J. Ellins and Landscape Architect, K. Chamberlain presented the proposed plans for a 14 unit residential building (with one comm ercial unit). A colour and material board was submitted. A review and discussion was held examining the proposal. It was suggested that the mullions be removed from the windows. Concern was ex pressed regarding the traffic and hours of operation of the proposed Laundromat commercial unit. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advi sory Design Panel (ADP) advises Council that it supports the rezoning to a 14 unit residential buildi ng and supports the proposed designs by J. Ellins (dated Dec. 2010). 5. NEW BUSINESS None. 6. MONTHLY BRIEFING Planner, L. Brinkman provided the ADP with an update of the current projects in Town. 7. NEXT MEETING – Tuesday, March 15, 2011 at 1:00 p.m. – Council Chambers, City Hall. 8.
