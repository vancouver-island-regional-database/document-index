OFFICIAL COMMUNITY PLAN/ZONING BYLAW APPLICATIONS a. OCP / Zoning Amendment Application 3360-09-08 1237 4th Avenue – Lot 1, District Lot 97, Oyster District, Plan VIP84065 Maureen Pilcher Planner, L. Brinkman provided a summary of the development proposal for 48 residential units at 1237 4 th Ave. Consultant, M. Pilcher provided a rationale for the proposed development and provided information regarding the design of the site. APC members asked questions and received the following information: • The size of condo units may be in the range of 900-1100 square feet; • The units will be strata owned (possibility for rental units - not certain); • Site will include a public trail; • Storm water will be managed onsite as much as possible; • The flat roofs shown on the condo buildings and townhomes will be advantageous for water detention and absorption – • The development is intended to offer a variety of residential units such that some units may be appropriate for families; • The footprint of a proposed condo building is 20mx24m; • Proposed height of condo building is 10.5 metres; Advisory Planning Commission December 15, 2009 2 The Advisory Planning Commission recommends to Council support fo r an OCP amendment and rezoning that would permit 48 resident ial units at 1237 Fourth Ave. (3360-09-08) subject to the following conditions: 1. There is a maximum of 48 residential units on the subject lands; 2. That Lot 2, VIP Plan 84065 (containing the Rocky Creek riparian area) is dedicated to the Town; 3. That the plans submitted by Delinea Design Consultants Ltd. (September 10, 2009) are secured as part of the rezoning; and 4. That the Development Permit process be thorough to ensure design commitments are adhered to. And that Council be aware that the Advisory Planning Commission is concerned about the viability of the development project and would li ke to see the project constructed in a timely manner. Also, the Advisory Planning Commission would like the applicant to report to the Town about the strata corporation to ensure the corporation is structured to maximize fairness to all residents. 5.
