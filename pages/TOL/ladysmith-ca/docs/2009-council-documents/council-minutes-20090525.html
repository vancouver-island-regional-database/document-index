Town of Ladysmith Minutes of the Special Meeting of Council of the Town of Ladysmith held in Council Chambers at City Hall on Monday, May 25, 2009 at 4:00 p.m. Mayor Hutchins called the meeting to order at 4:05 p.m. 2009-311: It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda for the Special Council meeting for Monday, May 25, 2009 be approved as circulated. 2009 GRANTS IN AID APPLICATIONS Council reviewed the 2009 Grant in Aid applications and requested that staff provide further information concerning some of the applications before a final decision is made on the distribution of funds. R. Malli, City Manager, vacated Council Chambers at 4:57 p.m. 2009-312: It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 5:32 p.m. Director of Corporate Services (S. Bowden) CERTIFIED CORRECT: Mayor (R.
