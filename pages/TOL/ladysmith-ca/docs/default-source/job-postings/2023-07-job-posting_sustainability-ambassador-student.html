Per CUPE Agreement Article 15.02: “applicants for this position shall agree that in the event of a grievance regarding filling of this posting, interview and selection documentation shall be released to the union.” POSITION DESCRIPTION TITLE: SUSTAINABILITY AMBASSADOR DEPARTMENT: UTILITIES DIVISION: INFRASTRUCTURE SERVICES CATEGORY: UNION CUPE LOCAL 401 BAND: STUDENT GENERAL ACCOUNTABILITY Reporting to the Manager of Operations, the Student Sustainability Ambassador is responsible for providing water conservation, recycling, compost, and waste disposal education to residents and businesses, as well as providing irrigation audit services to the infrastructure services division. PRIMARY DUTIES AND ACCOUNTABILITIES o Monitor residential water use, identify water waste and distribute water conservation information. o Identify public events (festivals, community events) throughout the summer where public displays can be set up and hosted. o Regularly set up and host a public display to distribute water conservation and recycling information. o Identify community groups (e.g.: Eagles, LRCA, Clubs, strata councils etc.) that would be interested in a water conservation and recycling presentation; deliver presentations. o Conduct curbside recycling audits o Develop strategies and information to promote and educate the public on solid waste reduction and recycling o Collect, compile, and analyze qualitative and quantitative fixture and behavior survey data from businesses and/or residents. o Submit weekly reports on residential visits, public interaction and irrigation audits. o Work with various non-profit/non-governmental organizations to provide information for advertising or other information going out to the public. o Provide end-of-summer report to Infrastructure Services. o Maintain courteous and professional interactions with the public, user groups and Town employees. o Attends professional development training as directed. o Other related duties as required.
