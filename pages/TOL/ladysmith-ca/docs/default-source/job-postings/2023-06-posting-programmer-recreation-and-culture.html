The incumbent coordinates and implements a variety of programs and activities for the commun ity and assists with ongoing evaluation and modification of services as required. The Programmer - Recreation and Culture provides excellence in customer service while maintaining and developing partnerships with the objective of increasing community service awareness and participation. PRIMARY DUTIES AND ACCOUNTABILITIES o Develops, promotes, monitors, and evaluates programs and services related to recreation, culture, special events, and health and wellness. o Plans, organizes, schedules, trains and supervises recreation and culture staff including day camp leader positions. o Recruits contract instructors and service providers, and negotiates and executes contracts for recreation and culture programs. o Under the direction of the supervisor, submits deta iled budget information for review and approval. o Monitors and reports the status of budgets relating to areas of responsibility as required. o Works collaboratively with other programmers on program delivery and planning. o On a regular basis, directly leads p rograms and classes as it relates to recreation, culture including day camps. o Identifies staff requirements relating to training strategies making training recommendations as necessary. o Recommends changes to and maintains procedures related to area of responsibility. o Supports service delivery to align with the department and organizational strategic objectives. o Responsible for compliance with safe work practices and safety guidelines as established by the Town and WorkSafe BC. o Responsible for compliance of procedures and policies as established by the Town. o Enhances programs by working with regional, provincial, and national affiliates. o Purchases supplies relevant to departmental operations. o Participates and provides input into the department marketing plan. o Participates in the Emergency Social Services (ESS) Program in a supervisory role. o Assists coordination relating to Health and Safety activities for department. o Facilitates and attends various community meetings, providing reports as required.
