PROGRAMMER - RECREATION &amp; CULTURE 250.245.6400 / info@ladysmith.ca / www.ladysmith.ca 410 Esplanade MAIL PO Box 220, Ladysmith, BC V9G 1A2 GET CONNECTED Position Overview Reporting to the Supervisor Community Programs and Services, the Programmer - Recreation and Culture is responsible for developing and monitoring programs related to the areas of recreation, culture, special events, and health and wellness. The incumbent coordinates and imple- ments a variety of programs and activities for the community and assists with ongoing evaluation and modification of services as required. The Programmer - Recreation and Culture provides excellence in customer service while maintaining and developing partnerships with the objective of increasing community service awareness and participation. Qualifications • A diploma in recreation administration, leisure services management or relevant field, plus three (3) years’ recent (within the last five (5) years)/relevant employment experience or an equivalent combination of training and recent/relevant experience. • Minimum of two (2) years’ prior job-related experience or an equivalent combination of training and experience, and one (1) year in a supervisory capacity. • Valid standard first aid certification including CPR-C. • Successful completion of high five trainer certification (BAHFT). • Successful completion of Autism Awareness for Recreation Leaders course. • Satisfactory police information and vulnerable sector check. • Valid B.C. Driver’s License – Class 5.
