August 29, 2018 POSITION DESCRIPTION TITLE: PARKS MAINTENANCE WORKER - STUDENT DEPARTMENT: PARKS DIVISION: INFRASTRUCTURE SERVICES CATEGORY: UNION CUPE LOCAL 401 BAND: STUDENT SCHEDULE B GENERAL ACCOUNTABILITY Reporting to the Senior Parks Maintenance Worker the Student Parks Maintenance Worker is responsible for a variety of unskilled manual tasks and duties. The tasks and duties of the Student Parks Maintenance Worker position will not occur without direct guidance and are typically physical in nature, require the use of simple tools and equipment and, will normally be carried out under close and direct supervision. PRIMARY DUTIES AND ACCOUNTABILITIES o Operates equipment such as, weed eaters, trimmers, push/self propelled lawn mowers , and other similar equipment required in the operation of parks and boulevards. o Performs routine manual tasks including but not limited to; Clearing brush from roadways and ditches, planting and weeding and maintaining flowers, shrubs, trees, and planters in parks and on downtown streets, refuse and litter pickup, sweeping and cleanin g, unloading and moving of supplies, cutting, weeding, raking, and watering, boulevards and lawns, repairing fences, minor repairs to playgrou nd equipment and park structure s, painting structures, hand excavations and back-filling. o Adhere to safety policies and regulations as established by WorkSafe BC, the Occupational Health and Safety committee, the Town of Ladysmith and regional and municipal statutes. o Responsible to document various goods and materials upon removal from inventory to ensure current supply levels are reflected accurately. o Assists other personnel to carry out their responsibilities. o Assists in the development of safety protocols and efficient work practices. o Attends professional development training as directed. o Performs other related duties as required. REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS, KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ABILITY 1. Demonstrated ability to fulfill assigned duties in all weather conditions. 2. Valid Workplace Hazardous Material Information System (WHMIS) certificate. 3. Demonstrated knowledge of safe work procedures, practices and obligations. 4. Demonstrated proficiency in the safe operation of medium and light weight motorized vehicles and equipment, and various infrastructure services tools and light industrial equipment including but not limited to: o Zero Turn Mower o Small equipment 5. Demonstrated initiative with specific direction; completing tasks by removing barriers and locating necessary resources. 6. Demonstrated ability to communicate effectively in a wide variety of situations applying diplomacy and interpersonal skill to establish and maintain productive relationships. 7. Demonstrated high level of focus on internal and external client service continuously seeking ways to meet and exceed expectations. 8. Demonstrated ability to work cooperatively within a team and with Town employees, stakeholders and partners to achieve optimal results. 9. Demonstrates valuing diversity.
