TOWN OF LADYSMITH Celebrate our Present. Embrace our Future. Honour our Past. Minutes of the Public Art Committee (PAC) Wednesday, October 8, 2025 at 4:00 p.m. Frank Jameson Community Centre COMMITTEE MEMBERS PRESENT: STAFF PRESENT: Gordon Filewych Councillor Tricia McKay Don Stewart Kim Perry Emma Alsop REGRETS: GUESTS: John Edwards Kathleen Darby CALLTOORDERAND _ The meeting was called to order at 4:01 p.m. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT G. Filewych acknowledged with gratitude that the meeting was taking place on the unceded territory of the Stz’uminus First Nation. AGENDA 2025-016: That the Public Art Committee adopt the agenda as presented. Motion Carried MINUTES 2025-017: NEW BUSINESS NEXT MEETING ADJOURNMENT That the Public Art Committee approve the minutes of the May 22, 2025 meeting as presented. Motion Carried New Members Welcome G. Filewych welcomed new member Emma Alsop, representing the Ladysmith Little Theatre to the committee. Elect Chair and Co-Chair 2025-18: That the Public Art Committee moved that K. Darby would be Chair for the 2025-2026 term. Motion Carried 2026 Banner Theme 2025-19: That the Public Art Committee moved that the Banner Theme for 2026 be “A Future Imagined”.
