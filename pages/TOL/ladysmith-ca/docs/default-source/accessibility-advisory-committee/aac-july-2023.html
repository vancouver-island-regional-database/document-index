Embrance our Future. Honour our Past. www.ladysmith.ca GET CONNECTED Ladysmith Council is inviting applications from residents who would like to make Ladysmith a more accessible community. The T own’s newly formed Accessibility Advisory Committee (AAC) has six vacancies, each for a two-year term, ending September 30, 2025. The goal of this Committee is to assist the town in identifying barriers to individuals, and to advise the T own on how to remove and prevent such barriers. Members at large will be appointed by Council based on their experience and involvement related to accessibility issues. T o the extent possible, the membership should reflect the following: a. At least half of the Committee members appointed should be persons with disabilities or persons representing a disability-serving organization. b. At least one member appointed should be Indigenous. Applications can be dropped off at City Hall or emailed to cs@ladysmith.ca until August 30, 2023.
