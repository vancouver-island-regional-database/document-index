TOWN OF LADYSMITH Celebrate our Present. Embrace our Future. Honour our Past. MINUTES OF THE ACCESSIBILITY ADVISORY COMMITTEE MEETING WEDNESDAY, MAY 22, 2024 LOWER PROGRAM ROOM, FJCC MEMBERS PRESENT Diane Hobelaid, Chair Andy Hobelaid Councillor Amanda Jacobson Shaun McKenzie Joel Helland Christina Stephen REGRETS Jordan Herbison STAFF PRESENT Sue Bouma Richard Frost CALL TO ORDER AND Ms. Hobelaid, Chair, called the Accessibility Advisory Committee meeting ACKNOWLEDGEMENT - to order at 7:00 p.m., recognizing with gratitude that it was taking place on the unceded territory of the Stz&#x27;uminus First Nation. AGENDA APPROVAL Moved and seconded: That the agenda for the May 22, 2024 meeting of the Accessibility Advisory Committee be approved as presented. Motion carried. MINUTES APPROVAL Moved and seconded: That the minutes of the April 3, 2024 Accessibility Advisory Committee meeting be approved. Motion carried. ACCESSIBILITY PLAN The Committee discussed preferred aspects of the accessibility plans from DISCUSSION the City of Duncan, the City of Parksville, and the Municipality of North Cowichan. They underscored the importance of using the accessibility plan to move from a “back door approach” of offering accommodation, to an explicit and welcoming approach, where Universal Design is considered in the design or renovation of Town-owned public spaces. Staff advised that they would bring a draft compilation of the recommendations to the next meeting for the committee’s review. Accessibility Advisory Committee Meeting Minutes - May 22, 2024 Page 1
