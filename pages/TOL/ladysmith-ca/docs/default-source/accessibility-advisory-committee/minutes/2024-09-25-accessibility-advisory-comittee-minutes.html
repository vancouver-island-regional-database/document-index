TOWN OF LADYSMITH Celebrate our Present. Embrace our Future. Honour our Past. MINUTES OF THE ACCESSIBILITY ADVISORY COMMITTEE WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 25, 2024 LOWER PROGRAM ROOM, FJCCS MEMBERS PRESENT Diane Hobelaid, Chair Andy Hobelaid Councillor Amanda Jacobson Shaun McKenzie (via Zoom) Joel Helland (via Zoom) Christina Stephen Jordan Herbison (via Zoom) STAFF PRESENT Sue Bouma (Manager of Corporate Services) Nick Pescod (Communications &amp; Engagement Richard Frost (Manager of Facility Operations) Specialist) CALL TO ORDER AND ACKNOWLEDGEMENT AGENDA APPROVAL MINUTES APPROVAL UNIVERSAL DESIGN PRINCIPLES ACCESSIBILITY PLAN FRAMEWORK FOR REVIEW MANAGER OF FACILITY OPERATIONS Ms. Hobelaid, Chair, called the Accessibility Advisory Committee meeting to order at 7:08 p.m., recognizing with gratitude that it was taking place on the unceded territory of the Stz&#x27;uminus First Nation. Moved and seconded: That the agenda for the September 25, 2024 meeting of the Accessibility Advisory Committee be approved as presented. Motion carried. Moved and seconded: That the minutes of the May 22, 2024 Accessibility Advisory Committee meeting be approved. Motion carried. Diane Hobelaid, Chair, provided background information and a link to the Universal Design Principles from the Centre for Excellence and asked that the document be added as an addendum to the Accessibility Plan. The Committee agreed to bring suggestions regarding the Accessibility Plan framework to the next meeting, including recommendations to add, delete, rename or reorganize anything within the document. Richard Frost, Manager of Facility Operations, reviewed recently completed accessibility upgrades, such as the installation of an automatic door opener from the everyone change room to the pool deck and the replacement of push buttons on other automatic door openers with more accessible push plates.
