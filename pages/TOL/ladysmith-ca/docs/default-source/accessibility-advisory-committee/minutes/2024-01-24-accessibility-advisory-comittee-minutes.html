TOW NO F LADYSMITH Celebrate our Present. Embrace our Future, Honour our Past. MINUTES OF THE ACCESSIBILITY ADVISORY COMMITTEE MEETING WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 24, 2024 COUNCIL CHAMBERS, CITY HALL MEMBERS PRESENT Councillor Amanda Jacobson Diane Hobelaid Joel Helland( via Zoom) Shaun McKenzie (v/a Zoom) Jordan Herbison Christina Stephen Andy Hobelaid STAFF PRESENT Sue Bouma Mike Gregory CALL TO ORDER AND Ms. Bouma called the Accessibility Advisory Committee meeting to order at ACKNOWLEDGEMENT 7:00 p.m., recognizing with gratitude that it was taking place on the unceded territory of the Stz&#x27;uminus First Nation. AGENDA APPROVAL Moved and seconded: That the agenda for the January 24, 2024 meeting of the Accessibility Advisory Committee be approved as presented. Motion carried. INTRODUCTIONS Members of the Committee introduced themselves and communicated their connection to accessibility. PRESENTATION AND Staff made a presentation outlining accessibility legislation, the role of the TERMS OF REFERENCE Committee, and best practices for orderly meetings. The Accessibility REVIEW Advisory Committee Terms of Reference were also reviewed and discussed. ELECTION OF CHAIR Moved and seconded: That the Committee appoint Diane Hobelaid as Chair of the Accessibility Advisory Committee. Motion carried. DISCUSSION AND Staff provided copies of the Official Community Plan, the Accessibility QUESTIONS Advisory Committee Terms of Reference, and accessibility reviews of the Frank Jameson Community Centre and Transfer Beach. The Committee discussed potential alternate meeting locations. Staff will review the options and advise the committee.
