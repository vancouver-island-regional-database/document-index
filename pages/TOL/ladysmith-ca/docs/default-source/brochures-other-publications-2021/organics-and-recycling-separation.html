Managing Household Waste in Ladysmith COLLECTED ON ALTERNATING WEEKS Unlimited amounts (Items must be clean &amp; dry) Printed Paper:  Newspapers, inserts and flyers  Magazines and catalogues  Writing and home office paper, paper envelopes and notepads  Paper gift wrap and greeting cards  Telephone books and other directories Paper Packaging (clean &amp; dry):  Cardboard and boxboard boxes  Gable top juice and soy milk cartons, substitute eggs  Ice cream cartons (wax coated)  Milk and cream cartons  Paper bags &amp; pet food bags  Paper cups for beverages  Unsoiled pizza boxes  Frozen dessert boxes  Waxed cardboard  Moulded boxboard for egg cartons, take out beverage trays, paper-based flower pots  Tetra-pack (aseptic) boxes or cartons for dairy, milk-type beverages, soup, broth, sauces Plastic Containers (clean &amp; dry):  Plastic jugs and bottles  Plastic jars (wide mouth)  Plastic clamshell containers  Plastic pails (less than 25L)  Plastic trays  Plastic garden pots and trays  Plastic tubs and lids (beware of non- MMBC containers)  Hard plastic packaging for screws, picture hangers, etc.  Plastic cold drink cups with lids  Microwavable bowls and cups Metals (clean &amp; dry):  Empty aerosol containers used for food, air fresheners, shaving cream, deodorant, hairspray  Aluminum foil and foil containers  Spiral wound cardboard cans and metal lids for frozen juice, potato chips, cookie dough, coffee, nuts, baby formula, etc.  Cans and tins with lids What Goes Where?
