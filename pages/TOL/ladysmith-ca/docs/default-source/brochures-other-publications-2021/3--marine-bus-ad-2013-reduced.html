A perfect fit for Your m Arine Business. Ladysmith’s beautiful natural harbour, with its sheltered waters, is perfect for all kinds of recreational boats. our moderate climate, magnificant scenery, and abundant wildlife provide a year-round boater’s paradise. Ladysmith offers excellent marina facilities and business infrastructure: » the Ladysmith marina is owned by oak Bay marine Group. the marina’s 50’ to 92’ boathouses provide protection, and year-round access to the surrounding area. » the Ladysmith maritime society operates a community marina within walking distance to downtown including 900 feet of visitor dock space as well as a modern reception centre offering meeting space, showers, washrooms, laundry, and a pump-out service. » Ladysmith is also home to a recreational boat launch, and a government wharf. » the town offers two industrial parks and a variety of light industrial commercial space options with excellent waterfront and highway access and appropriate servicing. » Ladysmith is an award-winning business-friendly community.
