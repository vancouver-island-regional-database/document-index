SHARE YOUR VISION FOR LADYSMITH WHAT’S AN OCP? PROJECT TIMELINE HELP US UPDATE OUR OCP! At its heart, an Official Community Plan (OCP) is about managing land use and physical growth of the town. The OCP determines the type and location of homes, businesses, parks, public spaces, and other places. As a result, an OCP influences transportation and housing choices, greenhouse gas emissions, community character, health and equity, housing affordability, protection of ecological areas, resource management, and how much it costs to pay for Town infrastructure such a pipes and streets. The Town of Ladysmith sits within the unceded territories of the Stz’uminus First Nation, whose people have been the inhabitants and stewards of the lands and waters since Time Immemorial.
