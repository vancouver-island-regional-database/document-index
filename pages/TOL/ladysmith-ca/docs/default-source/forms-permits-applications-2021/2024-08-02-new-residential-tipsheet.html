250.245.6415 / ds@ladysmith.ca / www.ladysmith.ca BUILDING PERMIT TIP SHEET NEW RESIDENTIAL CONSTRUCTION Building a new home or a coach house? Undertaking a substantial residential renovation? This TIP SHEET will help you with your building permit application. If you have any questions or require any additional information or clarifications, please contact the Building Inspector. BUILDING PERMIT APPLICATIONS : Please ensure that ALL “must have” items are included with your Building Permit Application. This will help us to process all permit applications as quickly as possible. Incomplete applications will not be accepted. Review the requirements carefully. BUILDING PERMIT APPLICATION FORM • Building Permit Application Form • Don’t forget to include the Value of Construction, as this is used when we calculate your building permit fee. Building Permit Fees are paid when you PICK UP your permit. • Don’t forget to check the Zoning for your property to make sure you can meet the requirements. • Zoning Bylaw and Map • Historic Building Permit File Information - The Town may have a site survey or drawings from a previous building permit application. Some costs may apply. Records may be available after 1970 APPOINTMENT OF AGENT • Appointment of Agent Form BUSINESS LICENCING (required for all applications) • Ladysmith is part of two business licence programs that might be of help to you. Check your business licence to see if it includes Ladysmith. - Business Licence Form - Business Licence Program &amp; Options The General Contractor and Sub-Trades require a valid business licence for operating in Ladysmith CERTIFICATE OF TITLE • You can order these documents from a notary, lawyer or surveyor or online from BC Land Title &amp; Survey. HOME WARRANTY INSURANCE • Make sure you print the correct page from the website! Look for “Download form” before you print the document. • BC Housing Home Warranty 250.245.6415 / ds@ladysmith.ca / www.ladysmith.ca SITE SURVEY AND HEIGHT CALCULATIONS (required for all applications) • Information for Surveyors • Building siting is to be based on a survey prepared by a BC Land Surveyor (BCLS) DRIVEWAY ACCESS PERMIT APPLICATION • Driveway Access Permit Application • Please ensure that the driveway meets the Town’s specifications including the maximum grade of the driveway shall be 20% with a change in rise or fall not exceeding 6% for each horizontal 3.5 m. The rise or fall of the driveway shall begin 2 m behind the curb towards the property. Refer to the Town of Ladysmith Engineering Standard R10 – Typical Driveway Grades. • Ensure that a driveway location, length, width, elevations, parking area and profile are shown on your Site Survey CONSTRUCTION PLANS (required for all applications) • Provide your plans on plain white paper with a scale (1/4”=1’). • Guide for Seismic Design of Houses and Lateral Loads Information • Spatial Separation Requirements and Fire Response Time • Radon Rough-In Requirements Bulletin ENGINEERED TRUSS DOCUMENTS • Depending on the point loads and/or spans, a Structural Engineer may be required. ENGINEERED FLOOR SYSTEM LAYOUTS AND BEAMS • Depending on the point loads and/or spans, a Structural Engineer may be required. PLUMBING ISOMETRIC DRAWING • A plumbing isometric drawing shows the plumbing design of the drain, waste, and venting (DWV) system.
