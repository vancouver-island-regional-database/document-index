SITE SURVEY AND HEIGHT CALCULATIONS Building siting is to be based on a survey prepared by a BC Land Surveyor (BCLS). On the survey drawing (also called a location certiﬁ cate) the following information must be provided: • Building envelope which means an illustration of the minimum required setbacks from all property lines for the Zone that your property is in. Setback information can be found in the Zoning Bylaw. • The location of all buildings (existing and proposed) with setbacks from property lines • Rights of way, easements, covenant areas, Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area (SPEA) which is usually associated with a watercourse on or near the property, if any • A note or chart including: Zone that your property is in (refer to the Zoning Bylaw Map) Proposed building height (m) Maximum permitted building height (see the Zoning Bylaw) Average natural grade (as deﬁ ned in the Zoning Bylaw) Average ﬁ nished grade (as deﬁ ned in the Zoning Bylaw) Parcel size (m2) Parcel coverage (%) (calculated as per the Zoning Bylaw) • Proposed retaining wall height, if any. A building permit is needed if the height is over 1.2 metres (4 feet). (Refer to the Zoning Bylaw for height regulations.)
