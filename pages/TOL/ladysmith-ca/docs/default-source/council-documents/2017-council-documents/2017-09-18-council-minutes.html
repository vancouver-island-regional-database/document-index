DELEGATION Gerry Yellowlees, Ladysmith Air Cadet Leader and Kae Griffin Duke of Edinburgh Award - Air Cadets Bronze Award Certificate Mr. Yellowlees introduced Kae Griffin, recipient of the Duke of Edinburgh award, and invited him to provide a summary of the time investment and accomplishments that led to his award. Mayor Stone presented the Duke of Edinburgh certificate to Mr. Griffin. Council commended Mr. Griffin for his achievements and thanked him for his work in the community. PROCLAMATIONS International Day of Older Persons Mayor Stone proclaimed October 1 as International Day of Older Persons and encouraged all citizens to recognize the crucial role played by older Canadians in our community. United Way Month Mayor Stone proclaimed the month of October as United Way Month, and encouraged all citizens to remember that it takes every part of the community - individuals, businesses and organizations - to supply the passion, expertise and resources needed to create lasting change to improve lives and build stronger communities.
