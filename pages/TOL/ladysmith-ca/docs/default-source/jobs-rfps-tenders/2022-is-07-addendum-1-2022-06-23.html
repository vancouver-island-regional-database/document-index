The following Revisions apply to the Tender Documents: QUESTIONS &amp; ANSWERS Q 1: The original tender included three streetlight electrical drawings that have not been included in this package of re-issued drawings. The original tender drawings included street lighting electrical drawings E-1 thru E-3. Are these missing from this new tender package? A1: Yes, the electrical drawings are the same as previous and are attached to this addendum. Q 2: Are inserta-tees on the storm mainline an acceptable alternative to wye’s or tees for storm service junctions and CB junctions? A2: No. Q 3: Are there any Town of Ladysmith supplied dumpsites for fill disposal that they could provide to reduce overall project costs? A3: Yes, Town land is available for common excavation/fill disposal. The property is located off of Thomas Road at coordinates 49° 02’22.75” N 123° 52’28.28” W Q4: Please confirm the budget for this project. A lot of time and effort goes into putting together a tender submission and we only intend to price this project again if the budget has been increased significantly. A4: The Town has made changes since the first tender to provide flexibility to the contractor including extending the completion date into 2023. It is anticipated that the extended completion date will also allow the Town to spread the budget over the 2022 and 2023 fiscal year budgets. The Town is motivated to complete the project.
