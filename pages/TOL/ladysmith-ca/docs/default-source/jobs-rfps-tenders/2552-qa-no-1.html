1 of 1 TOWN OF LADYSMITH Sandy Beach Lift Station Improvements Question and Answer Sheet #1 2026-05-29 Question 1 Can tenders be submitted digitally to reduce travel time and avoid printing/hard- copy delivery requirements? Answer 1 As per the tender documents, hard copy submissions are required. Question 2 Tender Form – Tenderer’s Questionnaire: For Item 4, can we reference “See Page 10 – Municipal References,” or is there additional information expected for Item 4 beyond what is already included on Page 10? Answer 2 Referencing page 10 is acceptable. Question 3 What is the anticipated Notice of Award date? Answer 3 Tender results are planned to be reviewed at the July 7, 2026, council meeting. Question 4 ITT – Article 40, Permits: The work is noted to occur during the period of least environmental risk (June 1 – September 1). Based on current anticipated lead times for specified valves, pump discharge fittings, and obtaining WorkSafeBC sign-off on the confined space entry procedure, there is a concern that the work may extend beyond the September 1 deadline. Would there be flexibility to extend this timeframe if required? Answer 4 Plan is for the work to occur in the least risk window from June 1 to September 1, 2026. The next least risk window period is December 1, 2026 - February 15, 2027. Alternative dates may be discussed with the successful tenderer if permits or materials cannot be obtained. Question 5 Please confirm the owner will be paying for the environmental monitoring. Answer 5 The owner will be paying for the environmental monitor. Question 6 Section 33 32 13 00 - Item 2.10.5&6 - Is the owner or contractor to pay for this testing?
