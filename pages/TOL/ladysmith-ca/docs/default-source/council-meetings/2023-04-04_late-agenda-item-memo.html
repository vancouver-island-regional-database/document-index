Recommendation: That Council amend the agenda to add the following item, received after publication of the agenda: Page(s) 11.1. Appeal to the Province for Financial Assistance for Costs Associated with the Replacement of the Stocking Lake Reservoir Dam Mayor Stone has requested that Council consider the following recommendation: That Council direct staff to draft a letter for the Mayor’s signature to Minister Ralston and Premier Eby to appeal for financial assistance for the costs associated with the replacement of the Stocking Lake reservoir dam.
