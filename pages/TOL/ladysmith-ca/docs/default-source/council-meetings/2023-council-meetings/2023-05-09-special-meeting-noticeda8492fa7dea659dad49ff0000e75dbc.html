NOTICE SPECIAL COUNCIL MEETING Pursuant to “Council Procedure Bylaw 2009, No. 1666” notice is provided that Council will hold a Special Meeting on Tuesday, May 9, 2023 at 6:30 p.m. The meeting will include the following business: • Adoption of “Financial Plan Bylaw 2023, No. 2141” • Adoption of “Property Tax Rates Bylaw 2023, No. 2142” • Adoption of "Sewer Parcel Tax Bylaw 2023, No. 2143" • Adoption of "Water Parcel Tax Bylaw 2023, No. 2144” Corporate Officer (M. O’Halloran) The Town of Ladysmith Council will meet in person at Ladysmith Seniors Centre 630 2nd Avenue on Tuesday, May 9, 2023at 6:30 PM for a Special Council Meeting, immediately followed by a Regular Committee of the Whole Meeting
