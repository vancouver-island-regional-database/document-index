TOWN OF LADYSMITH Celebrate our Present. Embrace our Future. Honour our Past. Minutes of the Parks, Recreation &amp; Culture Advisory Committee Wednesday, February 21, 2024 at 7:02pm Frank Jameson Community Centre COMMITTEE MEMBERS PRESENT: STAFF PRESENT: Lynda Baker Terri Merritt-Worden Richard Frost, Lead Colleen Butcher Jane Nettleton Kim Cheang, Minute Taker Geoff Dean Councillor Duck Paterson Mike Gregory, Guest Gordon Filewych Bruce Mason Pamela Walker REGRETS: Jacqueline Huard Kim Nakahara CALLTOORDERAND © The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:02pm and acknowledged with ACKNOWLEDGEMENT gratitude that the meeting was taking place on the unceded territory of the Stz’uminus First Nation. AGENDA 2024-01: That the Parks, Recreation &amp; Culture Advisory Committee approve the agenda for the meeting as presented. Motion Carried. MINUTES 2024-02: NEW BUSINESS OLD BUSINESS PRC DEPARTMENT UPDATE That the Parks, Recreation &amp; Culture Advisory Committee approve the minutes of the November 15, 2024 meeting as presented. Motion Carried Appropriate Behaviour - Code of Conduct — Recreation Facilities Update The Committee reviewed and provided feedback on the Appropriate Behaviour - Code of Conduct - Recreation Facilities Policy update. 2024-03: That the Parks, Recreation &amp; Culture Advisory Committee recommend that Council endorse the updates to the Appropriate Behaviour - Code of Conduct - Recreation Facilities Policy. Motion Carried Rutti Park Plan Public Engagement - Draft Survey and Plan The Committee reviewed and provided feedback on the Rutti Park Plan Community Engagement Survey. M. Gregory will refine the survey further and bring it back to the committee in another month. PRC Department Update R.
