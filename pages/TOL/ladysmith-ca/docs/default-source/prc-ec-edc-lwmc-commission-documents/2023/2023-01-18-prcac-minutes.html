TOWN OF LADYSMITH Celebrate our Present. Embrance our Future. Honour our Past. Minutes of the Parks, Recreation &amp; Culture Advisory Committee Wednesday, January 18, 2023 at 7:00pm Frank Jameson Community Centre Lynda Baker, Chair Colleen Butcher COMMITTEE MEMBERS PRESENT: STAFF PRESENT: Jacqueline Huard Chris Barfoot, Lead Bruce Mason Kim Cheang, Minute Taker Geoff Dean Gordon Filewych REGRETS: Kelly Daniels Lucy Partington Terri Merritt-Worden Councillor Duck Paterson CALL TO ORDER AND ACKNOWLEDGEMENT AGENDA MINUTES NEW BUSINESS The meeting was called to order at 7:00pm The Chair acknowledged with gratitude that the meeting was taking place on the traditional, unceded territory of the Stz’uminus First Nation. 2023-01: That the Parks, Recreation &amp; Culture Advisory Committee approve the agenda for the meeting as presented. Motion Carried. 2023-02: That the Parks, Recreation &amp; Culture Advisory Committee approve the minutes of the October 19, 2022 meeting as presented. Motion Carried Ladysmith Community Gardens Society (LCGS) Delegation T. Reynen of the Ladysmith Community Gardens Society provided background information about the society and discussed how the garden community functions, some of the challenges it faces, some potential solutions, and issues to consider for the future. Ms. Reynen completed her presentation by highlighting the important, multi-generational role the LCGS plays within the community, as it teaches food awareness, how to grow food and how to preserve it. She noted that there is a growing interest within the community and that there are 17 names on the waiting list for garden plots. Page 1|3
