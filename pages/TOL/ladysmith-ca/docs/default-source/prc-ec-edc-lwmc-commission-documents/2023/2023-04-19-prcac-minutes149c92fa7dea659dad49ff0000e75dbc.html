TOWN OF LADYSMITH Celebrate our Present. Embrance our Future. Honour our Past. Minutes of the Parks, Recreation &amp; Culture Advisory Committee Wednesday, April 19, 2023 at 7:00pm Frank Jameson Community Centre COMMITTEE MEMBERS PRESENT: Lynda Baker, Chair Colleen Butcher Geoff Dean Gordon Filewych REGRETS: Jacqueline Huard Terri Merritt-Worden STAFF PRESENT: Chris Barfoot, Lead Kim Cheang, Minute Taker Bruce Mason Councillor Duck Paterson Pamela Walker CALL TO ORDER AND ACKNOWLEDGEMENT AGENDA MINUTES NEW BUSINESS The meeting was called to order at 7:00pm The Chair acknowledged with gratitude that the meeting was taking place on the traditional, unceded territory of the Stz’uminus First Nation. 2023-11: That the Parks, Recreation &amp; Culture Advisory Committee approve the agenda for the meeting as presented. Motion Carried, 2023-12: That the Parks, Recreation &amp; Culture Advisory Committee approve the minutes of the March 15, 2023 meeting as presented. Motion Carried Ladysmith Boys and Girls Club - May 11 Staff will send invitations to Committee members to attend the Ladysmith BGC Central Vancouver Island - New Childcare Space Opening. PATG amended Terms of Reference &amp; Policy update C. Barfoot provided an update on the PATG Terms of Reference &amp; Policy streamlining process. The Committee of the Whole approved the amended changes on March 14 and Council ratified this approval on March 21. Earlier today, April 19, the Committee reviewed and approved the changes. Terms expire as of June 30 Staff to follow up with Committee members whose terms expire as of June 30 to advise them if they can reapply. Page 1/2
