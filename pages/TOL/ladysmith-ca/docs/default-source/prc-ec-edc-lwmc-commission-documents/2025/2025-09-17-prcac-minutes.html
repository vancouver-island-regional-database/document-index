TOWN OF LADYSMITH Celebrate our Present. Embrace our Future. Honour our Past. Minutes of the Parks, Recreation &amp; Culture Advisory Committee Wednesday, September 17, 2025 at 7:00pm Frank Jameson Community Centre COMMITTEE MEMBERS PRESENT: STAFF PRESENT: Gordon Filewych Dave Mack Bruce Mason Terri Merritt-Worden REGRETS: Colleen Butcher Jacqueline Huard Jane Nettleton Chris Barfoot, Lead Councillor Jeff Virtanen Kim Cheang, Minute Taker John Knutson - Guest Speaker Mitchel Lowe Pamela Walker CALL TO ORDER AND ACKNOWLEDGEMENT AGENDA MINUTES NEW BUSINESS Councillor Virtanen called the meeting to order at 7:00pm and acknowledged with gratitude that the meeting was taking place on the unceded territory of the Stz’uminus First Nation. 2025-11: That the Parks, Recreation &amp; Culture Advisory Committee approve the agenda for the meeting as presented. Motion Carried. 2025-12: That the Parks, Recreation &amp; Culture Advisory Committee approve the minutes of the June 18, 2025 meeting as presented. Motion Carried Welcome New Members e Newmembers were introduced, and each provided a brief background. Elect Chair and Co-Chair e The election of Chair and Co-Chair was deferred to the next meeting. 2025-13: That the Parks, Recreation &amp; Culture Advisory Committee defer the election of Chair and Co-Chair to the next meeting. Motion Carried Guest Speaker - John Knutson e J. Knutson provided an overview of the Hill Top (also known as Brown Drive) area, including its origins, naming history, and development background.
