CONTACT US 250.245.6400 / info@ladysmith.ca 410 Esplanade MAIL PO Box 220 . Ladysmith . BC . V9G 1A2 What is the Native Plant Giveway? The Town’s Sustainability Program will be offering native, drought-resistant, and fire-resistant plants to Ladysmith residents. These will include trees, shrubs, perennials, fruit, flowering plants etc. There is a limit of 1 for each household. FireSmart BC will also be there to educate the community on how these fire- resistant plants create a firesmart home. What is the purpose? An excellent and easy way to conserve water is by planting native plants that are adjusted to the local climate and are drought-resistant. This helps the community use less water throughout the dry summer and help conserve our limited water source. Native plants also attract pollinators which are important for our ecosystems and biodiversity.
