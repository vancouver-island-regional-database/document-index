Water Consumption – Staff Presentation and Update The Director of Infrastructure Services provided Council with an update on water consumption to date in 2015, outlining the effects of Stage 2 and Stage 3 water restrictions. He pointed out that overall water consumption in the Town is down by 25 per cent as a result of Stage 3 water restrictions , and the Town has reduced its own water consumption by 70 per cent with water restriction measures. Moved and seconded: That the Municipal Services Committee recommend that Council adopt the following water conservation measures:  Establish a Council policy on Sustaina ble Drought Tolerant Planting Strategies in Fall 2015  Establish a policy on Municipal Water Use during water restrictions in Fall 2015  Consider adopting further water conservation measures  Review Water Metering for non-residential properties. Motion carried.
