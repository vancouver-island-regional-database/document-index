QUESTION PERIOD  A maximum of 15 minutes is allotted for questions.  Persons wishing to address Council during “Question Period” must be Town of Ladysmith residents, non -resident property owners, or operators of a business.  Individuals must state their name and address for identification purposes.  Questions put forth must be on topics which are not normally dealt with by Town staff as a matter of routine.  Questions must be brief and to the point.  Questions shall be addressed through the Chair and answers given likewise.
