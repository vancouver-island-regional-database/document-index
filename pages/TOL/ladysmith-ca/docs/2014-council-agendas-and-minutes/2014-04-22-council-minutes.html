Town of Ladysmith Special Council Meeting Minutes, April 22, 2014 Page 1 MINUTES OF A SPECIAL MEETING OF THE COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF LADYSMITH HELD IN COUNCIL CHAMBERS, CITY HALL ON TUESDAY, APRIL 22, 2014 CALL TO ORDER 7:30 P.M. COUNCIL MEMBERS PRESENT: Mayor Rob Hutchins Councillor Steve Arnett Councillor Jillian Dashwood Councillor Bill Drysdale Councillor Gord Horth Councillor Duck Paterson Councillor Glenda Patterson STAFF PRESENT: Ruth Malli ALSO PRESENT: Jerry Berry, Consultant CALL TO ORDER Mayor Hutchins called this Special Meeting of Council to order at 7:30 p.m. in order to retire immediately into closed session CLOSED MEETING CS 2014-146 Moved and seconded: That Council retire into closed session in order to consider the following items: • personal information about an identifiable individual who holds or is being considered for a position as an officer, employee or agent of the municipality or another position appointed by the municipality • labour relations or other employee relations Motion carried. RISE AND REPORT CS 2014-147 Moved and seconded: That Council arise from closed session without report. Motion carried. ADJOURNMENT CS 2014-148 Moved and seconded: That this Special Meeting of Council adjourn at 9:09 p.m. Motion carried. CERTIFIED CORRECT: Mayor (R. Hutchins)) Corporate Officer (S. Bowden)
