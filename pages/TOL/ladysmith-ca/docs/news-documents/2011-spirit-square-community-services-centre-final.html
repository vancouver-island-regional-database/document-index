NEWS RELEASE -- For Immediate Release LADYSMITH SPIRIT SQUARE HOLDS GRAND OPENING Ladysmith, June 24, 2011 – Ladysmith’s newest community amenity – providing services for citizens of all ages — was opened with much celebration this afternoon. Ladysmith Spirit Square is the name given to the ‘campus’ that offers a wide range of community services as well as a public gathering space. The new homes of the Ladysmith Resources Centre, the Boys and Gi rls Clubs of Central Vancouver Island-- Ladysmith and the Ladysmith Seniors’ Cent re Society surround a public gathering space known as Spirit Square. The entire complex came about as a result of a new partnership between those organizations and the Town of Ladysmith. The project would not have been possibl e without the generous support of the provincial and federal governments. “I am pleased that our Government has de livered on this commitment for Ladysmith residents,” said the Honourable Lynne Yelich, Minister of State for Western Economic Diversification. “Our investment in the Community Services Centre here in Ladysmith has created jobs and will provide residents of all ages access to community services.” “I want to congratulate Ladysmith for its ambitious and creative use of Provincial Government funding, which the community us ed to generate significant additional investments in this tremendous new community resource,” said Comox Valley MLA Don McRae. “The Spirit Square program aimed to enrich B.C. communities by creating or improving outdoor gathering places and what Ladysmith has accomplished with this project is an outstanding example of its success.” The Government of Canada’s Economic Action Plan contributed $2.75 million through the Community Adjustment Fund towards the renovation of an existing building on the site and the construction of a new building. The Ministry of Children and Family Development provided a total of $750,000 from the BC Child Care Major Capital Grant Program. The Ministry of Community, Sport and Cultural Development had already provided a $500,000 grant to th e Town for the development of a Spirit LADYSMITH SENIORS CENTRE SOCIETY Square in the Town. Each of the partners al so contributed their own funds – and the land, in the Town’s case -- to ensure the success of the $5 million project. “This community gathering place will enhanc e our citizens’ sense of place and pride in our community, and be a welcome—and we lcoming—feature of our town, providing much-needed programs and services as well as a public space for all citizens to enjoy,” said Mayor Hutchins. “The Centre came about because organizations needed room to expand programs and services and the Town had land and buildings on High Street,” said Kevin Douville, President of the Boys and Girls Clubs of Central Vancouver island. “We sat down together one day to brainstorm what could happen if we all pooled our resources—and the result is this exciting new community services centre, that is better than we had even dreamed it could be,” said Donna Soules, president of the Ladysmith Resources Centre Association. The Ladysmith Seniors Centre will also have a new home at the Spirit Square. “We had outgrown our old building and now we can offer more entertainment and activities for Ladysmith seniors,” said Cora Maier, President of the Ladysmith Seniors Centre Society. The Spirit Square Grand Opening featured a ribbon cutting, entertainment by local musicians, and a large cake proclaiming “Ladysmith Spirit Square.
