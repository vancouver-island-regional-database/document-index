BEAR ALERT NOTICE TO HEART LAKE TRAIL USERS Please be aware that a bear has been spotted on the Heart Lake trail. Please keep pets on a leash and children close to you. Never approach or feed the bears or other animals. If you see a bear please call: Conservation Office 1-877-952-7277 Town of Ladysmith Parks Supervisor 250-245-6448
