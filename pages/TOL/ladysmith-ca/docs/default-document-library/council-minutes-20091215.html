Town of Ladysmith Minutes of the Special Meeting of Council of the Town of Ladysmith held in Council Chambers at City Hall on Monday, December 15, 2009 at 5:00 p.m. Mayor Hutchins called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m. 2009-614 It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda for the Special Council meeting for Monday, December 15, 2009 be approved as circulated. AWARDING CONTRACT FOR CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT SERVICES FOR RENOVATIONS TO 224/226 HIGH STREET R. Malli, City Manager, provided an over view of the process regarding the hiring of construction management services for the renovations to 224/226 High Street. 2009-615 It was moved, seconded and carried that the contract for the provision of construction management services for renovations to 224/226 High Street be awarded to Windley Contracting Ltd. in the amount of $36,300 and the Mayor and Corporate Officer be authorized to sign the contract on behalf of the Town. 2009-616 It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 5:12 p.m. Corporate Officer (S. Bowden) CERTIFIED CORRECT: Mayor (R.
