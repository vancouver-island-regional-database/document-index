COUNCIL MEMBERS PRESENT: Mayor Rob Hutchins Councillor Steve Arnett Councillor Jillian Dashwood Councillor Bill Drysdale Councillor Duck Paterson COUNCIL MEMBERS ABSENT: Councillor Gord Horth Councillor Glenda Patterson STAFF PRESENT: Ruth Malli Sandy Bowden Erin Anderson John Manson Joanna Winter CALL TO ORDER Mayor Hutchins called the Special Council Meeting to order at 4:30 p.m. AGENDA APPROVAL CS 2012-161 It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda for the Special Meeting of Council for May 10 , 2012 be approved as circulated.
