AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of June 10, 2010 be approved with the addition of New Business 8 a) Bylaw Enforcement. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of May 13, 2010 be approved. 3. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES a. HRAC Residency Requirement – Bylaw No. 1279 s.2.2(a) Councillor, L. Evans reported that residency is currently required for the HRAC. There doesn’t seem to be a consistency among the different Commissions/Committees regarding a residency requirement. L. Evans also provided information that the structures of the Commissions are currently under review. The HRAC has requested that Councillor L. Evans obtain clarification of Bylaw No. 1279 s.2.2(a) on the following: - Residency requirement for the Commissions and Committees. - How is residency being defined? - Can a variance to the Bylaw be sought? 4. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS a. Facade Development Permit Application – 3060-10-05 Darlene Munn/Chopstix Salon – 321 First Avenue Chair, M. Martin circulated the heritage colour palettes that were provided by the applicant. CURRENT PROJECT UPDATES/NEXT STEPS a. Heritage Building Brochure Update B. Laxdal reported that the brochure was reviewed by Staff and edits have been made.
