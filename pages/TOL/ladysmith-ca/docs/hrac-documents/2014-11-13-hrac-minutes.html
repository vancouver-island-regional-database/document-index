http://www.ladysmith.ca/docs/hrac-documents/2014-11-13-hrac-minutes.pdf October 27, 2016 MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Thursday, November 13, 2014 at 5:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall PRESENT: Chair – Marnie Craig; Members - Ann Rogers, Jennifer Robinson, Maureen Martin, Allen McDermid, Tamara Hutchinson, Gerry Beltgens; LDHS Member - Bernardien Knol; Council Liaison - Councillor Bill Drysdale; Staff Liaison & Recorder - Angela Vincent GUESTS: Lurene Haines, applicant The meeting was called to order at 5:00 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of November 13, 2014 be approved. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of October 7, 2014 be adopted. 3. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES None. 4. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS A. Vincent reviewed the role of the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission in consideration of signs and facades. Section 3.2.2 of the Sign & Canopy Bylaw (Bylaw 1176): The Building Inspector shall refer every sign application(s) for property located in the Downtown Specified Area to the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commissio n for consideration and report to him on colour, design, location and style prior to issuance of the permit. a. Stelur Eatery and Catering Company, #2-341 First Avenue Lurene Haines of the Stelur Eatery presented the proposed signage and façade for the building. She noted that the awning would be made from canvas material. Members appreciated the fresh looks of the design and colours. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission recommend the sign and façade proposal for the Stelur Eatery and Catering Company at 341 First Avenue, unit #2. 5. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES a. Heritage Building Plaques M. Martin noted that the sub -committee was working on the text and photos for the plaques. It was determined that if the building owners were amenable, there could be flexibility in sizes for the plaques for larger buildings such as the Eagles Hall, subject to budget. The sub-committee will provide the plaque information to A. Vincent by Friday, November 22nd. b. Heritage Week T. Hutchinson contacted Ladysmith groups regarding the organization of Heritage Week 2015. A group or individual interested in organizing the Heritage Week event is still required. Members discussed ideas, which focused on two separate projects: Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission November 13, 2014 2 Heritage Event  Saturday, February 21st on First Avenue.  Potentially involving: Ladysmith Museum, Ladysmith Singers, Ladysmith Little Theatre, Ladysmith Photography Club, Waterfront Art Gallery  Interactive event ideas: tours/characters in downto wn, treasure hunt, stamp books for children, photography/art competition or display, etc. Heritage Awareness  Display throughout Heritage Week  An information or image display for buildings in the downtown  Ideas: archive images vs current images, or before/after revitalization photos?  Posters can be hung in windows or incorporated into the window display  Size approximately 16” x 24” to 2’x3’, with foamcore/posterboard backing. Members will continue to approach local groups to encourage assistance with organization and participation. B. Knol will start to organize posters. M. Craig will assist with graphic design as needed. 6. NEW BUSINESS a. Alternate ADP (Advisory Design Panel) Representative Members chose M. Martin as an alternate representative for the Advisory Design Panel when existing representative A. Rogers is unable to attend. It was moved, seconded and carried that Maureen Martin will be the Heritage Revitalization and Advisory Commission’s alternate representative for the Advisory Design Panel. 7. CORRESPONDENCE None. 8. UPDATES A thank you card was signed by Members for Cathy Gilroy , who provided many photos of Aggie hall for the heritage plaque. Heritage BC is expected to announce heritage project funding by December 2014. The most recent Statements of Significance will be completed by the end of the year. 9. NEXT MEETING Thursday, December 1 1, 2014 at 5:00 pm – Council Chambers, City Hall. The following meetings will be held on the third Tuesday of the month. The first meeting for 2015 will be held on Tuesday, January 20th at 5:00 pm. 10. ADJOURNMENT It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 6:20 p.m. ____________________________________ Chair (M.
