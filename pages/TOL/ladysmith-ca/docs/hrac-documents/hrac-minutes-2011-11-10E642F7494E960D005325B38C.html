http://www.ladysmith.ca/docs/hrac-documents/hrac-minutes-2011-11-10E642F7494E960D005325B38C.pdf October 27, 2016 MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Thursday, November 10, 2011 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall PRESENT: Chair – Jennifer Robinson; Members - A llen McDermid, Brian McLaurin, Tamara Hutchinson, and Peter Blumel; LDHS Member – Bernardien Knol; Council Liaison – Mayor Rob Hutchins; Staff Liaison and Recorder – Angela Vincent-Lewis ABSENT: Lynne Brown, Maureen Martin The meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of November 10, 2011 be approved. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of October 6, 2011 be approved. 3. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES None. 4. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES Mayor R. Hutchins noted that he has encountered many interesting heritage stories while campaigning. He asked the Members to consider how Ladysmith should capture these memories. T. Hutchinson suggested an online forum (similar to that of Nanaimo) could help to keep the knowledge and share memories. Members were asked to consider solutions in the future. 5. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS None. 6. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES a. Heritage Building Plaques A. Vincent-Lewis noted that at its November 7 th meeting, Council had endorsed the plaque design as recommended by the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission, and moved that staff be directed to notify the seven building owners to advise them of the opportunity to receive a plaque, and arrange for distribution of the plaques. The quality and longevity of the aluminum and vinyl signs was discussed. According to the sign manufacturer, the signs will be made of an appropriate material and should be able to withstand some vandalism. The Members examined the cost for the plaque wall mounts, at approximately $550. The wall mounts include screw caps on the front of the plaques which will interfere with the existing design of the plaque. Members agreed to increase the size of the plaques by adding a rectangular black shap e which will serve as the location for the screw caps. It was noted that the owners of Bayview Framing have recently updated their historical plaque at a modest price, and will display the plaque soon. Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission November 10, 2011 2 b. HRAC 2012 Projects Prioritization The Members discussed the following projects : Transfer Beach Pictorial, Heritage Inventory Update, Heritage Building Br ochure Update, and Heritage Week to determine which project should be the main priority for 2012. J. Robinson reminded the Members that the Transfer Beach Pictorial has been of interest for many years. The Members discussed the projects and agreed that the Transfer Beach Pictorial will be the first priority for 2012. A. McDermid suggested that the project involve the Stz’uminus First Nation heritage through photographs, materials, etc. The Heritage Building Brochure was also identified as a priority since there are few remaining copies, and the Members felt it would be beneficial to make the brochure consistent with the Town’s other brochures through modest design changes. P. Blumel noted that budgeting for the Commission’s annual projects is difficult due to misalignment between the timing of the Town’s annual budget approval and the Member’s terms. It was moved, seconded and carried that th e HRAC recommend that Council consider the reconfiguration of the HRAC’s annual appointment cycle to coincide with the approval of the Town’s budget. 7. NEW BUSINESS a. Joint Meeting with Nanaimo Heritage Commission J. Robinson outlined the opportunity for the Commission to meet with the Nanaimo Heritage Commission. Nanaimo has invited the HRAC to join them for the December meeting, to be held at a restaurant in Nanaimo on Wednesday, December 7th at 5pm. It was noted that the Town will cover Members’ meals and drinks with the exception of alcoholic beverages. Members were interested in attending the event, and more information will be available soon. b. Heritage Week, February 20-26, 2012 Theme: Heritage of Power – Water Mills, Hydroelectric Plants, Transformer Buildings Members discussed various opportunities for Heritage Week. A. McDermid suggested that the new Heritage Plaque unveiling could coincide with Heritage Week. Members were in favour of this suggestion. R. Hutchins noted that historically, the Town was self-reliant on power generated by the local coal power plant. He suggested that the Ladysmith & District Historical Society could be offered support to research the municipal plant, and to create a presentation for Heritage Week. T. Hutchinson suggested that history classes at the High School could be an additional resource for the LDHS in the study. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission recommends $250 support to the LDHS for the stud y of Ladysmith’s former coal power plant, with the information to be released during Heritage Week 2012. The Members considered options for info rming Ladysmith residents about this heritage information, such as including information along with utility bills. c. Aggie Hall Signage A. Vincent-Lewis spoke about the Council resolution on January 17, 2011 to develop a proposal for directional and interpretive signage for Aggie Hall (including its two tenants, LAFF and the Cadets), and for the proposal to be referred to HRAC for Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission November 10, 2011 3 review. While a proposal is still in process, information is available regarding the location of the Cadet’s proposed signage. The Members were shown photos of the hall and potential sign locations. The Members agreed that the Cadets should be allowed to locate signs at the front peak of Aggie Hall (facing First Avenue) and at the entranceway on Symonds Street. d. Announcement A. McDermid noted that the 49 th Parallel Grocery Store would be celebrating the 60 th anniversary of the name on December 14th. 8. CORRESPONDENCE None. 9. NEXT MEETING Wednesday, December 7th at 5pm - Joint meeting with the Nanaimo Heritage Commission in Nanaimo. Restaurant location in Nanaimo TBA. 10. ADJOURNMENT It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 8:30 p.m. ____________________________________ Chair (J.
