http://www.ladysmith.ca/docs/hrac-documents/hrac-minutes-2010-09-15.pdf October 27, 2016 MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Wednesday, September 15, 2010 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall PRESENT: Members – Jennifer Lush, John Hinde, Ann Rogers and Maureen Martin; Council Liaison – Lori Evans; Mayor Rob Hutchins; Planning Technician & Recorder – Stephanie Blumensaat GUESTS: Angela Quek, Architect; Jenny and Cal Jarvis, Owners ABSENT: Member – Peter Blumel Mayor Hutchins explained that the Town’s advisory commission structure is currently under review. In the meantime, HRAC will continue with an extension of 2010 terms of appointment for up to 90 days instead of seeking new memberships. 1. ELECTION OF CHAIR A. Rogers was nominated and accepted the nomi nation as Chair. There were no further nominations. A. Rogers was acclaimed as Chair. The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. 2. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of September 15, 2010 be approved with the addition of: 6. (a) Heritage Building Brochure 7. (b) Upcoming Projects 3. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of June 10, 2010 be approved. 4. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES a. Development Permit Application 3060-10-07 524 1st Avenue – AYPQ Architecture Proposed second storey addition to the J. Cochrane Building Owner, Jenny Jarvis presented a history of the J. Cochrane building. Architect, Angela Quek reviewed the drawings, detailing the pr oposed second storey addition. A. Quek explained that the addition has been set back 14’ from the front façade making it barely visible from 1 st Avenue. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission advises Council that it supports the proposal (3060-10-07) for a second storey addition to the J. Cochrane building at 524 1st Avenue. Mayor Hutchins requested HRAC provide feed back regarding the proposed limit that on the front deck area no landscaping features (i.e. umbrella, potted tree) would be permitted to be taller than 1 metre to protect the streetscape views from First Avenue. Members discussed this limit and agreed that a limit on these types of landscaping features was not needed, but that permanent items such as signage should not be permitted. Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission September 15, 2010 2 5. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS None. 6. CURRENT PROJECT UPDATES/NEXT STEPS a. Heritage Building Brochure A. Rogers stated that since July 28, 2010 approximately 1100 brochures have been distributed. There are approximately 1300 brochures remaining. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission recommend to Council that the Heritage Building Brochure be available for distribution on the trolley. 7. NEW BUSINESS a. Advisory Design Panel Appointment Mayor Hutchins reviewed the background and purpose of HRAC having a member act as a liaison on the Advisory Design Panel (ADP). He explained as Jenny Van Horne is no longer on the HRAC; another HRAC member is required to fill the liaison role on the ADP. He further explained that Council, in the near future, may alter the liaison role and the development permit review process, however in the meantime, an HRAC member needs to fulfill the liaison role. It was moved, seconded and carried that the HRAC recommend to Council that Peter Blumel be nominated as the HRAC liaison to the Advisory Design Panel and Maureen Martin be the alternate liaison. b. Upcoming Projects Members listed potential projects for the upcoming year. These included: • Statement of Significance building plaques • School education program • Website improvements to include “heritage” content • Update and digitization of the Heritage Inventory • Development of relationship with Stz’uminus First Nations Mayor Hutchins suggested the following projects: • RBC Collage – 2nd side to reflect the logging history of the area • Signage Bylaw – sandwich board signs and directional signage • Pictorial history at Transfer Beach Amphitheatre • Repair of the 1980s’ revitalization building plaques • Paint colour palette development Members agreed to review and prioritize potential projects in preparation for the next HRAC meeting. 8.
