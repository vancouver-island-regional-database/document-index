Vincent provided Commission members with the Statements of Significance completed by Archivist Christine Meutzner. Members will review the 10 Statements of Significance and provide feedback at the next meeting. Christine Meutzner has offered to make a presentation to HRAC regarding the evaluation of heritage properties for inclusion on Community Heritage Registers. Members were interested in seeing the presentation, potentially scheduled for the February HRAC meeting. b. Heritage Building Plaques The remaining building plaque information will be forwarded to staff. There is a need for additional archival photos. Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission December 11, 2014 2 c. Heritage Week A. Vincent reviewed HRAC’s Heritage Week activities from 2011 -2014, and summarized ideas discussed in the previous meeting. T. Hutchinson provided an update from B. Knol, which included identifying buildings of interest to be included in the posters, and organizing an article in the newspaper by Ed Nichols on. G. Beltgens reported that the Rotary was interested in helping to organize an event. Mayor Stone suggested that HRAC could inform local groups (such as the Ladysmith Downtown Business Association (LDBA) and Chamber of Commerce) about the theme and ideas for Heritage Week , and allow the groups to organize an event if they wish. HRAC will focus attention on creating informative posters and Heritage Week awareness. Members decided to cha nge the direction of the poster concept to better reflect the general Main Street theme, instead of individual buildings. Archival street photographs will be printed at a large scale on posters . Photographs may represent various decades, or changes resulting from revitalization initiatives.
