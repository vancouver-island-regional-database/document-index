http://www.ladysmith.ca/docs/hrac-documents/2012-12-06-hrac-minutes.pdf October 27, 2016 MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Thursday, December 6, 2012 at 7:00 p.m. Ladysmith Museum, 721 First Avenue PRESENT: Chair – Jennifer Robinson; Members – Tamara Hutchinson, Allen McDermid , Maureen Martin, Ann Rogers ; LDHS Member – Bernardien Knol; Council Liaison – Councillor Bill Drysdale; Staff Liaison and Recorder – Angela Vincent GUESTS: Nanaimo Community Heritage Commission The meeting was called to order at 7:25 p.m. 1. INTRODUCTIONS Chair, J. Robinson welcomed the members of the Nanaimo Community Heritage Commission. Commission members, Council liaisons and Staff liaisons introduced themselves. 2. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of December 6, 2012 be approved. 3. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of November 8, 2012 be approved. 4. COUNCIL REFERRALS None. 5. NEW BUSINESS a. Information sharing with Nanaimo Community Heritage Commission City of Nanaimo Community Heritage Commission Heritage Planner, Chris Sholberg reviewed the activities and ongoing programs of the Commission, including: the Heritage Summit, Heritage Paint Out/Shoot Out, Community Heritage Commission Networking Conference, and the new heritage walking route brochure. C. Sholberg also outlined some potential additions and removals from the Community Heritage Register, and an update of the 2012 Heritage Façade Improvement Grant Program and the Heritage Home Grant Program. An upcoming project will be a field inventory of buildings in the Old City neighborhood. Town of Ladysmith Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission Members presented the projects the HRAC has completed over the past year, including: the installation of heritage plaques, Heritage Week display, updates to the Heritage Route Brochure and Heritage Building Brochure, and on -going projects including updates to the Community Heritage Register, potential heritage façade improvement grants and the Transfer Beach Pictorial. Members also discussed the development of the new Ladysmith Museum, and clarified that a museum tour would take place following the meeting. Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission December 6, 2012 2 6. ONGOING BUSINESS a. Community Heritage Commissions Networking Conference summary A. Vincent described the Commission networking conference, held at the Nanaimo Museum on November 24th, and also attended by Chair J. Robinson. The conference was well-attended by Heritage Commissions throughout the province, and included reports from the Commissions, a walking tour of downtown Nanaimo, and a roundtable discussion of an effective Heritage Commission. A. Vincent outlined some projects completed by other Commissions in the past year. 7. NEXT MEETING January 10, 2013 at 7:00 p.m. – Council Chambers, City Hall. 8. ADJOURNMENT It was moved seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 8:45 p.m. ____________________________________ Chair (J.
