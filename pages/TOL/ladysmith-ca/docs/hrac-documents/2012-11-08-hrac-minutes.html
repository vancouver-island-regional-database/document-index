http://www.ladysmith.ca/docs/hrac-documents/2012-11-08-hrac-minutes.pdf October 27, 2016 MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Thursday, November 8, 2012 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall PRESENT: Chair – Jennifer Robinson; Members – Tamara Hutchinson, Allen McDermid , Maureen Martin, Ann Rogers ; LDHS Member – Bernardien Knol; Council Liaison – Councillor Bill Drysdale; Staff Liaison and Recorder – Angela Vincent GUESTS: Cathy Goldie (Antique Addict) The meeting was called to order at 7:05 p.m. 1. INTRODUCTIONS Newly appointed HRAC members Maureen Martin and Ann Rogers were introduced and welcomed by the Commission. Bernardien Knol will serve as the LDHS representative until the position is officially nominated by the LDHS. 2. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of November 8, 2012 be approved with the following addition: 8(c) Heritage BC Annual Conference Report , and 8(d) Heritage Week. 3. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of September 13, 2012 be approved. 4. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES None. 5. COUNCIL REFERRALS a. Request for Town of Ladysmith Sponsorship of a Documentary Commemorating the Vancouver Island Miners’ Strike of 1913 Council’s request for comment from HRAC was distributed to Commission members via email on September 21st 2012. HRAC Members confirmed approval of the sponsorship by email and provided formal support. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission recommends the sponsorship of a documentary commemorating the Vancouver Island Miners’ Strike of 1913. Councillor Drysdale noted that the HRAC’s approval by email was used by Council to determine support for the documentary. At the November 5 th meeting, Council endorsed the sponsorship of the documentary. b. Community Heritage Register Project Update Councillor Drysdale reported that at the November 5 th meeting, Council supported the update of the Ladysmith Community Heritage by adding buildings from the Ladysmith Heritage Inventory. Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission November 8, 2012 2 6. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS a. Antique Addict – 12 Roberts Street The application for proposed façade colo urs and window replacement for the Antique Addict was distributed to Commission members for comment via email on September 26th and October 12 th 2012. Members confirmed the approval of the Antiques Addict signage by email and provided formal support. It was move d, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission recommend the façade colour and window replacement for Antique Addict at 12 Roberts Street. Cathy Goldie (co -owner of the Antique Addict) provided an update to HRAC regarding the building renovations, and will apply for further façade improvements and signage early next year. b. Yuppies & Hippies Apparel – 318 First Avenue Members reviewed the versions of the proposed window signage and recommended the white/cream paint option. Members felt the use of three different fonts was busy, and suggested that the unique handwritten font used on the door could also be used on the window signage. Members supported the proposed signage subject to changes, and asked that the applicant provide a final version for review via email. 7. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES a. Heritage Building Brochure A. Vincent reported that the updated Heritage Building Brochure had been printed and distributed in October. Copies were distributed to members. b. Transfer Beach Pictorial A. McDermid reported some potential funding opportunities offered through Heritage BC. Members discussed opportunities to provide the pictorial in an enclosed versus unenclosed space. The sub -committee will meet and determine the project timeline and responsibilities for the next HRAC meeting. c. Outstanding Citizen Display Members discussed options for the display, including the need to celebrate “outstanding moments” in Ladysmith versus only individuals. Members hope to meet with Mayor Hutchins at an upcoming meeting to clarify the project, and extent of involvement by HRAC. d. HRAC 2013 Projects – Budget Resolution A. Vincent outlined the bu dget for proposed 2013 projects. Members supported the inclusion of Heritage Week/Community Education, the Transfer Beach Pictorial, the Community Heritage Register update, and re -examining the Strategic Pl an (either through the Town’s own analysis, or through a facilitator). 8. NEW BUSINESS a. Advisory Design Panel (ADP) Appointment HRAC members nominated T. Hutchinson and A. Rogers to jointly serve as the HRAC representative for the Advisory Design Panel. Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission November 8, 2012 3 b. Joint Meeting with Nanaimo Heritage Commission Members were supportive of hosting the annual Nanaimo/Ladysmith Heritage Commission joint meeting for 2013. The suggested date is December 6th, at the new Ladysmith Museum. A. Vincent will contact the Nanaimo Heritage Commission to determine interest. c. Heritage BC Annual Conference Report A. McDermid presented a summary of the Conference and walking tour of Strathcona. He reported that Heritage BC intends to update guidelines for writing Statements of Significance, further develop the Heritage Legacy Fund, provide community workshops and improve communication. d. Heritage Week Members discussed opportunities for Heritage Week 2013, “Heritage Neighborhoods”. Suggestions for topics included former cultural neighborhoods, the miner’s cottages, and number of homes moved to Ladysmith from other locations. T. Hutchinson suggested that HRAC consider how to involve/educate more people. Event suggestions included scavenger/photo hunts, informational brochures, and using the museum as event space. A Heritage Week sub-committee, consisting of M. Martin and B. Knol, will begin preparations. 9. CORRESPONDENCE a. Heritage BC Quarterly: Summer 2012 The newsletter was distributed to members. 10. NEXT MEETING Tentatively set for Thursday, December 6, 2012 at 7:00 p.m. at the Ladysmith Museum. 11. ADJOURNMENT It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 8:55 p.m. ____________________________________ Chair (J.
