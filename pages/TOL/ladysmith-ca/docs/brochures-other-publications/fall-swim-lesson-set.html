Private lessons can be booked for $25 for 30 minutes &amp; semi-private are $15 per person for 30 minutes. Aqua Adults: Would you like to add swimming to your fitness &amp; recreation portfolio? Designed for beginner or intermediate swimmers, the Aqua Adult swim lessons are hour long sessions with a half hour lesson and half hour practice time. Parent-included Levels (6-36 months) Experiencing buoyancy, movement, entries, songs &amp; games in the water. (Must be able to hold head up) Starfish: 6-12mos. Duck: 12-24mos. Sea Turtle: 24-36mos. Preschool (3-5 yrs) Sea Otter: Transition from Sea Turtle &amp; greater independence. An introductory level for new swimmers. Salamander: Actively learn new swimming skills including floats &amp; glides through games &amp; songs. Sunfish: Work on stroke &amp; skill progressions, water safety &amp; deep water. Crocodile: Start independent glides &amp; kicking in deep water. Endurance increased by distance swims on front &amp; back. Whale: Swim independently &amp; participate in team games. Increase distance swims &amp; improve skills in front &amp; back swims. School age levels (5 yrs+) Swim Kids Level 1: Entry level; orientation to the water &amp; pool, introduces submersions, front &amp; back floats &amp; glides with kicks. Swim Kids Level 2: Increase skills in front &amp; back swims, introduced to deep water activities &amp; use of floatation devices. Swim Kids Level 3: Introduction to front crawl, diving &amp; learn wise choices for when &amp; where to swim. Swim Kids Level 4: Advancing front crawl &amp; begin back crawl, kneeling dives, surface support &amp; developing self- safety. Swim Kids Level 5: Introduction to back crawl, sculling &amp; whip kick on back, stride dives &amp; safe boating skills. Swim Kids Level 6 : Improved technique of front &amp; back crawl, introduction backstroke, ice safety, throwing assists &amp; front dives. Swim Kids Level 7: Build skills &amp; endurance for front &amp; back crawl, backstroke. Sculling, whip kick &amp; breathing obstructions. Swim Kids Level 8: Introducing breaststroke, eggbeater kick, foot-first surface dives &amp; standing dives, learn about hypothermia, the dangers of open water swimming &amp; rescue breathing. Swim Kids Level 9 : Increased development of strokes learned; Head first surface dives, making wise choices; complications with rescue breathing, ice self-rescue, &amp; boating regulations. Swim Kids Level 10: Refine all strokes, fitness &amp; safety; rescue of others, surface dives with underwater swims &amp; what’s next.
