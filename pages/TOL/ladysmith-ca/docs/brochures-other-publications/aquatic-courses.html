INFO / REGISTRATION 250.245.6424 | ladysmith.ca / ladysmithPRC follow us subscribe prc@ladysmith.ca Step 1 involves 3 components: Swim stroke &amp; water safety skills screening, online modules &amp; a teaching practicum. The swim stroke &amp; water safety skills screening is designed to ensure candidates can swim to a Red Cross Kids Level 10 standard &amp; will include an evaluation. At the end of the stroke screening session, successful candidates will continue onto the online portion of the course. *Candidates must have a current Emergency First Aid Certificate. SUNDAY / OCT 2 / 9:00-5:00 PM $225 Develop swimming &amp; lifesaving skills, knowledge, personal fitness, leadership &amp; teamwork. Designed by the Lifesaving Society for a serious purpose, but the process is pure fun. FRIDAY / OCT 7 - DEC 16 / 5:30 - 6:30 PM / $67.05 (10 Sessions) T eaches an understanding of the lifesaving principles embodied in the four components of water rescue education - judgement, knowledge, skill &amp; fitness. Rescuers learn both tows &amp; carries, &amp; release methods in preperation for challenging rescues of increased risk. Lifesavers learn to respond to increasingly complex situations involving concious &amp; unconcious victims with an obstructed airway. Lifesavers also develop stroke efficiency &amp; endurance in a timed swim.
