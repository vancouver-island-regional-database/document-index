2014 LOCAL GOVERNMENT ELECTIONS – IMPORTANT DATES 2014 Tuesday, May 13: Last day to meet the six-month BC residency requirement for voting and being a candidate Friday, August 22: Candidate nomination packages available at City Hall and www.ladysmith.ca Tuesday, September 30: Start of candidate nomination period Tuesday, September 30: Information Session for Potential Candidates at 6:30 p.m., City Hall (date changed from September 29) Friday, October 10: Nomination period ends at 4:00 p.m. Tuesday, October 14: Deadline for challenge of nominations at 4:00 p.m. Wednesday, October 15: Last day to meet the 30-day Ladysmith residency / property ownership requirement to be eligible to vote in Ladysmith Friday, October 17: Deadline for withdrawal of candidates at 4:00 p.m. Friday, October 31: Last day to request mail-in ballots from City Hall Wednesday, November 5: Advance Voting Ladysmith City Hall, 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Wednesday, November 12: Advance Voting Ladysmith City Hall, 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Saturday, November 15: ELECTION DAY Frank Jameson Community Centre, 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. 2015 Friday, February 13, 2015: Deadline for all candidates to file Financial Disclosure Forms with Elections B.C. at 4:30 p.m. For More Information: Joanna Winter, Chief Election Officer: jwinter@ladysmith.ca 250.245.6417 Sandy Bowden, Deputy Chief Election Officer: sbowden@ladysmith.ca 250.245.6404 elections@ladysmith.ca 250.245.6400
