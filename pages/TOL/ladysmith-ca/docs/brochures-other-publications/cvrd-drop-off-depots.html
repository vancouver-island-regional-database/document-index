All waste cannot exceed 1.25 m in length or 100 kg in weight and must be free from recyclables. 3. No person shall deposit prohibited waste at the CVRD depots. Call the recycling hotline for more information. 4. All fiberglass insulation and dusty material must be contained in a double bagged system. 5. Bings Creek and Peerless Road have locations where you can drop off items in good condition that are usable/ repairable. Items are accepted at the discretion of the recycling attendant and are charged applicable fees for drop off. Items in the free store are free to pick up.
