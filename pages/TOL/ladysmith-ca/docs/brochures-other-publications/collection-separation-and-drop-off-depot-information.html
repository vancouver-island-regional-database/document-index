What Goes Where? Managing Household Waste in Ladysmith COLLECTED ON ALTERNATING WEEKS Unlimited amounts (Items must be clean &amp; dry) Printed Paper:  Newspapers, inserts and flyers  Magazines and catalogues  Writing and home office paper, paper envelopes and notepads  Paper gift wrap and greeting cards  Telephone books and other directories Paper Packaging (clean &amp; dry):  Cardboard and boxboard boxes  Paper bags &amp; pet food bags  Paper cups for hot and cold beverages  Unsoiled pizza boxes  Moulded boxboard for egg cartons, take out beverage trays, paper-based flower pots  Frozen dessert boxes  Tetra-pack (aseptic) boxes or cartons for dairy, milk-type beverages, soup, broth, sauces  Gable-top cartons for dairy, milk-type beverages, substitute eggs Plastic Containers (clean &amp; dry):  Plastic jugs and bottles  Plastic jars (wide mouth)  Plastic clamshell containers  Plastic pails (less than 25L)  Plastic trays  Plastic garden pots and trays  Plastic tubs and lids  Hard plastic packaging for screws, picture hangers, etc.  Plastic cold drink cups with lids  Microwavable bowls and cups Metals (clean &amp; dry):  Empty aerosol containers used for food, air fresheners, shaving cream, deodorant, hairspray  Aluminum foil and foil containers  Spiral wound cardboard cans and metal lids for frozen juice, potato chips, cookie dough, coffee, nuts, baby formula, etc.  Cans and tins with lids (paper taken off) COLLECTED EVERY WEEK Unlimited amounts in paper bags or certified compostable bags Food:  Baked goods &amp; ingredients  Butter, cheese, mayonnaise  Coffee grounds, filter, tea bags  Cooked food, leftovers  Meat, poultry &amp; bones  Bread, cereal, grain  Fish, seafood  Fruit &amp; vegetable scraps  Nuts and shells  Candy  Egg shells  Pasta, rice Soiled Paper/Paper Packaging:  Gable top juice and soy milk cartons (plastic spout removed)  Ice cream cartons (wax coated)  Milk and cream cartons  Paper cups and plates  Paper fast food packaging  Soiled and waxed cardboard  Paper towels, napkins, tissues  Pizza boxes  Waxed paper  Parchment paper Other:  Houseplants, cut or dried flowers Use a lid to keep animals out * No sharp objects such as syringes or broken glass COLLECTED ON ALTERNATING WEEKS ONE 77L/17 Gallon container up to 20kg/44lbs  Baby wipes  Candy wrappers  Cigarettes and butts  Cotton balls and swabs  Dental Floss  Dryer Sheets &amp; lint  Feminine products  Foil butter wrappers  Hair, wool, feathers  Make up remover pads  Pet waste (must be double bagged)  Rubber bands  Soiled plastic wrap and foil  Soiled diapers and sanitary products  Textiles  Twist ties  Vacuum bags Additional garbage container tags can be purchased at City Hall and the Frank Jameson Community Centre Note: All depots will be closed on Statutory Holidays, Easter Monday and Boxing Day. Asbestos Contaminated Materials Construction / Demolition Waste Please note:  All loads must be secure and covered by a tarp or other overlay so materials cannot blow off or fall out while in transit. Unsecure loads may be fined!  All waste cannot exceed 1.25 m in length or 100 kg in weight and must be free from recyclables.  No person shall deposit prohibited waste at the CVRD depots. Call the recycling hotline for more information.  All fiberglass insulation and dusty material must be contained in a double bagged system.  Vermiculite is not accepted at any CVRD recycling depot unless proper documentation is provided certifying that it is asbestos-free.  Children and pets must remain in the vehicle at all times.
