DELEGATE Bob Yates / Ladysmith Secondary School Community Theatre – Bob explained that he is a consultant assisting Sch ool District 68 with School Community Connections, which is a program that ha s been helping schools around BC to bring in and involve their communities. The program encourages the co-location of services for students, their families and the community at large within school facilities making greater utilization of available facilities and encouraging collaborative, long-term facilities planning that takes into account the needs of the whole community. Bob is investigating the opportunity to use Ladysmith Secondary School’s theatre for community use. OLD BUSINESS DL108 Update – Staff are working on signage for the park. The Vancouver Island University soccer team will be playing the Victoria Highlanders Development Team on the DL108 Turf field this weekend. Needs Assessment Update – Staff will be meeting with staff from CVRD to determine if our needs assessment can be included in their upcoming recreation survey. High Street / Spirit Square Update – The project is progressing and will be completed by March 31, 2011. Kinsmen Park – Construction for Phase 1 of the playground project will begin March 12.
