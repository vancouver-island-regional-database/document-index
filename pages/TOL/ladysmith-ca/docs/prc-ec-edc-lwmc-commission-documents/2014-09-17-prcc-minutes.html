Page 1 of 1 Town of Ladysmith PARKS, RECREATION &amp; CULTURE COMMISSION Minutes of the Commission Meeting held at Frank Jameson Community Centre Wednesday, September 17, 2014 at 7:00pm PRESENT: Will Vandergrift, Chair; John Perry; Chantal Blumel; Alison Bailie; Bryon Adams; Duck Paterson, Councillor; Clayton Postings, Staff; Julie Tierney, Recorder. ABSENT: Kathy Holmes; Brad Uytterhagen, Area H; Glen Hammond, Area G; Gord Horth, Councillor AGENDA APPROVAL 2014-14: It was moved, seconded and carried that the agen da for the Commission meeting for Wednesday, September 17 , 2014 be approved as presented. MINUTES APPROVAL 2014-15: It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes from the Commission meeting of Wednesday, June 18, 2014 be approved as presented. OLD BUSINESS Sports Tourism Region Select Committee – John Perry informed Commission that at the CVRD board meeting held July 30, 2014, a motion was passed to establish a specific sport tourism function under the existing Economic Development authority, for the purpose of developing sport tourism, and support for community organizations hosting events, throughout the Cowichan Region. NEW BUSINESS Review Position of Chair – Commission discussed the appointment of Chair. 2014-16: It was moved, seconded a nd carried that the Commission elect Will Vandergrift as chair of the Parks, Recreation and Culture Co mmission until Jun 30, 2015. Request from Ladysmith Baseball Association (LBA) – Rod McLachlan, Vice President of LBA presented their request to construc t a scorekeepers building and replace batting cage at Holland Creek Ball Fields. 2014-17: It was moved, seconded and carried that the Commission recommend that Council consider this proposal and that Council request staff to confirm specifications prior to construction.
