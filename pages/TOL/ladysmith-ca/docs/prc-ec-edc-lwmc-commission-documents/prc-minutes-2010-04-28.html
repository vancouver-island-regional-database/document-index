MINUTES APPROVAL 2010-07: It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes for the Commission meeting held Wednesday, February 17, 2010 be approved as presented. OLD BUSINESS Lot 108 Update – The area has been cleared with site preparation scheduled to begin within a week. The turf selection committee, consultants, and staff will meet this week to review the six RFP’s received for turf, supply and installation. Brown Drive Park – Kinsmen Playground – Commission members working on this subcommittee discussed the donations received to date and the work completed to secure additional funding through grants. Dehumidification at FJCC – The upgrade to the dehumidification system at FJCC has been completed. Staff are currently working on the process to upgrade the gymnasium lights to a more energy efficient system as well as more control for light settings. NEW BUSINESS Dog Off Leash Park Proposal – Commissioners reviewed a proposal from residents requesting that a portion of Brown Drive Park be fenced to allow for dogs to run off leash and so cialize in a safe environment. Commissioners further discussed the area at Transfer Beach. This will be discussed in more detail at the next meeting, so that Commissioners can view the proposed area for a dog off leash park. Special Occasion License Requests – Commissioners reviewed the two applications. 2010-08: It was moved, seconded and carried that the Parks, Recreation & Culture Commission re commend that Council consider approving the requests for a slo-pitc h food bank tournament at Aggie Minutes to the Parks, Recreation & Culture Commission Meeting April 28, 2010 Page 2 of 2 Fields on September 4 th and 5th and the Royal Canadian Legion picnic at the horseshoe pitch on September 12. Scotch Broom - Commissioners reviewed a request to destroy invasive, non-native scotch broom plants from schools, playgrounds, parks, along roadways and power lines. Staff will work with the Public Works Department to determine what work can be completed.
