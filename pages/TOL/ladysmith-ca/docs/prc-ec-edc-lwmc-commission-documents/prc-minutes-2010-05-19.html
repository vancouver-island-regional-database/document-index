MINUTES APPROVAL 2010-10: It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes for the Commission meeting held Wednesday, April 28, 2010 be approved as presented. OLD BUSINESS Lot 108 Update – Commissioners will be viewing Lot 108 at the close of this meeting. Brown Drive Park – Kinsmen Playground – Commission members working on this subcommittee discussed the donations received to date including $50,000 from the Town and if acceptable, $15,000 worth of wood fibre from Mike Steeves. Playground equipment has been selected from Henderson Playground, which is a treated scotch pine. The total cost of the playground will be approximately $232,000. Dog Off leash Area – Commissioners will be viewing the proposed area at Kinsmen Park at the close of this meeting. Commissioners reviewed the present dog off leash practice at Transfer Beach noting the confusion with signage in the area above DeKonick Way is a year-round off-lease area. Follow ing further discussion the following resolution was made: 2010-11: It was moved, seconded and carried that the Parks, Recreation & Culture Commission recommend that Council consider implementing the times for the Transfer Beach Dog Off Leash area to year-round.; A N D T H A T d o g s n o t b e p e r m i t t e d a t a n y a r e a o f T r a n s f e r B e a c h d u r i n g large community events. Minutes to the Parks, Recreation & Culture Commission Meeting May 19, 2010 Page 2 of 2 Scotch Broom - Commissioners were updated on the work that volunteers would be doing to remove Scotch Broom in areas around Town. NEW BUSINESS Gazebo & Washroom – Commissioners were informed that the Gazebo and Washroom constructed by the Kinsmen on 6 th Avenue has been completed and that the Grand Opening will be taking place on June 12 at 11am.
