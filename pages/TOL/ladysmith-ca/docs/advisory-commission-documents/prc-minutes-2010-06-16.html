Town of Ladysmith PARKS, RECREATION &amp; CULTURE COMMISSION Minutes of the Commission Meeting held at the Frank Jameson Community Centre Wednesday, Jun 16, 2010 ATTENDANCE Chantal Blumel, Chair Wendy Waage Pat Durban, Director Glen Popenko Lisa Bates Julie Tierney, Recorder Lorena Mueller John Perry Steve Arnett, Councillor Duck Paterson, Councillor ABSENT Will Vandergrift Brad Uytterhagen AGENDA APPROVAL 2010-12: It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda for the Commission meeting of Wednesday, Jun 16, 2010 be approved as amended. MINUTES APPROVAL 2010-13: It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes for the Commission meeting held Wednesday, May 19, 2010 be approved as presented. OLD BUSINESS Lot 108 Update – Field preparation progressing as scheduled. Invasive Species – Chantal Blumel, Wendy Waage and Lorena Mueller will be charting locations of several invasive species in Ladysmith and will present information at the next meeting. Brown Drive Park – Kinsmen Playground – Commission members working on this subcommittee discussed the donations received to date including $50,000 from the Town and if acceptable, $15,000 worth of wood fibre from Mike Steeves. Playground equipment has been selected from Henderson Playground, which is a treated scotch pine. The total cost of the playground will be approximately $232,000. Gazebo &amp; Washroom – The grand opening went well. Staff will be working with Shirley Blackstaff to have interpretative signage completed. NEW BUSINESS Special Occasion Licence Request - Special Occasion License Requests – Commissioners reviewed the two applications. 2010-14: It was moved, seconded and carried that the Parks, Recreation &amp; Culture Commission re commend that Council consider approving the Special Occasion requests for Ladysmith Kinsmen Tournament to be held at Aggie Fields on Jun 19 &amp; 20 11:00am to 6:30pm and Fraternal Order of Eagles to be held at Transfer Beach in the Horseshoe Pitch area on August 8 from 10:00am to 4:00pm. Minutes to the Parks, Recreation &amp; Culture Commission Meeting Jun 15, 2010 Page 2 of 2 Needs Assessment – Staff to create a needs assessment to be completed by residents to ensure the needs of the community are being met. Gurley Jane Sea Wall - Staff informed the Commission that the seawall will require repair and maintenance soon.
