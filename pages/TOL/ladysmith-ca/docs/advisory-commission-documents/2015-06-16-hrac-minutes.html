MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Tuesday, June 16, 2015 at 5:00 p.m. Harbour Heritage Centre, 610 Oyster Bay Drive PRESENT: Chair – Marnie Craig; Members – Ann Rogers, Tamara Hutchinson, Allen McDermid; Council Liaison – Mayor Aaron Stone; Staff Liaison &amp; Recorder - Angela Vincent ABSENT: Jennifer Robinson, Maureen Martin, LDHS Member – Bernardien Knol, GUESTS: Shirley Blackstaff, Barry McDonald and Betty Pearson (Ladysmith Maritime Society) The meeting was called to order at 5:00 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of June 16, 2015 be approved. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of May 19, 2015 be adopted. 3. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES Mayor Stone noted that the Town had applied for a Canada 150 Community Infrastructure Program grant for the Comox Logging &amp; Railway Machine Shop as part of the Sesquicentennial celebrations in 2017. 4. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS None. 5. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES a. Transfer Beach Pictorial A. Vincent provided a summary of the progress of the Transfer Beach Pictorial project, including the work by a graphic designer to create four interpretive boards with the following themes: introduction to Ladysmith history, industrial use of Trans fer Beach , mining and forestry, and the transformation to a recreation space. Members appreciated the design concept and style. The pictorial is intended to be installed in September. 6. NEW BUSINESS a. Joint Meeting with Ladysmith Heritage Groups The Ladysmith &amp; District Heritage Society (LDHS) initiated a mapping project to identify heritage locations with links to stories and photos, to be mapped on a cadastral map base, ideally GIS. LDHS feels there are opportunities to work closely with other local heritage interest groups to integrate the maps with tourism products (i.e. historic walking tour apps). Staff have suggested that holding a joint meeting of HRAC, the Ladysmith Maritime Society and LDHS would be beneficial for sharing in formation and would provide an opportunity to update each other with projects or initiatives re cently completed or in progress, and to increase communicati on, awareness and understanding . This would help to determine opportunities for collaboration and cou ld set the stage for the heritage tourism mapping project. Members were very supportive of holding a joint meeting. Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission June 16, 2015 2 Members also discussed other opportunities for further collaboration and cohesion between heritage interest groups. It was suggested that invitations could be sent to heritage interest groups in Ladysmith to attend HRAC meetings (in addition to the LDHS, who provide a formal representative to attend HRAC meetings as per the HRAC Bylaw). Whereas in the near future, it is the intent of Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) to discuss the potential for external liaison members in addition to L adysmith &amp; District Historical Society for information sharing and joint projects, therefore it was moved, seconded and carried that HRAC provide an invitation to the Ladysmith Maritime Society, the Heritage Preservation Group, and the Stz’uminus First Nation to attend HRAC meetings. b.
