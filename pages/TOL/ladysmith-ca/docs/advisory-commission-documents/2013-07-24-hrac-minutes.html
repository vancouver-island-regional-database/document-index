MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Wednesday, July 24, 2013 at 5:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall PRESENT: Chair – Jennifer Robinson; Members – Tamara Hutchinson, Allen McDermid, Maureen Martin, Ann Rogers ; LDHS Member - Bernardien Knol; Council Liaison – Councillor Bill Drysdale; Staff Liaison and Recorder – Angela Vincent REGRETS: Members – Roman Buta, Gerry Beltgens The meeting was called to order at 5:15 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of July 24, 2013 be approved. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of June 13, 2013 be adopted. 3. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES None. 4. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICTIONS a. Wild Poppy Bistro – 541 First Avenue Members reviewed and supported the facade development proposal for new paint colours, new awning, and signage. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission support the proposed paint colours, awning, and signage for Wild Poppy Bistro at 541 First Avenue. b. Twisted Scissor Lounge – 809 First Avenue Members reviewed and supported the proposed fascia sign. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission support the signage proposal for Twisted Scissor Lounge at 809 First Avenue. c. Little Town Family Consignment – 30 Roberts Street Members reviewed and supported the proposed projecting and fascia signs. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission support the signage proposal for Little Town Family Consignment at 30 Roberts Street. d. Claire’s Hair Design – 834 First Avenue Members reviewed and supported the design of proposed sign, but noted that the sign should be a projecting sign, or located on the staircase or above the garage. It was moved, seconded and carried t hat the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission support the design of the proposed sign for Claire’s Hair Design at 834 First Avenue , subject to a satisfactory location. Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission July 24, 2013 2 e. CMF Construction – 521 First Avenue Members reviewed and accepted the proposed changes to the siding material, including replacing the existing 3” cedar siding with 4” hardi -plank on the south and west-facing facades, with the exception of the lower portion of the façade to the west. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission support the replacement of cedar siding with Hardi-plank as proposed at 521 First Avenue. Members discussed the benefits of upgrading the existing façade colours to coincide with the new siding. The following recommendations were made:  Select a paint colour that is not used in excess in the downtown (i.e. not grey or blue).  Colour suggestions include sage green with off -white trim, or cream/yellow with blue trim, etc., with a third or fourth complementary colour.  Consider reducing the visual impact of a large building by painting as two attached buildings, or using different paint colours , shades, or material in the upper or lower aspects of the building. Once available, members will review the proposed paint colours via email to hasten the process. 5. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES a.
