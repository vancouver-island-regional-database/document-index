MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Thursday, April 16, 2009 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall LADYSMITH PRESENT: Chair – Maureen Martin; Members – John Hinde, Bruce Laxdal, Ann Rogers, Peter Blumel; Council Liaison – Lori Evans; Manager &amp; Recorder – Felicity Adams The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of April 16, 2009 be approved. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of March 17, 2009 be approved. 3. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES None. 4. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES Councillor, L. Evans reported that the He ritage Tourism workshop final report was presented to Council on April 6, 2009. A. Rogers reported that there is continued interest and she will follow-up with the members of Tourism Advisory Committee that were present at the workshop. 5. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS a. Façade application - Lorne &amp; Verna Hastings – 626-622 First Avenue The Development Permit proposal (D P 09-02) for the façade improvement was circulated to members of the HRAC. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission supports the façade improvement for the Hastings Buildings, and respectfully wonders if the colour “Blair Gold” is compatible with the awning. 6. CURRENT PROJECT UPDATES/NEXT STEPS a. Heritage Protection/Statements of Significance B. Laxdal reported that the Province has funding available. The members discussed the project and resources. The focus is on First Avenue buildings by preparing SOS and identifying values to property owners. B. Laxdal will work on the project proposal and consult the staff. b. Extension Mine Explosion M. Martin suggested that a plaque be prepared for the RBC collage to commemorate the Extension Mine explos ion. J. Hinde obtained plaque Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission April 16, 2009 2 information from Heritage B.C. The following wording was suggested for the plaque: “In commemoration of the 32 local coal miners who perished in the Extension Mine explosion of 5 October 1909”. J. Hinde will obtain quotes and timeline. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission support a mine memorial at the RBC collage. 7. CORRESPONDENCE None. 8. NEW BUSINESS a. St. John’s Anglican Church update F. Adams reported that the church is looking at residing. Funding opportunities may be available as a SOS has been prepared for the church. Staff are in contact with the building committee. 9. NEXT MEETING The next meeting is scheduled for Thursday, May 14, 2009 at 7:00 p.m. 10. ADJOURNMENT It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 8:35 p.m. ____________________________________ Chair (M.
