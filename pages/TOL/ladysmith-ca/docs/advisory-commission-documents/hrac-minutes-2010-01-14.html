MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Thursday, January 14, 2010 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall PRESENT: Chair – Maureen Martin; Members – Bruce Laxdal, John Hinde, Ann Rogers, Peter Blumel, Jennifer Lush; Council Liaison – Lori Evans; Planning Technician &amp; Recorder – Stephanie Blumensaat The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of January 14, 2010 be approved. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of December 10, 2009 be approved. 3. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES Councillor Evans reported that at the January 4, 2010 Council meeting, Council made a motion to support the preparation of Statements of Significance for the five buildings the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission recommended be added to the Ladysmith Community Heritage Register. L. Evans also shared Council’s questions and suggestions regarding the Ladysmith Community Heritage Register. 4. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS Façade/Sign Development Permit Application 3060-09-12 Brian &amp; Cheryl Bancroft – 411 First Avenue and 30 Roberts Street The Development Permit proposal (3060-09-12) for the façade improvement was circulated to Members. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission recommend a continuous awning be installed, at 411 First Avenue and 30 Roberts Street, for aesthetic purposes and to provide pedestrians with protection from the elements. Members supported the style and colour of the signage. It was noted that if a continuous awning is installed the signage would need to be relocated. 5. CURRENT PROJECT UPDATES/NEXT STEPS a. Community Heritage Register Update Project S. Blumensaat circulated copies of the draft Statements of Significances as prepared by Christine Meutzner. Staff will email copies to Members for their review. L. Evans relayed that Council inquired whether plaques/signage are, or would be, installed to identify Communit y Heritage Register buildings. b. St. John’s Anglican Church Feasibility Study Update S. Blumensaat noted J. Yardley, is completing the Feasibility Study and will be forwarding Staff a copy upon its completion. He explained to Staff that, in his report, Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission January 14, 2010 2 he will be recommending the removal of the Church’s existing cedar siding and the repair and restoration of the original dr op siding which he discovered underneath. c. Heritage Building Brochure Update B. Laxdal proposed that two heritage brochures be produced. One brochure highlighting downtown heritage resources and a second brochure with heritage resources outside of the downtown core. B. Laxdal circulated a draft downtown heritage brochure. S. Blumensaat explained that the brochure will need to be reviewed by Staff to ensure if meets the standards of the ne w Communications Plan adopted by Council. Members will fact-check and edit the content of the brochure in preparation for the brochure to be forwarded to Staff in March. d. Heritage Week 2010 Update M. Martin explained Heritage Week displays will be set up at the Ladysmith Archives, in the façade window on First Avenue and in a window at the 49 th Parallel Grocery S t o r e . 6. CORRESPONDENCE None. 7. NEW BUSINESS None. 8. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES a. Traveller’s Hotel This item was deferred from the December meeting. Members would still like the Mayor to attend a meeting to provide them with an update on the Traveller’s Hotel. L. Evans will invite the Mayor to attend the February HRAC meeting. 9.
