MINUTES Advisory Planning Commission (APC) Tuesday, August 27, 2013 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall PRESENT: Members – Todd Hancock, Tina Donovan, Pamela Fraser, Bruce Laxdal; Council Liaison – Gord Horth; Staff Liaison – Lisa Brinkman, Planner and Recorder. ABSENT/REGRETS: Tina Donovan GUESTS: Applicant – Brian Senini; Owners – Earl Wallace and Angela Jurisic The quorum requirement was not met. The Acting Chair (P. Fraser) called the meeting to order at 7:05 p.m. 1. AGENDA The APC recommends that the agenda of August 27, 2013 be approved. 2. ADOPTION OF MINUTES The APC recommends that the APC minutes of February 13, 2013 be adopted. 3. COUNCIL REFERRALS See 4a. 4. OFFICIAL COMMUNITY PLAN (OCP)/ZONING BYLAW PROPOSED AMENDMENTS a. OCP Amendment and Rezoning Application 3360-13-02 Portion of Lot A, District Lot 146, Oyster District, Plan VIP78848 Except Part of Plan VIP81319 (Cook Street) Brian Senini APC members asked questions and discussed the proposed rezoning. The Advisory Planning Commission recommends that proposed lots 5 and 6 be removed from the park designation and park zone to permit residential use on the condition that an area for a children’s playground of a standard size be secured in the immediate neighbourhood of the subject property ; and that a suitable amenity be secured for park land development such as a multi -use path, playground or both. 5. CORRESPONDENCE None. 6. NEW BUSINESS None. 7. MONTHLY BRIEFING Planner L. Brinkman provided information regarding proposed Bylaw 1831. 8. NEXT MEETING The next meeting is scheduled for Wednesday, September 11, 2013 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall. 9. ADJOURNMENT It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 8:15 p.m. ____________________________________ Acting Chair (P. Fraser) RECEIVED: ________________________________________ Corporate Officer (S. Bowden)
