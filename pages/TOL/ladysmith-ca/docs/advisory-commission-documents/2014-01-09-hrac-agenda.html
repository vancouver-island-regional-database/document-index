ADJOURNMENT *Attachment MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Thursday, December 12, 2013 at 5:00 p.m. Boardroom, Development Services Office PRESENT: Chair – Jennifer Robinson; Members – Gerry Beltgens, Tamara Hutchinson, Allen McDermid, Ann Rogers ; LDHS Member - Bernardien Knol; Council Liaison – Councillor Bill Drysdale; Staff Liaison and Recorder – Angela Vincent REGRETS: Members – Marnie Craig, Maureen Martin The meeting was called to order at 5:05 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of December 12, 2013 be approved with the following changes: Remove: item 2 Welcome/Introduction Add: New Business item 7(a) Machine Shop Opportunity 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of November 14, 2013 be adopted with the following changes to item 5(b): The Ladysmith Maritime Society (LMS) is interested in organizing a community eve nt on Saturday February 27 , 2014. HRAC will contribute some funding and assist with organizing the event. 3. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES None. 4. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS a. The Revival Shop – 32 High Street Members confirmed the approval of The Revival Shop signage by email and provided formal support. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission support the sign proposal for The Revival Shop at 32 High Street. 5. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES a. Transfer Beach Pictorial Members reviewed the archive photographs to be included in the Transfer Beach Pictorial, and noted that there were no changes to be made to the pictorial text provided at the November 14, 2013 HRAC meeting. Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission December 12, 2013 2 b. Community Heritage Register Update A copy of the completed Statements of Significance (SOS’s ) for the 13 selected heritage buildings were provided to HRAC members. Members were asked to review the SOS’s and provide any feedback at the next HRAC meeting. A. Vincent reviewed the next steps in updating the Community Heritage Register, including notification of building owners, approval by Council, and reporting to the BC Register for Historic Places. c. Heritage Week 2014 B. Knol reported that the Ladysmith Maritime Society (LMS) will be organizing the Heritage Week event. The event will be held on Saturday, February 27, 2014 and will feature the new Harbour Heritage Centre, the Maritime Museum, and heritage boats, along with activities for children and warm refreshments. A. McDermid will work with the Chronicle and Take 5 to ensure there are relevant articles advertising the Heritage Week event. B. Knol will draft a notice to be included in the Town’s utility bill, whether it be a paragraph within the newsletter or a separate in sert. External communication will be reviewed by Corporate Services prior to publication. J. Robinson and T. Hutchinson volunteered to coordinate a potential design and quote for a large coroplast sign advertising Heritage Week . Options will be presented at the January 9, 2014 HRAC meeting. Members recommended that LMS should pursue the placement of an event advertisement hand -out to local schools. 6. NEW BUSINESS a. Machine Shop Opportunity A. McDermid reported that there is a group of residents who are interested in the lease of space at the Machine Shop on Oyster Bay Drive. The space would be used for the purpose of restoring and maintaining heritage rail and machine artifacts, potentially in cluding the Dunsmuir Coach and Locomotive #11. The group is looking for additional volunteers to assist with the administration and formation of a society, and also support for the project. Members invited the group to make a presentation to HRAC. 7. CORRESPONDENCE a. Heritage BC Quarterly: Fall 2013 A limited number of copies of the latest Heritage BC Quarterly were sent to HRAC. Members were asked to share the copies amongst members. This edition includes an article on the restoration of the MV Saravan by LMS. b. BC Association of Heritage Professionals A letter was sent by the BC Association of Heritage Professionals (BCAHP) to notify the Commission of the BCAHP’s mandate, and to request that HRAC hire individuals who are accredited through the association. Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission December 12, 2013 3 8. UPDATES Member A. Rogers will attend the Advisory Design Panel meeting on December 16, 2013 as the HRAC representative to review the draft Development Permit guidelines for Development Permit Area 2. A summary will be provided at the HRAC meeting on January 9, 2014. It was noted that HRAC members should maintain awareness of the review process, and are welcome to attend the public consultation session in the new year. The Commission note d that member A. Rogers will be unable to attend HRAC meeting from January to April as a result of a conflicting work schedule. A. Rogers will continue to work on various HRAC projects and will provide updates to be discussed at the meeting until her return. 9.
