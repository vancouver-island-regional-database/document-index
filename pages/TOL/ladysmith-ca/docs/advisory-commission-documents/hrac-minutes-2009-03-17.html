MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Tuesday, March 17, 2009 at 7:00 p.m. Multi-Purpose Room, Ladysmith Secondary School LADYSMITH PRESENT: Chair – Maureen Martin; Members – Bruce Laxdal, Ann Rogers, Peter Blumel; Council Liaison – Lori Evans; Planner &amp; Recorder – Lisa Brinkman ABSENT: John Hinde The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:05 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of March 17, 2009 be approved with one addition 8(b) Historical Society Annual General Meeting. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of February 12, 2009 be approved. 3. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES a. Meeting with the City of Nanaimo Heritage Commission The HRAC has been invited to meet with the City of Nanaimo Heritage Commission on May 6 2009, 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. in the Board Room of the Nanaimo City Hall at 455 Wallace Street, Nanaimo. A light dinner will be served. The purpose of the meeting is to exchange information regarding heritage matters and initiatives in the respective communities. 4. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES a. Park /Building Naming Councillor, L. Evans reported on the March 2nd, 2009 Council motion regarding the naming of a pavilion to be constructed at Transfer Beach Park. b. Heritage Protection/Statement of Significance Project B. Laxdal reported that Council passed a motion to support HRAC to develop a plan for a Community Heritage Register and Statement of Significance Project. 5. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS None 6. CURRENT PROJECT UPDATES/NEXT STEPS a. Heritage Tourism Workshop A. Rogers summarized the report regarding the Heritage Tourism workshop. A thank-you was extended to those who participated in organizing the workshop. Councillor, L. Evans commented that the Chemainus First Nation has expressed interest in participating in future HRAC events. Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission March 17, 2009 2 b. Heritage Protection/Statements of Significance B. Laxdal reported on the steps that will be taken to apply for funding for the Heritage Protection/Statement of Significance Project. c. Extension Mine Explosion J. Hinde was not in attendance to report on the progress of the initiative to commemorate the extension mine explosion. Comments regarding this initiative were: • A coal image/artifact is needed for the artifact route; • October 2009 is the anniversary of the explosion; • Idea to have a Heritage BC sign – similar to the one by the Credit Union; and • Idea to place a plaque on the existing historical collage in front of the RBC Bank on First Avenue. 7. CORRESPONDENCE a. Heritage BC Quarterly – Winter 2009 L. Brinkman distributed the Winter 2009 ‘Heritage BC Quarterly’ to meeting attendees. 8. NEW BUSINESS a. Bamberton Historical Theatre Production A. Rogers reported on the Bamberton Historical Society ‘Theatre in Museums Project’. b. Historical Society Annual General Meeting The Historical Society will be sending invitations to HRAC members to invite them to their annual general meeting to be held on April 21, 2009 at 7:00 p.m. 9. NEXT MEETING The next meeting is scheduled for Thursday, April 9, 2009 at 7:00 p.m. 10. ADJOURNMENT It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 7:55 p.m. ____________________________________ Chair (M.
