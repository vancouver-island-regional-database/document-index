MINUTES Economic Development Commission (EDC) Tuesday, April 28 2009 at 7:00 a.m. Council Chambers, City Hall LADYSMITH PRESENT: Chair – Peter Richmond; Members – Rob Waters, Alan Burrows, Joan Phillps, Jeremy Lupien; Council Liaison – Steve Arnett; Mayor – Rob Hutchins; City Manager – Ruth Malli; Manager of Development Services - Felicity Adams; Recorder – Diane Webber ABSENT/REGRETS: Moe Lessan, John Craig 1. C A L L T O O R D E R P. Richmond called the meeting to order at 7:12 a.m. 2. AGENDA APPROVAL It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of April 28, 2009 be approved. 3. ADOPTION OF MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of the March 17, 2009 meeting be adopted. 4. CHAIR’S REPORT Chair, P. Richmond reported on the following: • Along with J. Craig met with Marilyn Hutchinson, Economic Development Officer, City of Nanaimo. M. Hutchinson shared information on Nanaimo’s business application and renewal processes. Surveys sent out along with the renewal forms are found to be useful in helping local businesses. • May 14, 2009 American Consul General meeting from 12:00 a.m. – 2:00 p.m. M. Lessan, J. Craig and Councillor, S. Arnett will be attending. • Home Hardware Grand opening was well attended. • Participated in the Ladysmith Chamber of Commerce monthly “Visioning” round table. • LDBA is looking at local resident discount cards as a way to assist businesses during these economic times. • Met with A. McGilvary of Employment Navigators along with J. Surtees, R. McLeod and other members of local groups. Funding is available to support local business programs such as management training. • Vancouver Island Economic Alliance report dated February, 2009 regarding collaborative workforce strategy also supports training as a priority. 5. CORPORATE UPDATE Mayor Hutchins discussed the following: • Top 25 Council priorities. • Town projects related to economic development, including the Trolley service. Economic Development Commission April 28, 2009 • Budget constraints and tax rates, holding commercial and industrial increase to 0 %. • The EDO position will not be replaced at this time; however, the Town will be working with the CVRD EDO, Mr. Geoff Millar. The town contributes to the regional economic development function. • Economic development achievements. Mayor Hutchins answered questions from the EDC members. 6. MANAGER’S REPORT F. Adams reported on the following topics: • Holland Creek Area Plan review. Meeting with development parties and consulting team. • TAC tourism signage recommendations presented to Council. • Regional signage has been forwarded to CVRD. • Marine Sector news release and backgrounder has been sent to sector publications, local media and survey respondents. • Tourism Vancouver Island initiative to update Ladysmith content on the HelloBC website. • Take 5 plans to produce a new “Enterprise” magazine later in 2009. 7. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES a. A. Burrows reported that he distributed the notes from the Heritage Tourism workshop to the members. 8. COMMITTEE REPORTS a. Tourism Advisory Committee • R. Waters reported that the committee met April 8th and will be meeting again on May 6, 2009. • Authors of a new book “The Most Beautiful Villages &amp; Towns of the Northwest” will be visiting Ladysmith and writing an article on Ladysmith. • R. Waters, C. Bancroft, R. Gauthier and G. Millar met to discuss signage at the CVRD north end boundary. C. Bancroft is working on a sign with wording that will include the Chemainus First Nation territorial lands and CVRD with a direction to the Visitor Information Centre. • C. Randall of the TAC has developed a plan for tourism industry development and education, including Superhost. b. Local Harbour Management Committee The committee has been awaiting a new Chief for the Chemainus First Nation. This election is taking place April 28, 2009. c. Marine Sector Survey F. Adams reported that a package has been mailed to the local newspapers and Marine Sector related publications which includes a Press Release, Backgrounder, Boat-Building and Related Marine Manufacturing – Business 2 Economic Development Commission April 28, 2009 Profile and “Where Opportunity Docks” brochure. A letter from P. Richmond along with the Press Release and Backgrounder has been mailed to the respondents of the Marine Sector Survey. d. Professional Service &amp; Knowledge Based Business Sector F. Adams reported that staff investigated additional interview information that might be available for further sector communications and found that there were no additional stories. e. Green Industry A. Burrows and R. Waters met with John Wilson, chair, Environment Commission. 9. NEW BUSINESS a. LDBA Questionnaire Summaries The survey results were reviewed. b. Ladysmith Green Fair Members were encouraged to attend. c. 2009 “Best of Economic Development” Awards The information was received. 10. NEXT MEETING The next meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, May 26, 2009 at 7:00 a.m. 11. ADJOURNMENT It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 9:25 a.m. ____________________________________ Chair (P.
