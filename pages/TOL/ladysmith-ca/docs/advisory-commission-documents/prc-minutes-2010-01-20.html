http://www.ladysmith.ca/docs/advisory-commission-documents/prc-minutes-2010-01-20.pdf November 13, 2014 Town of Ladysmith PARKS, RECREATION & CULTURE COMMISSION Minutes of the Commission Meeting held at the Frank Jameson Community Centre Wednesday, January 20, 2010 ATTENDANCE Chantal Blumel, Chair Will Vandergrift Steve Arnett, Councillor Glen Popenko Lisa Bates Pat Durban, Director Lorena Mueller Brad Uytterhagen Duck Paterson, Councillor John Perry Julie Tierney, Recorder ABSENT Jessica Brown Wendi Waage AGENDA APPROVAL 2010-01: It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda for the Commission meeting of Wednesday, January 20, 2010 be approved as amended. MINUTES APPROVAL 2010-02: It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes for the Commission meeting held Wednesday, November 18 2009 be approved as presented. DELEGATION Arts on the Avenue Committee – Kathy Holmes and Rita Balbona attended the meeting to provide information on Arts on the Avenue. This will be the 12 th year that local artists and entertainers are selected by a jury to showcase their work on First Avenue. This event takes place annually on the last Sunday of August. Changes to this year’s event include: 10 new artists, bringing the number to 40; children’s entertainer; a community group section; and a juried artisan food section. Arts on the Avenue are in need of volunteers both for the day of the event and on an ongoing basis. Ladysmith Sportmen’s Club – Dave Clough and Dave Judson updated the Commission on the important work completed this summer by the club in the various creeks in and around Ladysmith, which include creating fish habitats, creating side channels, and removing garbage. Town staff will meet with them to discuss ways of dealing with storm water runoff into Holland Creek. OLD BUSINESS Lot 108 Update – Issues with bordering land have made it necessary for the plan of the park to be modified slightly; as a result, the Town is in the process of acquiring an acre of land from the landowner on the westerly side. Residents of the Ivy Green mobi le home park have been given all the details they require to make their decision to commit to moving to the new location. Minutes to the Parks, Recreation & Culture Commission Meeting January 20, 2010 Page 2 of 2 Old Business Con’t Tree Protection Bylaw – The status of this bylaw will be considered during budget discussions. Regional Parks – Commissioners were provided with a map of the Stocking Lake area recently purchased by the CVRD’s Park Acquisition Fund. Holland Creek Trails – Work on the trails will commence once the budget process has been completed. Brown Drive Park – Kinsmen Playground – Commissioners were informed that a local church would like to donate money for this project; commissioners will work with Duck to determine next steps for creating this playground; Duck will email commissioners information he has gathered; Julie will also help with this project. Camping in Parks – City of Victoria’s anti-camping bylaw’s unconstitutional status was upheld by Court of Appeal because the number of homeless people exceeds the number of available shelter beds in Victoria. This ruling may set a precedent in our community if the same circumstance exists.
