MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Thursday, January 15, 2009 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall LADYSMITH PRESENT: Chair – Maureen Martin; Members – Ann Rogers, John Hinde, Peter Blumel; Council Liaison – Lori Evans; Manager of Development Services &amp; Recorder – Felicity Adams; Mayor Rob Hutchins ABSENT: Bruce Laxdal The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda be approved with two additions; 7 a) Heritage BC Quarterly and 8 b) Resignations. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of December 11, 2008 be approved. 3. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES a. Park/Building Naming M. Martin reported that the Sub-committee has not met. b. Extension Mine Memorial – seed money This item was deferred to a budget discussion. 4. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES None. 5. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS None. 6. CURRENT PROJECT UPDATES/NEXT STEPS a. Heritage Protection/Statements of Significance It was moved, seconded and carried that Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission recommends that Council supports proceeding with the Heritage Protection/Statements of Significance Project, and that the Town apply for up to $20,000 from BC Heritage Branch for this project. b. Heritage Pamphlet This item is now a part of the Heritage Protection/Statements of Significance Project. c. Street Signs This item has been deferred until Staff’s return. Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission January 15, 2009 2 d. Heritage Tourism Workshop The HRAC agreed to the workshop being held on Saturday, February 28, 2009. Logistics and invitees were discussed. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission budget up to $1,000 for its Heritage Tourism Workshop. 7. CORRESPONDENCE Heritage BC Quarterly was distributed to members. Heritage week is February 16 – 22/09. The Heritage of Education is the theme. M. Martin volunteered to work with Ladysmith &amp; District Historical Society on a display in the façade. 8. NEW BUSINESS a. Joint meeting with City of Nanaimo Heritage Commission C. Sholberg, Nanaimo Heritage Planner, invited HRAC members to attend a joint meeting with Nanaimo’s Heritage Commission. M. Martin will follow-up with C. Sholberg on a date in March, 2009. b. Resignations J. Van Horne and D. Winter have resigned and HRAC requests that these two positions be filled as soon as possible. M. Martin will send J. Van Horne a get well card from the HRAC. Both Members were thanked for their contributions to the work of the HRAC. 9. NEXT MEETING The next meeting is scheduled for Thursday, February 12, 2009 at 7:00 p.m. 10. ADJOURNMENT It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 8:15 p.m. ____________________________________ Chair (M. Martin) RECEIVED: ________________________________ Corporate Officer (S. Bowden)
