MINUTES Advisory Design Panel (ADP) Tuesday, October 13, 2009 at 1:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall LADYSMITH PRESENT: Chair – Brian Bancroft; Members – Brian Childs, Kaien Shimizu; Alternate Council Liaison – Bruce Whittington; Planner– Li sa Brinkman; Recorder – Diane Webber ABSENT: Member - Ron Kinney; Council Liaison – Scott Bastian GUESTS: Luke Kolk, applicant; Alan Lowe, architect for R. Jackson; Dave Ehrismann, Peggy Polkinghorn, Bud Pollack, Martin Dunsmuir and Tim Richmond – Ladysmith Maritime Society, applicant. The meeting was called to order at 1:00 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of October 13, 2009 be approved. 2. NOMINATION/ELECTION OF CHAIR Brian Bancroft was nominated and accepted the no mination as Chair. There were no further nominations. B. Bancroft was acclaimed as Chair. 3. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of the April 21, 2009 meeting be adopted. 4. DEVELOPMENT APPLICATION REFERRALS a. Official Community Plan and Zoning Bylaw Amendment Application 3360-09-03 Holland Creek Holdings – Luke Kolk B. Childs left the meeting at 1:10 p.m. due to a conflict of interest. Planner, L. Brinkman provided a summary of the Official Community Plan and Zoning Bylaw Amendment application for 315 Holland Creek Place to the ADP Members. A topographic survey of the parcel was also provided by Planner, L. Brinkman. The Advisory Design Panel discussed the application and asked questions of the applicant, L. Kolk. L. Kolk offered the following information: • Lower density with a larger square footage per unit was chosen. • The subdivision would be a 55+ retirement setting. • The cost of the units will be in the $350,000 - $400,000 price range. • Noise from Dogwood Drive should not be an issue because of the lower elevation of the property. Councillor, B. Whittington suggested dedicating a portion of the land bordering Holland Creek Trail to the Town, as a Park, as opposed to having a covenant placed on the Title. L. Kolk stated he would support this option. Advisory Design Panel October 13, 2009 2 It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Design Panel advises Council that it supports the Official Community Plan and Zoning Bylaw Amendment application (3360-09- 03), Holland Creek Holdings for a density of 13 units and support the site plan by delinea designs, dated August 28, 2009. B. Childs rejoined the meeting. b. Development Permit Application 3060-09-09 R. Jackson – White Street and Esplanade Avenue Planner, L. Brinkman presented a review of the application for the 11 unit project, consisting of a duplex, triplex and sixplex on the corner of White Street and Esplanade Avenue. Planner, L. Brinkman explained the prop osed setbacks which do not meet the required setbacks of the R-3 zone. The proposed setbacks are 4.5 metres from the front lot line, and 3 metres from the rear and side lot lines. The ADP asked questions of the project architect, A. Lowe, and discussed the proposed plans. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Design Panel advises Council that it supports the reduced setbacks for the Development Permit application (3060-09-09). It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Design Panel advises Council it supports the design as presented for the Development Permit application (3060-09-09), White Street and Esplanade Avenue. c. Development Permit Application 3060-09-10 Ladysmith Maritime Society – Visitor Centre Planner, L. Brinkman provided a summary of the Development Permit application (3060-09-10) for the Ladysmith Maritime Society - Visitor Centre. D. Ehrismann, Executive Director of the Ladysmith Maritime Society, explained the uses of the Visitor Centre at the community marina and the specifics of the proposed building. P. Polkinghorn, designer, presented the plans for the proposed Visitor Centre. After a discussion, the members of the Advisory Design Panel had the following concerns: • No “WOW” factor. • Nothing showing West Coast design. • Doesn’t look like a Visitor Centre - when approached from the water it should be clear where to go (presenting entrance to Ladysmith from the water). • Would like to see a break up of the massing. • Add green building design – water retention (opportunity of interior savings). Advisory Design Panel October 13, 2009 3 It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Design Panel does not support the design as presented for the Development Permit application (3060-09-10), Ladysmith Maritime Society – Visitor Centre. 5. NEW BUSINESS None. 6. MONTHLY BRIEFING Planner L. Brinkman reported that the Town has received funding for the new Community Service Centre/Civic Space that is currently undergoing the rezoning process. The architect for the project is Franc D’Ambrosio. 7. NEXT MEETING – Tuesday, November 17, 2009 at 1:00 p.m. – Council Chambers, City Hall. 8. ADJOURNMENT It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 3:00 p.m. __________________________________ Chair (B.
