Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission February 17, 2015 2 6. NEW BUSINESS a. Community Heritage Register Presentation Archivist Christine Meutzner presented to HRAC members on determining the heritage value of sites and buildings. She noted that Statements of Significance are not intended to provide a story of a heritage place , but to record the value. T ypes of value may include (but are not limited to),  Aesthetic - Architectural style, the work of an architect, engineer or planner, or a contextual position in a larger landscape/streetscape  Cultural/Historical Value - Association with a particular historical event, trend , person, organization, activity, pattern, etc.  Scientific - Capacity of place to provide evidence that advances knowledge/ appreciation of culture  Social - Meanings attached to a place in the present time.  Spiritual - Meanings attached to a place with religious or spiritual meanings. C. Meutzner discussed examples within Ladysmith and HRAC members were invited to identify the value associated with the heritage place. She noted that the Community Heritage Register should include heritage sites or buildings that are representative of the general lifestyle and norms of a community, such as the miner’s/worker’s residences, and could consider entire residential blocks that demonstrate this history. C. Meutzner also noted that while Ladysmith had a very ethnically diverse population, there is little representation of this on the Community Heritage Register. Members thanked C. Meutzner for providing a thought-provoking presentation. 7. CORRESPONDENCE a. Heritage BC – Heritage Week poster Members viewed the 2015 Heritage Week poster and commented on the interesting photo compilation of new and archival photos. 8.
