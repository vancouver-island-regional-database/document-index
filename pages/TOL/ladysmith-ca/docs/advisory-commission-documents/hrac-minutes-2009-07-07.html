SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Tuesday, July 7, 2009 at 3:30 p.m. Boardroom, Development Services Office PRESENT: Chair – Maureen Martin; Members – Bruce Laxdal, John Hinde, Ann Rogers, Peter Blumel, Jennifer Lush; Council Liaison – Lori Evans; Manager of Development Services and Recorder – Felicity Adams ABSENT: Dana McFarland 1. CALL TO ORDER The Chair called the meeting to order at 3:30 p.m. 2. AGENDA Reconsideration of Resolution regarding St. John’s the Evangelist Anglican Church The Members met to reconsider the staff recommendation regarding support for St. John’s the Evangelist Anglican Church, and the June 30, 2009 resolution. It was moved and seconded and carried that we: Recommend to Council to allocate $2250 of HRAC funds to assist with the undertaking of the ‘St. John’s the Evangelist Anglican Church Feasibility Study’ so that the Church may apply for heritage restoration funding; and Undertake to meet with the Church, architect and ot her involved parties as the project unfolds, and be copied into relevant reports and documents, with a view to enhancing our understanding of such projects in order to increase our group’s and the Town’s capacity in this regard. We would also offer whatever assistance we can as appropriate; and Request that Council make its support sufficiently broad so as to be useful as a statement of support to the Church and consultant in requesting funds from the BC Heritage Legacy Fund. 3. ADJOURNMENT It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 3:45 p.m. ____________________________________ Chair (M.
