MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Thursday, February 12, 2009 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall LADYSMITH PRESENT: Chair – Maureen Martin; Members – Bruce Laxdal, Ann Rogers, John Hinde, Peter Blumel; Council Liaison – Lori Evans; Manager of Development Services &amp; Recorder – Felicity Adams The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda be approved with the addition of 8 b) Take 5 Advertising. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of January 15, 2009 be approved. 3. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES a. Park/Building Naming The sub-committee met and concluded that a park pavilion was the best idea. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission recommend to Council that if and when a park pavilion is built at Transfer Beach Park, it be named the “Kay Grouhel Pavilion”. b. Joint meeting with City of Nanaimo Heritage Commission M. Martin will arrange a joint meeting for May 6, 2009 at 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. Location to be determined. 4. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES a. Heritage Tourism Workshop Councillor L. Evans reported that Council supported the Heritage Tourism Workshop. b. Commission Vacancies Councillor L. Evans reported that Council is reviewing applications. 5. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS None 6. CURRENT PROJECT UPDATES/NEXT STEPS a. Heritage Tourism Workshop A. Rogers reported that the project is going well. About 15 people have confirmed; expecting more. A $1200.00 grant has been received from Heritage BC for the facilitator. Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission February 12, 2009 2 b. Heritage Protection/Statements of Significance B. Laxdal will contact the Heritage Branch regarding funding for 2009/2010. Councillor L. Evans recommended the Cumberland layout for its heritage buildings brochure. A. Rogers will speak to M. Sacht, of Take 5, regarding a Town/HRAC credit on the artifact route map in the Visitor’s Guide. 7. CORRESPONDENCE None 8. NEW BUSINESS a. Budget F. Adams reviewed a draft budget. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission include $2,000.00 for the interpretative history program in the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission 2009 budget. Members discussed the idea of a plaque installed at the Ore Car on the Artifact Route to commemorate the October 1909 Extension Mine explosion. J. Hinde will contact R. Goodacre, Heritage BC, regarding heritage plaques. b. Take 5 Advertising This item was discussed under 6 b). 9. NEXT MEETING The next meeting is scheduled for Thursday, March 12, 2009 at 7:00 p.m. It was noted that a joint commission meeting may replace the regular HRAC meeting. 10. ADJOURNMENT It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 8:20 p.m. ____________________________________ Chair (M.
