MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Thursday, November 14, 2013 at 5:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall PRESENT: Chair – Jennifer Robinson Members – Gerry Beltgens, Maureen Martin , Tamara Hutchinson, Allen McDermid, Ann Rogers ; LDHS Member - Bernardien Knol; Council Liaison – Councillor Bill Drysdale; Staff Liaison and Recorder – Angela Vincent The meeting was called to order at 5:00 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of November 14, 2013 be approved with the following addition: 4(a) RBC Sign Permit Application. 2. RBC – 521 First Avenue Members reviewed the proposed signage. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission approve the sign proposal for RBC at 521 First Avenue. 5. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES a. Transfer Beach Pictorial On November 6 2013 A. McDermid and A. Vincent met with Archivist C. Meutzner and Director of Development Services F. Adams in regards to the pictorial text and photographs, as well as potential design concepts and locations. Members reviewed the pictorial content prepared by C. Meutzner and commented that the text was concise, well put-together and interesting. Next steps include obtaining quotes for graphic design and installation services. b. Heritage Week 2014 (“Heritage Afloat!” February 17-23, 2014) B. Knol updated the Commission members with progress on contacting other local heritage organizations. The Ladysmith Maritime Society (LMS) is interested in organizing a community event on Saturday February 27 , 2014. HRAC will contribute some fund ing and assist with organizing the event. The Arts Council may create a “Heritage Afloat” art display in the Coast Realty window. Heritage BC Conference Report M. Martin and G. Beltgens provided a report on the Heritage BC Conference held on October 18 and 19, 2013. They reported on the changes taking place at Heritage BC, inc luding a new Executive Director, a new President, and a renewed focus on sustainability. Some concepts discussed at the conference included the integration of heritage and technology (i.e. QR codes on buildings), and the integration of heritage and economic development (i.e. affordable rent for businesses in heritage buildings to attract young entrepreneurs).
