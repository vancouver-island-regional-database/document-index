MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Thursday, January 10, 2013 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall PRESENT: Chair – Jennifer Robinson; Members – Tamara Hutchinson, Allen McDermid , Maureen Martin, Ann Rogers , Gerry Beltgens; LDHS Member – Bernardien Knol; Council Liaison – Councillor Bill Drysdale; Staff Liaison and Recorder – Angela Vincent; Director of Development Services – Felicity Adams ABSENT: Member – Roman Buta GUESTS: R. Basi, Altus Group (for TELUS) The meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m. 1. INTRODUCTIONS Chair, J. Robinson welcomed new member, Gerry Beltgens, to the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission. New member Roman Buta provided his regrets. 2. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of January 10, 2013 be approved. 3. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of December 6, 2012 be approved. 4. COUNCIL REFERRALS a. Monopole Tower Proposal – TELUS building, 11 Roberts Street R. Basi of the Altus Group, representing TELUS, presented the proposed TELUS monopole tower, to be located at 11 Roberts Street. The proposed wireless communication tower is 14.9m high and contains the antennas in the pole’s interior. Three siting options at 11 Roberts Street were provided for the towe r and ancillary equipment cabinets , including alternatives with reduced visibility from the street, retention of the existing garage, and retention of the mature tree on site. HRAC members reviewed the siting options and sample visual impact photos. The Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission provide d the following recommendations to Council for the proposed TELUS Monopole Tower at 11 Roberts Street: 1. That the tower be located at Siting Option C; 2. That the tower be painted dark green to reduce visual impact; and, 3. That TELUS also consult with the surrounding neighbours. b. Outstanding Citizen Display Councillor Drysdale noted that Council intends to ask R. Johnson to oversee the Outstanding Citizen Display project. Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission January 10, 2013 2 5. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICTIONS None. 6. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES a. Transfer Beach Pictorial A. McDermid reported on his meeting with Mayor Hutchins regarding the Transfer Beach Pictorial, and provided a summary of the project for the newer members. The pictorial will focus on the historical use of Transfer Beach, in particular by the coal industry. Members discussed ideas for the physical layout of the pictorial, and agreed that it would be beneficial to proceed with the creation of a design to (a) help inform the pictorial’s content, and (b) assist in securing sources of funding. A. Vincent will pursue options for a professional design. The Transfer Beach Pictorial sub -committee intends to meet on January 30 th at the La dysmith Archives to continue work on the content required. G. Beltgens joined the sub-committee. b. Heritage Inventory Digitization A. Vincent provided an update of the photo digitization work completed by the Ladysmith Archives. All images contained in the 1990 Heritage Inventory have been digitized at a high r esolution, along with the digitization of many other photos that may be useful in the updated heritage inventory. Volunteers at the Archives completed the task in a short time period, and are commended by HRAC members for their hard work. c. Heritage Week Heritage Week will be held February 18 th to 24 th, and the 2013 theme is “Good Neighbours – Heritage Homes and Neighbourhoods”. Members discussed options and decided to highlight the uniqueness of Ladysmith’s miner’s cottages , transportation of homes, and former cultural neighbourhoods. The Ladysmith Archives will be approached to determine interest in conduct ing research into these topics. The research will be presented in a poster format to be displayed at several locations in Town, such as the libra ry, FJCC, City Hall and 49 th Parallel Grocery Store. Members also discussed coordinating with the Chronicle to raise awareness of Ladysmith’s heritage homes and the Heritage Week displays. 7. NEW BUSINESS a. HRAC meeting time discussion A. Vincent noted that there is the option of moving the HRAC monthly meeting from 7:00 pm to 5:00 pm. Members were in favour of the time change. Staff will ensure the new meeting time is also convenient for new member R. Buta prior to formally changing the meeting time. Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission January 10, 2013 3 8.
