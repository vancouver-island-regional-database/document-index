MINUTES OF A MEETING OF COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF LADYSMITH HELD IN COUNCIL CHAMBERS AT CITY HALL ON LADYSMITH TUESDAY, OCTOBER 4, 2011 COUNCIL MEMBERS PRESENT: Mayor Rob Hutchins Councillor Steve Arnett Councillor Jillian Dashwood Councillor Lori Evans Councillor Duck Paterson Councillor Bruce Whittington COUNCIL MEMBERS ABSENT: Councillor Scott Bastian STAFF PRESENT: Ruth Malli Felicity Adams Erin Anderson Sandy Bowden Joanna Winter CALL TO ORDER Mayor Hutchins called the meeting to order at 7:01 p.m. AGENDA APPROVAL Mayor Hutchins requested Council’s consideration of the following additions to the agenda: 10.5 Town of Ladysmith Development Cost Charges Bylaw 1781 11.14 Smart Meters 11.2 Affordable Housing Tax Exemption 12.1 Lights on Stairs to Waterfront 12.2 Playground Signage 2011-420 It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda for the Council Meeting of October 4, 2011 be adopted as amended. MINUTES 2011-421 It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes of the Regular Meeting of Council held September 19, 2011 be approved as circulated. PROCLAMATIONS Mayor Hutchins proclaimed e The week of October 16 to 22 as ‘Small Business Week’ in the Town of Ladysmith COUNCIL COMMITTEE REPORTS Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission—Proposed Town Logo Update 2011-422 It was moved, seconded and carried that the motion of support from the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission for the proposed design of an updated Town of Ladysmith logo be received. Government Services Committee Recommendations 2011-423 It was moved, seconded and carried that the Emergency Management Agreement with the Cowichan Valley Regional District be endorsed and that the Mayor and Corporate Officer be authorized to sign the agreement. 2011-424 It was moved, seconded and carried that the existing solid waste collection contract with BFl Canada be extended for an additional one year term to expire on September 30, 2012. 2011-425 It was moved, seconded and carried that staff be authorized to issue a Request for Proposals for the % ton Public Works truck scheduled for replacement, with the funds to come from the Equipment Replacement Reserve. STAFF / ADVISORY Permissive Tax Exemption Bylaws COMMITTEE REPORTS Mayor Hutchins and Councillor Arnett declared a conflict of interest with the following agenda item and excused themselves from the meeting. Deputy Mayor Dashwood assumed the Chair. It was moved, seconded and carried that first three readings be 2011-426 given to Bylaw 1775 (a bylaw to exempt from taxation certain lands and buildings for the year 2012), and to Bylaw 1776 (a bylaw to exempt from taxation certain Island Corridor Foundation lands and buildings for the year 2012). Mayor Hutchins and Councillor Arnett returned to the meeting. Cowichan Valley Regional District Transit Future Plan It was moved, seconded and carried that the vision, goals and 2011-427 network as presented in the Cowichan Valley Regional District Transit Future Plan be supported, and that the Cowichan Valley Regional District Transit Committee be so advised. Council Remuneration 2011-428 It was moved, seconded and carried that staff be directed to prepare a bylaw to establish Council remuneration as follows: Mayor Council Current 20,677 9,466 Jan 1. 2012 21,842 10,467 Jan 1. 2013 23,007 11,468 Jan 1. 2014 24,172 12,470 and that another review be undertaken in three years, and that the remuneration for the Deputy Mayor remain at its current level. CORRESPONDENCE Rear-Admiral N.S. Greenwood, Maritime Forces Pacific Cadet Population Growth Initiative 2011-429 It was moved, seconded and carried that the request for Town support of the Cadet Program be referred to the Parks, Recreation and Culture Commission to determine whether there are other measures to offer the Cadets in addition to the use of the Aggie Hall already provided at no charge by the Town. Maureen Martin, Ladysmith and District Historical Society Request for Grant in Aid 2011-430 It was moved, seconded and carried that a grant in aid of $1,000 be provided to the Ladysmith and District Historical Society in 2011 for the Ladysmith Museum, and that the Society be advised of the process and schedule for applying for Grants in Aid for 2012. BYLAWS Town of Ladysmith Development Cost Charges Bylaw 2011, No. 1762 2011-431 It was moved, seconded and carried that Town of Ladysmith Development Cost Charges Bylaw 2011, No. 1762 be adopted. Town of Ladysmith Permissive Tax Exemption Bylaw 2011, No. 1775 2011-432 It was moved, seconded and carried that Town of Ladysmith Permissive Tax Exemption Bylaw 2011, No. 1775 be read a first, second and third time. Town of Ladysmith Permissive Tax Exemption Bylaw 2011, No. 1776 2011-433 It was moved, seconded and carried that Town of Ladysmith Permissive Tax Exemption Bylaw 2011, No. 1776 be read a first, second and third time. Town of Ladysmith Building and Plumbing Bylaw 1994, No. 1119, Amendment Bylaw 2011, No. 1782 2011-434 It was moved, seconded and carried that Town of Ladysmith Building and Plumbing Bylaw 1994, No. 1119 Amendment Bylaw 2011, No. 1782, be read a first, second and third time. Town of Ladysmith Development Cost Charges Reduction Bylaw 2011, No. 1781 2011-435 It was moved, seconded and carried that Town of Ladysmith Development Cost Charges Bylaw 2011, No. 1781, be read a first, second and third time. New BUSINESS Smart Meters 2011-436 It was moved, seconded and carried that staff be requested to contact BY Hydro to request information on determining the cost of relocating a BC hydro smart meter further from a residence, and to request BC Hydro to communicate this to resident prior to installation of the smart meters. Affordable Housing Tax Exemption The issue of including affordable housing in permissive tax exemptions in the Town of Ladysmith was referred to the October 17, 2011 Government Services Meeting. Installation of a Light at the Top of the Stairs to the Ladysmith Community Marina Staff were requested to report back to Council regarding timelines for installation lights on the stairs to the Ladysmith waterfront. ‘Children at Play’ Signage Staff were requested to report back to Council regarding timelines for installation of ‘Playground’ or ‘Children at Play’ signs at Ladysmith Little League Ball Fields, the Boys and Girls Club and other playgrounds. EXECUTIVE SESSION 2011-437 It was moved, seconded and carried at 6:55 p.m. that Council retire into Executive Session after a two-minute recess, to consider the following items: e the acquisition, disposition or expropriation of land or improvements, if the council considers that disclosure could reasonably be expected to harm the interests of the municipality e the receipt of advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege, including communications necessary for that purpose ARISE AND REPORT Council arose from Executive Session at 8:22 p.m. with no report. ADJOURNMENT 2011-438 lt was moved, seconded and carried that this meeting of Council be adjourned at 8:22 p.m. Mayor (R. Hutchins) CERTIFIED CORRECT Corporate Officer (S. Bowden)
