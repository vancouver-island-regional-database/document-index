TOWN OF LADYSMITH JOB CLASSIFICATION DESCRIPTION TITLE: CITY MANAGER JOB SUMMARY The City Manager reports to Council. This position provides leadership in strategic planning, policy advic e and implements work plans, oversees and is responsible for the operations of the Municipality. PRIMARY DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES  Provides leadership and Initiative in improving the efficiency and effectiveness of all programs and services through ongoing review of programs and services and making recommendati ons for change to the Council.  Coordinates and participates in the ongoing strategic planning process and in the establishment of the annual goals and objectives.  Liaises with and provides assistance to Council. Develops and maintains a resource network regarding local government and related issues.  Provides policy advice and support to Council and Committees by coordinating agendas, ensuring the required information is provided, making recommendations for changes in programs and services, providing commentary on the achievement of goals and objectives and attending meetings.  Develops and maintains positive external relations with Provincial Mi nistries, Federal Ministries and First Nations, educational institutions and other municipal and business associations.  Ensures the development of, recommends and monitors financial plans and budgets (multi -year and annual) by interpreting the strategic direction and goals and objectives set by Council.  Develops alternative sources of revenues and increases reven ues from existing programs and services.  Supervises, provides direction and carries out the performance evaluation of management and contact staff. AREAS OF RESPONSIBILITY  Policy Advice  Performance Management  Strategic Planning  Municipal Service Delivery  Management Team  Town Council  Protective Services - 2 – DECISIONS MADE BY THE CITY MANAGER  Interprets and carries out Council’s direction in terms of strategic plans, goals, objectives, policies and budgets.  Determines in conjunction with Council how to carry out the responsibilities of the position. SPECIFIC DELEGATED AUTHORITY FROM COUNCIL DECISIONS REFERRED TO COUNCIL  New or significant changes in policies, programs and services.  Annual and multi-year budgets.  Expenditures outside of the approved budget.  Changes in strategic direction. PERFORMANCE INDICATORS - Attention to the strategic direction of the Town of Ladysmith - Achievement of work plan targets - Achievement of financial goals - Quality of policy and program advice - Positive relations with external agencies - Positive team relationship with Council, Staff and Community - Responsive to committee needs - Sensitivity to translate citizen needs into programs - Attention to quality control - Effective supervision of management and contractors REPORTING RELATIONSHIP Reports to Council.
