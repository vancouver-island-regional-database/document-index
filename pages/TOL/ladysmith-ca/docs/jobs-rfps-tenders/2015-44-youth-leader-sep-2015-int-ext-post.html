Postings #201 5-44 March 2010 TOWN OF LADYSMITH JOB DESCRIPTION TITLE: YOUTH LEADER DEPARTMENT: PARKS, RECREATION &amp; CULTURE JOB SUMMARY Under the supervision of the Child &amp; Youth Program Coordinator, the Youth Leader contributes to the effective operation of the Department of Parks, Recreation and Culture as a key member of the youth services team. The Youth Leader assists in the planning, coordination and supervision of youth programs, services and events. This posit ion supports the Department by assisting team members in their duties as required, to meet servic e expectations, departmental goals and objectives.
