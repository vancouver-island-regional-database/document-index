We were asked by the Vancouver Island Health Authority | to switch over to the Stocking Lake water supply until the source of the : contamination could be identified and removed. During the switch over, administration determined that there was a trend of utilizing the Stocking Lake water source more and more each subsequent year since 2002. Where in 2002 approximately 30% of the Towns water requirements came from Stocking Lake and 60% came from the Holland Creek watershed, by 2006 the requirement from | Stocking Lake went to 60% of the total and the amount from the Holland Creek | watershed went to 30% of the total. | Council directed staff to engage Castor Consultants Ltd. to determine the cause that reduced the use of the Holland Creek watershed as the main source of the Towns total water supply. Extensive testing throughout the watershed was unable to determine the exact cause but identified a number of contributing factors including natural conditions. In order to minimise the time required to wait for test results which in turn lengthens the time required to switch from Holland Creek watershed to Stocking Lake, the report suggests that an expanded water quality monitoring plan be initiated with the . purchase of state of the art field instrumentation.
