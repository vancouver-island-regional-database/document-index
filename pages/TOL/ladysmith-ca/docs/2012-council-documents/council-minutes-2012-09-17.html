DELEGATIONS Joe Burnett, Mike Hooper, Nanaimo Airport Joe Burnett, Town of Ladysmith representative on the Nanaimo Airport Commission presented an update on activities at the Nanaimo during the past year and the Commission’s strategic plan. J. Burnett and M. Hooper responded to questions. Council thanked J. Burnett for representing the Town of Ladysmith on the Nanaimo Airport Commission.
