Larry Chomyn, Const. Jessica Barnett Royal Canadian Mounted Police Bar Watch Program Const. Barnett reviewed a proposed Good Neighbour agreement to support a Bar Watch Program in Ladysmith, noting that the program is modelled after the Nanaimo Bar W atch Program. Council thanked Const. Barnett for her presentation and agreed to consider the agreement, and provide a response as soon as possible. S/Sgt Chomyn and Const. Barnett left the meeting. It was moved, seconded and carried that the matter of a Bar Watch Program in Ladysmith be referred to the next meeting of the Government Services Committee, and that staff be requested to discuss the program with owners and/or managers of bars in Ladysmith and report back to Council.
