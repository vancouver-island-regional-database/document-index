This serene hillside town overlooks Ladysmith Harbour, just off tranquil Stuart Channel, with the Gulf Islands in the foreground and the spectacular Coast Mountains beyond. Many homes have ocean views, and the water is never more than a few minutes away. One of Ladysmith’s greatest assets is its spectacular 5-mile natural harbour, with sheltered waters deep enough for large yachts. The harbour, with its three marinas, is a favourite of commercial and pleasure boaters alike. It provides a perfect launching point for exploring the Gulf Islands: Thetis and Kuper Islands lie just offshore, and Saltspring, Galiano, Valdes, Gabriola and several smaller islands are just a few miles away.
