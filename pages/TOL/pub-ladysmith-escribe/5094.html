Received May 30, 2022 To whom it may concern, My husband and I are the owners of the property 740 4th Avenue and we are planning to build a single family dwelling. We have no current or future plans to integrate any type of commercial business on the property. We plan to build an energy efficient home that includes a heat pump, energy saving windows, hot water on demand, smart thermostat, smart lighting, paver driveway and patio and be professionally landscaped. Sincerely, Dean & Magdalene Henderson
