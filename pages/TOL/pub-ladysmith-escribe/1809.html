P a g e 1 | 2 Minutes of the Parks, Recreation & Culture Advisory Committee Wednesday, December 15, 2021 at 7:00pm via ZOOM and Frank Jameson Community Centre, 810 6th Avenue COMMITTEE MEMBERS PRESENT: STAFF PRESENT: Tim Richards, Chair Geoff Dean Chris Barfoot, Lead Councilor Duck Paterson Lesley Lorenz REGRETS: Emily Weeks Bruce Mason Kim Cheang Lucy Partington Kelly Daniels Jacqueline Huard Lynda Baker CALL TO ORDER AND ACKNOWLEDGEMENT AGENDA MINUTES The meeting was called to order at 7:37pm. Approve the theme “Community on the Coast” for the 2022 Community - Public Art Banners; 2. Approve the 2022 Community Celebration Banners, celebrating the Centennial of Aggie Hall in a way that is mindful and sensitive of our Stz’uminus First Nation neighbours.
