CommUNITY Together To End Poverty Hw-nuts’-ulwum Town of Ladysmith Delegation to Council October 1, 2024 Community Engagement to Develop the Poverty Reduction Strategy 2020-2021 Stream 1: Community Engagement to develop the Poverty Reduction Strategy 2022-2023 Stream 2: Community Engagement Taking Action 2023-2024 Stream 2, Intake 2: Community Engagement Taking More Action We will end poverty when we understand and make decisions acknowledging that: • Everyone has equal value • Everyone has something to contribute • Everyone deserves a happy and healthy life CommUNITY Vision CommUNITY Values RESPECT INCLUSION DIGNITYCARING SAFETY BELONGING COMMUNITY VISION, VALUES AND PRINCIPLES FOR ENDING POVERTY www.ladysmith.ca/povertyreductionstrategy @communitytogethertoendpoverty • Demonstrate that we are a caring community, that is committed to supporting each other • Ask others what they need - don’t assume - and engage those with lived experience in a meaningful way • Spread random acts of kindness • Achieve a greater impact by working together • Involve everyone to find a way to participate • Understand that our differences are our strengths • Know that change starts within - we are all responsible for our own learning and commitment to better understand the impacts of poverty • Recognize that many living in poverty have experienced trauma that may affect different areas of their life CommUNITY Principles Poverty Reduction Actions & Goals for 2023-2024 Goals: • Improve Access to Education and Employment Options. • Continue to improve Food Security • Move from Education and Awareness into Action on the CommUNITY Poverty Reduction Strategy themes Actions: • In partnership, we will deliver four employer seminar series and connect employers with additional resources and supports following the seminars. • LaFF, LRCA, Stz’uminus Recreation and Town of Ladysmith Recreation programs will develop and deliver a minimum of 12 food equity building workshops. • Host four community dinner and dialogue events to engage community members and develop new community partnerships to move some of the more challenging actions forward. Poverty Reduction Task Group (PRTG) • Terms of Reference Included members from: • Town of Ladysmith • Stz'uminus First Nation • Ladysmith Resource Centre Association • Ladysmith Family & Friends • Ladysmith Chamber of Commerce • Island Health • Our Community Health Network • Social Planning Cowichan • Community Members Food Equity Sub-Committee: • Town of Ladysmith • Stz'uminus First Nation • Ladysmith Resource Centre Association • Ladysmith Family & Friends • Island Health • Social Planning Cowichan ➢ Select Advisory Committee of Council Looking Ahead • With the newly released BC Poverty Reduction Strategy, funding opportunities from the Province for municipalities is currently unknown. • 18 community volunteers have raised their hands to help out with one of more of the selected actions from the Dinner & Dialogues based on the following topics: • Transportation • School Food program • Improving Mental Health Services • Food Equity activities and workshops/community building & food sharing events • Income Supports/GBI • Secondary Suites • The PRTG is committed to continuing with a designated “backbone” organization facilitating the process. Thank you all - Huy tseep q’u https://www.ladysmith.ca/povertyreductionstrategy
