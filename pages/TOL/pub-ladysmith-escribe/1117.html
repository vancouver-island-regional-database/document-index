​ A CLOSED MEETING OF THE COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF LADYSMITH AGENDA Monday, April 15, 2019 Council Chambers, City Hall 1. CALL TO ORDER (5:30 p.m.) 2. AGENDA APPROVAL Recommendation That Council approve the agenda for this Closed Meeting of Council for Monday, April 15, 2019. 3. MINUTES 3.1 Minutes of the April 8, 2019 Closed Session of Council Recommendation That Council approve the minutes for the April 8, 2019 Closed Session of Council. 4. REPORTS 4.1 Stz'uminus Road Access Request Recommendation That Council: 1. Receive the updated staff report regarding the request from the Stz’uminus First Nation to utilize 340 meters within the Park (as shown on Plan VIP84843) to reconstruct a road to access logging blocks in Stz’uminus Woodlot W1713. 2. Consider the alternatives provided in this staff report. 5. RISE AND REPORT Staff recommend rising without report. 6. ADJOURNMENT Page 2 of 2
