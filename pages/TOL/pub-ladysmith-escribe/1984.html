P a g e 1 | 1 Special Meeting Minutes of the Parks, Recreation & Culture Advisory Committee Monday, March 7, 2022 at 7:00pm via ZOOM COMMITTEE MEMBERS PRESENT: STAFF PRESENT: Tim Richards, Chair Geoff Dean Bruce Mason Chris Barfoot, Lead Councilor Duck Paterson Lesley Lorenz REGRETS: Emily Weeks Lynda Baker Lucy Partington Kelly Daniels Kim Cheang Jacqueline Huard CALL TO ORDER AND ACKNOWLEDGEMENT AGENDA The meeting was called to order at 7:03pm. Motion carried. Approve production of the following banners for the 2022 Community Banner Program: I. Hummingbird Season - (Artist Jason Harris) II. Sea Searching (Otter) – (Artist Jason Harris) Motion carried.
