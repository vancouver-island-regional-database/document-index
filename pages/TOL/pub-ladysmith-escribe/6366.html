ATTACHMENT E INFORMATION REPORT TO COUNCIL Report Prepared By: Sue Bouma, Corporate Officer Meeting Date: May 14, 2024 File No: 4200-20 Re: ATTACH E Determination of Estimated Eligible Electors - AAP CITY HALL.docx PURPOSE: The purpose of this report is to show the basis for determining the total number of eligible electors in relation to the Alternative Approval Process (AAP) for the borrowing of up to $13.5 million to finance the building of a new City Hall and Institutional/Commercial Space. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: Section 86 of the Community Charter requires Council to make a fair determination of the total number of electors of the area to which the a pproval process applies (in this ca se, the Town of Ladysmith as defined by its municipal boundaries). In addition, Council must make available to the public on request, a report on the approach used for making the determination. The number of people eligible to be a resident elector or a non -resident elector is determined based on those individuals who, when signing an elector response form:  Are 18 years of age;  Are a Canadian citizen;  Have lived in British Columbia for at least the last six months;  Have lived or owned property in the Town of Ladysmith for at least the last 30 days;  Live or own property in the area defined for the AAP; and  Are not disqualified by law from voting in local elections.
