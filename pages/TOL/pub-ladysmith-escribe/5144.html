| JUL 15 2022 TOWN GF LADYSMITH Ladysmith Lions Club P O Box 2293 Stn Main Ladysmith BC V9G 1B8 ladysmithlions@gmail.com July 15, 2022 to the Mayor &amp; Council Ladysmith BC Dear Mayor &amp; Council At the start of 2022, the Ladysmith Lions, in partnership with McNabs Corn Maze, sponsored a weekly free swim on Wednesday afternoons from 1 ‘til 2. The swim was initiated to accommodate the needs of persons managing developmental challenges in the Ladysmith Community Link group and also to open up a free time for others when our pool would otherwise be closed. Our club would tike to continue this sponcorship in the fall, but would also like the Town to participate as a 1/3 partner in the program. We would then have a public/private/npo partnership that could be promoted in a very favourable light for the Town. The cost of your 1/3 share would be only $742.44 from Sept. through Dec., which we believe would be very good value for the Town. Sincerely, Eric Mah, President Ladysmith Lions Club
