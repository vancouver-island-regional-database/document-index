MINUTES OF A SPECIAL MEETING OF COUNCIL WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 27, 2019 CALL TO ORDER 3:00 P.M. COUNCIL CHAMBERS, CITY HALL COUNCIL MEMBERS PRESENT: Councillor Duck Paterson (Deputy Mayor) Councillor Amanda Jacobson Councillor Robert Johnson Councillor Tricia McKay Councillor Marsh Stevens Councillor Jeff Virtanen (via telephone) COUNCIL MEMBERS ABSENT: Mayor Aaron Stone STAFF PRESENT: Guillermo Ferrero Geoff Goodall Joanna Winter Sue Bouma CALL TO ORDER Deputy Mayor Paterson called this Special Meeting of Council to order at 3:00 p.m. CLOSED SESSION CS 2019-067 Moved and seconded: That, in accordance with section 90(1) of the Community Charter, Council retire into closed session at 3:01 p.m. in order to consider items related to the following: • Sale of Property - section 90 (1)(e), the acquisition, disposition or expropriation of land or improvements, if the council considers that disclosure coul d reasonably be expected to harm the interests of the municipality Motion carried. RISE AND REPORT Council rose from closed session at 3:15 p.m. without report. ADJOURNMENT CS 2019-068 Moved and seconded: That this Special Meeting of Council adjourn at 3:16 p.m. Motion carried.
