TOWN OF LADYSMITH BYLAW NO. 2021 A Bylaw to amend “Town of Ladysmith Zoning Bylaw 2014, No. 1860” WHEREAS pursuant to the Local Government Act, the Council of the Town of Ladysmith is empowered to amend “Town of Ladysmith Zoning Bylaw 2014, No. 1860” ( the “Zoning Bylaw”); AND WHEREAS Council considers it advisable to amend the Zoning Bylaw to permit the retail sale of cannabis in certain zones; NOW THEREFORE the Council of the Town of Ladysmith in open meeting assembled enacts as follows: 1.
