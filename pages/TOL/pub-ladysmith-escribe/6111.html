TOWN OF LADYSMITH BYLAW NO. 2164 A Bylaw to amend the Waterworks Regulations The Council of the Town of Ladysmith in open meeting assembled amends “Waterworks Regulations Bylaw 1999, No. 1298” as follows: 1. Delete Schedule “A” in its entirety and replace with the attached Schedule “A”. Effective Date 2. The provisions of this bylaw shall become effective and be in force as of January 1, 2024. Citation 3. This bylaw may be cited as Town of Ladysmith “Waterworks Regulations Bylaw 1999, No. 1298, Amendment Bylaw 2023, No. 2164".
