MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Thursday, January 12, 2012 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall PRESENT: Chair – Jennifer Robinson; Members - Alle n McDermid, Brian McLaurin, Lynne Brown, and Peter Blumel; LDHS Member – Maureen Martin; Council Liaison – Councillor Bill Drysdale; Staff Liaison and Recorder – Angela Vincent-Lewis ABSENT: Tamara Hutchinson The meeting was called to order at 7:04 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of January 12, 2012 be approved. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of November 10, 2011 be approved. 3. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES a. Summary of Joint Meeting with Nanaimo Heritage Commission Chair J. Robinson presented a review of the joint meeting held with the Nanaimo Heritage Commission in December. The Nanaimo Commission will be hosting the Community Heritage Commission Networking Conference in November 2012. P. Blumel noted the success of the Nanaimo Heritage Façade Improvement Grant Program, and suggested Ladysmith could organize a similar program for downtown commercial buildings. Commission members were in favor of investigating this possibility at a future Heritage meeting. b. Heritage Building Brochures P. Blumel inquired as to the status of the Heritage Building brochures following the discussion at the November meeting. It was explained that the redesign of the brochure cover and subsequent printing has been included in the proposed tourism budget. The brochures will be printed in time for summer. 4. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES a. Introduction of New Council Liaison Councillor Bill Drysdale was welcomed as the HRAC Council Liaison. Mayor Rob Hutchins will serve as the HRAC Council Alternate. 5. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS None. 6. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES a. Heritage Building Plaques A. Vincent-Lewis has worked with the graphic designer to produce the finalized versions of the heritage building plaques. In December, the building owners were sent a letter, along with an image of the plaque created for their building. A. Vincent- Lewis will contact those building owners who have not replied to the letter. The plaques are intended to be unveiled during Heritage Week.

Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission January 12, 2012 2 As discussed at the November meeting, in formation regarding the heritage plaques and Heritage Week will be included in the January utility bill newsletter. b. Heritage Week Display by LDHS M. Martin reviewed the progress of the LDHS Heritage Week display, including the limited information and photographs available. Councillor Drysdale suggested that LDHS approach historian Tom Paterson if further information is required. The façade window located next to Pharmasave has been booked for the Heritage Week display from February 20 th to 27th 2012. 7. NEW BUSINESS a. Transfer Beach Pictorial Members discussed potential designs for th e pictorial, referring to notes from initiating this project in 2005/06. Members were interested in a large collage-style display similar to the collage located on First Avenue, with an artistic and industrial feel. There was some discussion regarding th e location of the pictorial, and reducing the potential for vandalism. L. Brown suggested that a collage would be less vulnerable to vandalism, and could be added to over time. Commission members agreed that a pictoria l sub-committee was required. Volunteer members will include A. McDermid, B. McLaurin, M. Martin and J. Robinson. The sub- committee will begin research into the history of Ladysmith’s waterfront. A. Vincent-Lewis will research the collage on First Avenue and will discuss potential locations with Parks, Recreation and Culture. 8. CORRESPONDENCE a. Nanaimo workshop: Social Media & Online Marketing for Heritage Tourism Operators, February 1st 2012 This free workshop will be held at the Nanaimo Museum on February 1 st 2012. Members are encouraged to sign-up if interested. b. Vancouver Heritage Foundation courses and lectures The Vancouver Heritage Foundation is offering a range of daytime and evening courses and lectures. Further information will be sent to members. 9.
