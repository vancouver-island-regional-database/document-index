TOWN OF LADYSMITH
BYLAW NO. 2151

A Bylaw to amend the Fees & Charges Bylaw

The Council of the Town of Ladysmith in open meeting assembled enacts the following
amendments to “Fees and Charges Bylaw 2008, No. 1644”:

aL, Delete Schedule "1" in its entirety and replace with the attached Schedule "1".
2: Delete Schedule "3" in its entirety and replace with the attached Schedule "3".

Effective Date
3. This bylaw comes into effect January 1, 2024.

Citation

4. This Bylaw may be cited for all purposes as “Town of Ladysmith Fees and Charges
Bylaw 2008, No. 1644, Amendment Bylaw 2023, No. 2151”.
