NOTICE OF ANNUAL
CEMETERY CLEANUP

Town of Ladysmith Parks Crews will be performing
annual maintenance of the Cemetery Grounds from:

March 15th - September 30th, 2023

Pursuant to Town of Ladysmith, Bylaw No. 1668, Section
55, floral tributes will be removed by the Caretaker and
disposed of when their condition is considered to be
detrimental to the appearance and beauty of the
Cemetery.

Floral tributes and trinkets, in an acceptable condition,
removed to allow for maintenance can be picked up by
contacting the Parks Supervisor at 250.245.6448.

Thank you for your patience and co-operation.
