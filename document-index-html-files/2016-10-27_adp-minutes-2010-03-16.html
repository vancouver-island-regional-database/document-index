MINUTES Advisory Design Panel (ADP) Tuesday, March 16, 2010 at 1:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall LADYSMITH PRESENT: Chair – Brian Bancroft; Members – Ron Kinney, Brian Childs; Council Liaison – Bruce Whittington; Planner – Lisa Brinkman; Recorder – Diane Webber; Ruth Malli, City Manager. ABSENT: Member - Kaien Shimizu GUESTS: D’Ambrosio Architecture + Urbanism – Franc D’Ambrosio; HB Lanarc Consultants Ltd - Doug Backhouse. The meeting was called to order at 1:00 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of March 16, 2010 be approved. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of the December 15 2009 meeting be adopted. 3 . C O U N C I L R E F E R R A L S None. 4. DEVELOPMENT APPLICATION REFERRALS a. COMMUNITY SERVICES CENTRE/CIVIC SPACE – F. D’Ambrosio (Architect) 208 HIGH STREET and 217 BULLER STREET The design and landscape plan for the Co mmunity Services Centre/Civic Space was presented to the ADP for information and co mment. Architect, F. D’Ambrosio, along with D. Backhouse of HB Lanarc Consultants Ltd., presented the design and answered questions asked by the Members. City Manager, R. Malli was also present to offer information and answer questions. The Advisory Design Panel had no objections to the plans for the Community Services Centre/Civic Space. 5. NEW BUSINESS None. 6. MONTHLY BRIEFING Planner, L. Brinkman explained to the Me mbers that the Ladysmith Maritime Society (LMS) would like to meet with the ADP with an updated plan for the LMS Visitor Centre. The ADP members agreed to meet with the LMS on Tuesday, March 30, 2010.
