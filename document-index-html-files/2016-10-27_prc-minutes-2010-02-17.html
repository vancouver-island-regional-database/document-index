Town of Ladysmith PARKS, RECREATION & CULTURE COMMISSION Minutes of the Commission Meeting held at the Frank Jameson Community Centre Wednesday, February 17, 2010 ATTENDANCE Wendy Waage Will Vandergrift, Chair Steve Arnett, Councillor Glen Popenko Lisa Bates Pat Durban, Director Lorena Mueller Brad Uytterhagen Duck Paterson, Councillor John Perry Julie Tierney, Recorder ABSENT Chantal Blumel, Chair Lisa Bates AGENDA APPROVAL 2010-03: It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda for the Commission meeting of Wednesday, February 17, 2010 be approved as amended. MINUTES APPROVAL 2010-04: It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes for the Commission meeting held Wednesday, January 20, 2010 be approved as presented. DELEGATION Sealegs Owner, Sheryll Bell – Sealegs Kayaking Adventures operating from Transfer Beach re quested permission to host a kayak and gear demonstration for BC outfitters. Th e event is proposed to take place Saturday, April 3 and Sunday, April 4. Request for space includes the land adjacent to the Eco-Adventure Centre and the kayak launch site and small amphitheatre for boat testing. Sealegs is also requesting permission for vendors to camp on-site with their equipment displays as a security measure. 2010-05: It was moved, seconded and carried that the Parks, Recreation & Culture Commission re commend that staff approve the request for the use of Transfer Beach area adjacent to the Eco-Adventure Centre and the kayak launch site an d camping for vendors at Transfer Beach for this event taking place on April 3 and April 4. Urban Designer & Site Planner from Lanarc – The Commissioners were shown the plans for the Spirit Square, which is to be constructed on the property at High Street. Commission ers were able to provide comment and offer feedback. Commissioners were also invited to the upcoming public meeting to discuss this further. OLD BUSINESS Lot 108 Update – Debris will be hauled away. Site preparation will begin next.

Minutes to the Parks, Recreation & Culture Commission Meeting February 17, 2010 Page 2 of 2 Old Business con’t Aggie Hall Olympic Viewing – Opening ceremony was well attended. Public viewing on the big screen will continue through to the closing ceremony, with each event being hosted by a different group. The Commission request that staff draft a letter to be signed by the chair praising staff for the work completed to ensure this event be successful. Brown Drive Park – Kinsmen Playground – Commission members working in this subcommittee will m eet on Saturday to decide which grants will be applied for and by whom. Dehumidification at FJCC – Staff updated Commission on the work being completed at FJCC to replace the 25-year-old system that is no longer adequate to dehumidify the community centre. New Business Bush Creek Bridge – CVRD have surveyed the creek in order to have a footbridge built. The Ladysmith Sportsmen’s club would rather be wide enough to ATVs so that they stay out of the creek when they cross. Planners at CVRD will be contacted with this request. Chronicle Sports Coverage – a Commissioner commented on the lack of local sports coverage in the Chronicle. Commissioners involved with local sports groups will encourage coaches to provide information.
