Boys and Girls Club (B&G)

The Boys and Girls Club of Central Vancouver Island (BGCCVI) serves communities in

Civic Square

Ladysmith has successfully ‘branded’ itself as a town with strong heritage preservation

residents arriving by foot or by vehicle.

By integrating the development of the Civic Square with the other elements on the site,
the Civic Square will serve to visually and physically link facilities and activities. People
moving from one part of the site to another, or those who simply wish to spend time to
enjoy the spectacular views, will make good use of this legacy.

Ladysmith. The site slopes towards the water, and is presently graded to form three
benches starting at Second Avenue. Each bench is approximately one storey above the
other. Due to its slope and height, views from the site are panoramic, spanning an arc
including southern views of the Gulf Islands towards Nanaimo to the North.
