Town of Ladysmith Curbside Collection Program Notice of Changes Effective May 20, 2014 As of May 19 th, some types of the materials accepted by our curbside recycling program will change, coinciding with a shift in how the program is funded. New products accep ted for recycling include clean paper drink/coffee cups, milk and soup cartons, aerosol cans and plant pots. Plastic bags and film plastics (such as bread bags and kitchen wrap) will no longer be accepted at the curbside. However, plastic bags and some types of film plastic will be accepted at Encorp Return-it Centres and CVRD recycling depots. This change is happening because the BC Government now requires that businesses producing and supplying packaging and printed paper to residents also be responsible for collecting and recycling these materials. As a result, the costs for collection and recycling will shift away from taxpayers and local governments and back to the businesses that produce the materials. Multi-Material BC (MMBC) is a non-profit organization working on behalf of those businesses. Starting on May 19 th, 2014 MMBC will be responsible for residential recycling programs in most areas of BC including the Town of Ladysmith. MMBC will pay for our recycling program by charging its member businesses a small recycling fee for each item they produce, and this money in turn will be passed on to the Town of Ladysmith who will continue to provide curbside collection. The payments provided by MMBC for this service will go directly back into our curbside program. The allocation of MMBC payments will be reviewed once actual funds are recovered later in the year. MMBC is one of more than 20 residential stewardship programs that have been introduced in BC over the past two decades. Through these programs, industry is directly responsible for the end-of-life management of several consumer products including beverage containers, electronics, paint, used oil, tires and batteries. Stewardship programs provide a way for industry to manage environment impact during every stage of a product’s lifecycle, from production to collection and recycling. What do these changes mean for me? The only variation in your service will be the slight change to the items that can or cannot be placed in your yellow bag as of May 20th. Please note that some of the information on the yellow bags will no longer be current. Your day-to-day service will not be affected and your collection day and frequency will remain the same.
