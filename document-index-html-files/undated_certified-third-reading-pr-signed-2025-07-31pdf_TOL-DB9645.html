COWICHAN VALLEY REGIONAL DISTRICT

ByYLAw No. 4650

A Bylaw to Establish and Operate the Extended Service of Regional Emergency
Management for the Municipalities and Electoral Areas
of the Cowichan Valley Regional District.

The Board of Directors of the Cowichan Valley Regional District in open meeting enact as follows:
CITATION

1. This Bylaw may be cited for all purposes as "CVRD Bylaw No. 4650 - Regional
Emergency Management Establishment Bylaw, 2025".

INTERPRETATION

2. In this Bylaw:

(1) "Board" means the Municipal Directors and Electoral Area Directors of the
Cowichan Valley Regional District;

(2) "CAO" means the Chief Administrative Official for the municipality or regional
district.

(3) "Chairperson" means that person elected by the Board as Chairperson;

(4) "Phases" means the emergency management phases as defined by the
Emergency and Disaster Management Act as amended.

(5) "Regional Emergency Management Plan" means the document that guides
the roles and responsibilities, and, sets out the actions for the Regional
Emergency Management program.

(6) "Regional Emergency Operations Centre" includes the team, resources or
location that coordinates and supports the response and initial recovery to
emergencies coterminous with the Service Area Boundaries.

SERVICE BEING ESTABLISHED

3. The service being established is pursuant to Section 332 (1) of the Local Government
Act as amended, and the Emergency and Disaster Management Act as amended.

4. This Bylaw is to establish a Regional Emergency Management Service in and for the
participating members; to be known as the "Regional Emergency Management
Service".

SERVICE AREA BOUNDARIES

5. The boundaries of the service area shall be coterminous with the boundaries of the
Cowichan Valley Regional District.
