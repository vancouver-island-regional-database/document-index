WHAT IS PRIMARY HEALTH CARE? Primary health care includes your first and most frequent contact with the health system, such as regular check ups with a family physician, health promotion, screening tests, treatment, vaccinations, prenatal care, home visits, nutritional counseling, some mental health services and palliative care. Primary health care providers include family physicians, nurses, nutritionists, midwives, long-term care providers, mental health practitioners, physiotherapists and psychologists. WHY IS THE FAMILY PRACTICE CLINIC USING A NEW MODEL OF CARE? In the past, many health care providers dealt with different parts of patient care. This approach made it difficult for people trying to move between different services, particularly those with chronic health problems. Research shows that more coordinated primary health care services can improve health and are more cost effective. So Vancouver Island Health Authority is redesigning primary health care to: • Improve people’s health and well being • Improve access to physician services and interdisciplinary team care • Improve continuity of care • Improve prevention and coordinate care, especially for people with chronic conditions such as diabetes and heart disease • Improve integration with hospitals and other types of care, and help people navigate through these services • Reduce use of hospital emergency services by providing better community-based care Our goal is to ensure you receive the right care, at the right time, in the right place. HOW DOES THE LADYSMITH FAMILY PRACTICE CLINIC PROVIDE THIS NEW MODEL OF CARE? Improved Access The Family Practice Clinic is now open 2 evenings a week and alternate Saturdays. Initiatives are in place or being developed to increase availability of Care Providers. Electronic registry We’ve created a new electronic registry to better coordinate your care between providers and to help schedule check up visits that will prevent complications in patients with chronic illnesses. 2005/09/13

Nurse/Nurse practitioner The Family Practice Clinic will first have a Registered Nurse join the team who will be able to provide care that does not need a Physician’s skill level but is within an RN’s scope of practice. We also plan to hire a Nurse Practitioner as soon as the legislation is passed in BC. The Nurse Practitioner can diagnose and treat patients with referral to Physicians for complex conditions.The Nurse providers will work with the Physicians and other team members to help manage care for patients with chronic diseases, particularly with health education and promotion; provide specific care such as injections which will free up the Physicians’ time for more complex cases or better access for patients; and assist in coordinating patient care with other care providers. A new way of paying for doctors’ services Traditionally, doctors have been paid a fee for each patient visit, called fee-for-service. A new payment method blends an annual fee for each registered patient, based on a patient’s age and health care needs. This new system allows physicians at the clinic to involve other health professionals in care, and spend more time helping patients manage complex and chronic conditions. JOINING OUR PATIENT REGISTRY Our team of care providers offer regular medical check-ups, treatments, health promotion information, and referrals to specialists and other services. We are here to support you in leading a healthy lifestyle and working with you to make the best decisions about your health. Our commitment involves mutual trust, respect, cooperation, listening, and consideration for your unique situation. There are two types of relationship you can choose with our practice: • Registered patient • Drop-in or casual patient Why should I register? By registering as a patient with the Family Practice Clinic, we can better coordinate your care, because we have health care providers working as a team. Computerized files enable us to track patients’ conditions, with a recall system that flags the need for follow-up support. This better support for patients with chronic conditions such as diabetes, heart or lung disease and asthma should decrease the need for complications and hospitalization. By managing your care proactively, we hope to reduce the use of emergency rooms and walk-in clinics, where people usually see a different doctor each time. This approach will improve the continuity of your care and reduce costs to the health care system. Our team works together to ensure everyone has a complete picture of your overall health, so you get the care you need when you need it. And we give our patients support and information to manage their conditions and live healthier lives. What’s involved in joining the registry? As a registered patient, we ask you to commit to coming to Family Practice Clinic for all of your primary, non-hospital care, so we can ensure the best continuity of care for you. If your own doctor is not available, you can visit the other physicians at the clinic any time. Just call the Family Practice Clinic to register. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS Will my doctor also be able to see me at home or in the hospital and can I see a Specialist? Our physicians and other health professionals make house calls when necessary, and can visit you in hospitals where they have privileges. We will refer you to a Specialist if needed. This does not impact our funding. We will be inviting some Specialists to be on site. Will my health records be confidential? Your health information is kept confidential by your family physician and all other health care professionals. Information is shared with those who “need to know” in order to provide continuity of care. If you do not want your medical information shared with other care providers, let us know. If I have a health care problem when the Clinic is not open, what do I do? If your health care problem is not an emergency, call the Clinic and leave a message. We will contact you as soon as we open (by 0930 weekdays). If you have a health care emergency call 911 for an ambulance. If you are not sure you could refer to the BC Healthguide publication or call the BC Nurseline at 1-866-215-4700. I have heard something about “group visits”. What does that mean? If you have a chronic condition like Diabetes, you may be invited to a group visit where you will have a check up and education by various health professionals plus the opportunity to share with others who have similar health issues. This has been proven successful in assisting patients to manage their own health.
