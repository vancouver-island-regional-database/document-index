Town of Ladysmith PARKS, RECREATION & CULTURE COMMISSION Minutes of the Commission Meeting held at FJCC Lower Program Room Wednesday, January 19, 2011 at 7:00pm PRESENT: Will Vandergrift, Acting Chair; Glen Popenko; Lisa Bates; Lorena Mueller; John Perry; Brad Uytterhagen; Glen Hammond, Area G Alternate; Duck Paterson, Councillor; Scott Bastian, Councillor, Patrick Durban, Staff; Julie Tierney, Recorder. ABSENT: Chantal Blumel, Chair; Lori Evans, Councillor. AGENDA APPROVAL 2010-23: It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda for the Commission meeting of Wednesday, January 19, 2011 be approved as presented. MINUTES APPROVAL 2010-24: It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes for the Commission meeting held Wednesday, De cember 15, 2010 be approved as presented. DELEGATE Paul Legacy / Geocaching – Paul Legacy explained the worldwide game of geocaching, which involves hiding and seeking treasures, usually of little value, using GPS technology. Paul has organized an event to take place on March 12, 2011 near the end of Watts Road. This is a “Cache In Trash Out” event that will help to remove the garbage dumped in this area. The CVRD will be waiving the tipping fees at the Solid Waste Facility. Participants will also have the opportunity to win prizes, which Paul is hoping the Town will donate something. Will Vandergrift thanked Paul for his presentation. OLD BUSINESS Lot 108 Update – Staff are working on signage for the park. A senior’s men soccer team may be established once again in Ladysmith. Commissioners discussed the naming options for this park. It was decided that the Commissioners will supply to Julie their top 5 possible names for the park, or park and fields. It was also the consensus of the Commission that they would be agreeable to consider, subject to a sizeable contribution on an ongoing basis, a corporate name. Needs Assessment Update – Staff finalizing assessment and determining delivery of survey. Spirit Square Update – progressing and will be completed by March 31, 2011. Gourlay Jane’s Park – Work by the arborist has been completed and are now cleaning the debris. The park will reopen to the public soon. Page 1 of 2
