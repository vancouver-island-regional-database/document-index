DIVISION OF MAINTENANCE RESPONSIBILITIES
TRANS CANADA HIGHWAY #1: WITHIN MUNICIPALITY OF
LADYSMITH

BETWEEN

Town of Ladysmith and Ministry of Transportation

The following document highlights the Arterial Highway maintenance responsibilities,
excluding street lighting and traffic signals, on the Trans Canada Highway #1 within the
Municipal boundaries of the Town of Ladysmith.

The intent of this document is to establish an operational agreement between the Town and
the Ministry, but it should not be interpreted to supercede the Ministry’s policies on this
subject as detailed in the following documents, as they may be modified from time to time:
G23/87 Responsibility and Cost Sharing Guide or the Ministry’s Landscape Policy and
Design Standards.

Ladysmith Maintenance Responsibilities:

e Pavement surfaces of intersecting side streets and frontage roads (patching, crack sealing
and rehabilitation). See Appendix “‘A” (Laning Plans)

e Roadside litter and rubbish removal within the curb and gutter sections of the Trans
Canada Highway (Approximately from Methuen Street to Strathcona Road), also on all
frontage roads and side streets.

e Sweeping the roadway within the curb and gutter sections of the Trans Canada Highway,
frontage roads and side streets.

e Non regulatory signs at the intersections of and are for frontage roads, side streets and
accesses.

¢ Repair and cleaning of sidewalks, pedestrian/wheel chair ramps and paths.

e Repair and cleaning of storm drain systems within the curb and gutter section of the Trans
Canada Highway, frontage roads and side streets (including pipes, catch basins and
manholes). See Appendix “‘B” (Drainage Plans)

e All things relating to the pedestrian underpass located in the vicinity of Methuen Street,
including the access to and from the underpass and signing.

e All landscaping works and associated irrigation systems/costs within the Trans Canada
Highway, adjacent side streets and frontage roads, except for landscaping within the
median of the Trans Canada Highway. Date of take over by the Town shall be the same
as the expiry of the installers maintenance agreement, December 8, 2001.

e Removal of snow plowed from the Trans Canada Highway.

e Plowing of intersecting side streets and frontage roads.

e Repair and maintenance of all sewer and water services.
