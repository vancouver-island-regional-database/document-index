Re: JANITORIAL SERVICES FOR TOWN BUILDINGS
RECOMMENDATION: .
THAT Council award the contract for janitorial services for City Hall, Development
Services and Public Works to ABM Janitorial Services and that the Town of Ladysmith
enter into a two-year contract with ABM Janitorial Services at the rate of $1,592 per
month plus HST. .
INTRODUCTION/BACKGROUND:
Janitorial services for City Hall, Development Services and Public Works are currently
provided by Sheila’s Janitorial Services. Sheila Williams has given notice that she is
retiring effective August 31, 2010. A request for proposals was issued on August 6,
2010 for janitorial services for the three work sites and eight proposals were
received.
The. proposals were assessed on the basis of the following criteria:
°-Fee~:
© Overall company experience
e Staff experience on similar projects
*® Quality & completeness of proposal ;
e Ability to provide ‘green’ supplies and cleaning products
ABM Janitorial is based in Vancouver. The site supervisor is a Ladysmith resident.
ALTERNATIVES:
Council can choose to award the contract to another bidder.
FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS;
The proposed fee is comparable to what the Town is currently paying for janitorial
services.
The cost is $1,783 per month, including HST, or $42,792 over two years.
