—= a Co-operation Protocol
Se Between the
Stz’uminus First Nation and Town of Ladysmith

(SFN) (Town)
(The Communities)

LADYSMITH

Whereas:
1) The SFN asserts aboriginal rights and title to its traditional territory; and

2) The Town is a municipality with governance authorities as set out in Provincial legislation;
and

3) The SFN and the Town have mutual interests and shared objectives; and

4) The SFN and the Town are building a working relationship based on mutual respect,
friendship, and trust; and

5) The SFN and the Town wish to further their relationship by entering into this Co-operation
Protocol.

Now Therefore the Parties agree as follows:
1.0 Purpose:

4.4 The purpose of this Protocol is to continue to develop the cooperative
relationship that exists between the Communities and form an agreement to
pursue initiatives of common interest.

2.0 Objectives:

2.1 The objectives of this Protocol are:

(a) to establish an effective government-to-government working relationship
between the Communities and other agencies;

(b) to promote efficiency and effectiveness in the working relationship
between the Communities.

(c) to establish an ongoing policy level and technical level dialogue;

(d) to establish information sharing and cooperative planning process
between the Communities.
