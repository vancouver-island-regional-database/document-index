MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Thursday, March 11, 2010 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall PRESENT: Chair – Maureen Martin; Members – Bruce Laxdal, John Hinde, Ann Rogers, Peter Blumel; Council Liaison – Lori Evans; Mayor Rob Hutchins; Planning Technician & Recorder – Stephanie Blumensaat ABSENT: Member – Jennifer Lush The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:01 p.m. 1. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES a. Traveller’s Hotel Mayor Hutchins joined the meeting at 7:45 p.m. Mayor Hutchins responded to Members questions regarding potential reuse of the Traveller’s Hotel. Community Heritage Register Update Project Council Member L. Following the Council meeting, Planning Technician, S. Blumensaat forwarded the required documentation to the Heritage Branch and building owners. b. Heritage Building Brochure Update Members discussed styles of heritage brochures and building plaques. Staff provided information regarding the To wn’s Signage Plan.
