The Cowichan Valley Regional Hospital District

Capital funding of the new hospital build is
cost-shared, with the province funding 60%
and the CVRHD funding 40%.

Since 2010, the CVRHD has been reserving
funds to meet its 40% contribution.

Construction began in 2022 and it is
anticipated that the hospital will be ready to
welcome the first patients into the new
facility in 2027.
