Cultural Awareness Workshop

C. Barfoot provided an overview of the Poverty Reduction Strategy - Cultural
Awareness Workshop, noting the differences between the pyramid structure
of Colonial governance compared to the family structure of First Nation
governance.

Park Implementation Plan - Confirm priorities and phasing

The Committee reviewed and provided feedback regarding the priorities and
phasing of the Park Implementation Plan.
