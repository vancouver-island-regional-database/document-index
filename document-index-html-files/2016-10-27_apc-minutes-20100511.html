MINUTES Advisory Planning Commission (APC) Tuesday, May 11, 2010 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall LADYSMITH PRESENT: Chair – Cyndi Beaulieu; Members – Katherine Turner , Pamela Fraser, Evelyn Kaatz, John Wilson; Staff Liaison – Felicity Adams, Director of Development Services and Recorder. ABSENT/REGRETS: Member - Douglas Blake; Council Liaison – Scott Bastian. The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. It was announced that Marsh Stevens has resigned from the APC. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda of May 11, 2010 be approved. 2. ADOPTION OF MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the APC minutes of April 13, 2010 be adopted. 3. COUNCIL REFERRALS See 4 a. 4. OFFICIAL COMMUNITY PLAN AMENDMENT APPLICATION a. Town of Ladysmith Zoning Bylaw Amendment – Accessory Buildings F. Adams presented photographs of existing one storey accessory buildings in Ladysmith. Members discussed current regulations in Ladysmith for accessory buildings. The APC provided the following recommendation to Council: It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Planning Commission concurs with Council’s recommended new regulations for accessory buildings in residential zones: (1) setback regulations for accessory buildings in residential zones as outlined in section 5.5, (2) limit the height of an accessory buil ding in residential zones to one storey (5.0m), and (3) limit the maximum combined gross floor area of an accessory building in residential zones to 50m2. 5. CORRESPONDENCE None. 6. NEW BUSINESS None.
