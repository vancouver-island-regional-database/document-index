TOWN OF LADYSMITH
BYLAW NO. 1895

A Bylaw to amend the Ticket Information Utilization Bylaw to include fines associated with
Nuisance Abatement Bylaw No. 1893 and Property Maintenance Bylaw No. 1894.

WHEREAS the Community Charter empowers the Council, by bylaw, to authorize the use of
any word or expression on a municipal ticket information to designate an offense against a
bylaw;

AND WHEREAS the Community Charter empowers the Council, by bylaw, to establish
penalties in relation to an offence against a bylaw;

AND WHEREAS it is deemed appropriate to amend “Ticket Information Utilization Bylaw
2002, No. 1457”;

NOW THEREFORE the Council of the Town of Ladysmith in open meeting assembled enacts
as follows:

1. Schedule 1 of “Ticket Information Utilization Bylaw 2002, No. 1457” is hereby amended

by adding the following:

Designated Bylaw
Designated Bylaws Enforcement Officers
12. Nuisance Abatement Bylaw 2015, No. 1893 Peace Officer

Bylaw Enforcement Officer

13. Property Maintenance Bylaw 2015, No. 1894 Peace Officer
Bylaw Enforcement Officer

following “Schedule “13” as follows:
