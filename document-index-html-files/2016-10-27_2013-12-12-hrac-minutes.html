Community Heritage Register Update A copy of the completed Statements of Significance (SOS’s) for the 13 selected heritage buildings were provided to HRAC members. Vincent reviewed the next steps i n updating the Community Heritage Register, including notification of building owners, approval by Council, and reporting to the BC Register for Historic Places. c. Heritage Week 2014 B. Knol reported that the Ladysmith Maritime Society (LMS) will be organizing the Heritage Week event. The event will be held on Saturday, February 2 2, 2014 and will feature the new Harbour Heritage Centre, the Maritime Museum, and heritage boats, along with activities for children and warm refreshments. A. McDermid will work with the Chronicle and Take 5 to ensure there are relevant articles advertising the Heritage Week event. B. Knol will draft a notice to be included in the Town’s utility bill, whether it be a paragraph within the newsletter or a separ ate in sert. External communication will be reviewed by Corporate Services prior to publication. J. Robinson and T. Hutchinson volunteered to coordinate a potential design and quote for a large coroplast sign advertising Heritage Week . Options will be presented at the January 9, 2014 HRAC meeting. Members recommended that LMS should pursue the placement of an event advertisement hand -out to local schools. 6. NEW BUSINESS a. Machine Shop Opportunity A. McDermid reported that there is a group of residents who are interested in the lease of space at the Machine Shop on Oyster Bay Drive. The space would be used for the purpose of restoring and maintaining heritage rail and machine artifacts, potentially including the Dunsmuir Coa ch and Locomotive #11. The group is looking for additional volunteers to assist with the administration and formation of a society, and also support for the project. Members invited the group to make a presentation to HRAC. 7. CORRESPONDENCE a. Heritage BC Quarterly: Fall 2013 A limited number of copies of the latest Heritage BC Quarterly were sent to HRAC. Members were asked to share the copies amongst members. This edition includes an article on the restoration of the MV Saravan by LMS. b. BC Association of Heritage Professionals A letter was sent by the BC Association of Heritage Professionals (BCAHP) to notify the Commission of the BCAHP’s mandate, and to request that HRAC hire individuals who are accredited through the association.
