Previously Recreation Facilities Worker I November 2007 TOWN OF LADYSMITH JOB DESCRIPTION TITLE: FACILITIES MAINTENANCE WORKER I DEPARTMENT: PARKS, RECREATION & CULTURE JOB SUMMARY: Reporting to the Facilities Maintenance Supervisor, this position is responsible for taking various mechanical readings and reporting any unusual items. The tasks performed by the Facilities Maintenance Worker I primarily involve custodial maintenance. PRIMARY DUTIES & RESPONSIBILITIES: • Maintains a high degree of cleanliness of Parks, R ecreation & Culture Department facilities; • Makes routine equipment checks and reports any pro blems to the Facilities Maintenance Supervisor; • Opens various Parks, Recreation & Culture faciliti es and prepares them for use by the public; • Locks up Parks, Recreation & Culture facilities an d ensures that they are safe and secure; • Sets up and takes down equipment as required by us er groups; • Performs regular water tests and adjusts chemicals accordingly to meet Health Act and Parks, Recreation & Culture Department standards; • Other duties as required. REQUIRED SKILLS, KNOWLEDGE & ABILITIES: • Completion of Grade 10 or equivalent; • Pool Operator I; • BC Drivers Licence; • Ability to work with community groups, organizatio ns and individuals utilizing Parks, Recreation & Culture facilities; • Ability to work with custodial equipment including floor burnishers and mechanical floor scrubbers; • Previous experience in custodial work is an asset; • A team player, highly collaborative and collegial; • Stamina, energy, and willingness to commit to qual ity pro-active results; • Ability to work with minimal supervision and be se lf-motivated; and • A personal commitment to service, ability to provi de friendly, helpful, knowledgeable, and timely assistance.
