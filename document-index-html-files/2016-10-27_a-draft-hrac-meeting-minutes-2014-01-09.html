MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Thursday, January 9, 2014 at 5:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall PRESENT: Chair – Jennifer Robinson ; Members – Maureen Martin, Gerry Beltgens, Tamara Hutchinson ; LDHS Member - Bernardien Knol; Council Liaison – Councillor Bill Drysdale; Staff Liaison and Recorder – Angela Vincent REGRETS: Marnie Craig, Allen McDermid, Ann Rogers GUESTS: Shirley Blackstaff, Harry Blackstaff, Nita Grant, John Plecas, Councillor Duck Patterson The meeting was called to order at 5:05 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of January 9, 2014 be approved. 2. WELCOME/INTRODUCTION Chair J. Robinson invited guests to introduce themselves. 3. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of December 12, 2013 be adopted with the following changes: Correct the date of the Heritage Week event from February 27, 2014 to February 22, 2014. 4. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES None. 5. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS a. Doug Routley MLA – 16 High Street Members reviewed the proposed fascia and projecting signage. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission approve the sign proposal for Doug Routley MLA for 16 High Street. 6. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES a. Heritage Week 2014 B. Knol provided an update for the Heritage Afloat event, to be held on February 22, 2014 and coordinated by the Ladysmith Maritime Society (LMS). B. Knol has submitted event information for the Town’s newsletter , to be distributed to residents with utility bills in January.

Community Heritage Register Update A. Vincent reviewed the next steps to be undertaken for the Community Heritage Register update, including notification of building owners and a report to Council. Members supported the Statements of Significance provided at the December HRAC meeting. 7. NEW BUSINESS a. Industrial Heritage Preservation/Proposal for Machine Shop Lease Presentation Shirley Blackstaff presented on behalf of a group proposing to lease the Machine Shop space for the purpose of restoring and displaying industrial heritage artifacts. The group has formed a core committee and will operate through the Ladysmith & District Historical Society. S. Blackstaff outlined the objectives of the group, including revitalizing artifacts from Ladysmith’s industrial history, providing an educational experience for visitors and residents, and involving the community in helping to build a sense of place. After discussion with the delegation, HRAC members determined that Chair J. Robinson will write a support letter for the group to be sent to Council. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission write a letter in support of the group proposing to lease the Machine Shop for the purpose of industrial heritage preservation. b. Advisory Design Panel – Downtown Development Permit Area Guidelines Chair J. Robinson presented a report on behalf of member A. Rogers, that summarized the December 16, 2013 Advisory Design Panel meeting regarding Downtown Development Permit Area guidelines. The report included highlights of the guidelines, and acknowledged that the guidelines would be beneficial for heritage. Copies of the Development Permit Area guidelines were distributed to HRAC members, to be reviewed at the next meeting.
