POSITION DESCRIPTION TITLE: CERTIFIED UTILITY OPERATOR II DEPARTMENT: WATER AND WASTEWATER –TREATMENT DIVISION: INFRASTRUCTURE SERVICES CATEGORY: UNION CUPE LOCAL 401 BAND: 14 GENERAL ACCOUNTABILITY Reporting to the Utilities Supervisor - Treatment, the Certified Utility Operator II –Treatment is responsible for making operational decisions in the operation and maintenance of the Town’s Water and Wastewater treatment plants and Compost Facility, in accordance with Town policy and regulatory guidelines to produce treated water and wastewater that exceeds all compliance and permit obligations . PRIMARY DUTIES AND ACCOUNTABILITIES o Operating and maintaining all mechanical equipment and instrumentation within the water and wastewater treatment plants, compost facility, and water supply system. o Maintenance related cleaning of primary and secondary wastewater treatment processing equipment. o Maintenance related cleaning of intake screens and DAF process equipment at the water treatment plant. o May be required to assist in the e xcavation, pipework installation (to lines and grades as directed), backfilling, and compaction of trenches for construction of sewer and water mains and services. o May be required to assist in the c onstruction of sewer manholes and the installation of wat er valves curb stops, hydrants, bends and fittings; including thrust blocks. o Maintaining daily, monthly and annual records regarding the Water and Wastewater Treatment Plants in accordance with Federal and Provincial Regulations. o Performing laboratory tests as required for quality and process control. o Assists in the processing of bio-solids and composting processes. o Ensures the efficient operation of waste water and water treatment facilities and equipment through regular inspection and operation checks. o Monitors water / wastewater treatment plant operation via taking samples, analyzing results and , adjusting flows and chemical additions as required. o Under the direction of the Utilities Supervisor, ensures that all permit requirements and maintenance objectives are met. o Adhere to safety policies and regulations as established by WorkSafe BC, the Occupational Health and Safety committee, the Town of Ladysmith and regional and municipal statutes. o Assists in the development of safety protocols and efficient work practices. o Respond to call-out for emergency maintenance and repair work on short notice and ensure availability to support various other operations in fulfilling call-out requirements. o Required to work weekends and shift work as scheduled and overtime as directed. o Assists in the training of new employees to ensure familiarity with safety regulations, safe work practices and the correct and safe use of all tools and equipment necessary to perform their duties. o Conducts facility tours for the general public. o Responsible to document various goods and materials upon removal from inventory to ensure current supply levels are reflected accurately. o May be required to procure and purchase goods and materials associated with the daily mainte nance of the Water and Wastewater department. o Attends professional development training as directed and to maintain EOCP licensing.

o Required to participate as a member of the Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) standby group and fulfills stand-by shift requirements on a rotating basis. o May be required to assist with collection and distribution duties as deemed necessary to fulfill operational demands. o Maintains courteous and professional interactions with the public, user groups and Town e mployees. o Other related duties as required.
