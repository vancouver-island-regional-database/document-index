ACCESSIBILITY PLAN The Committee discussed preferred aspects of the accessibility plans from

DISCUSSION the City of Duncan, the City of Parksville, and the Municipality of North
Cowichan. They underscored the importance of using the accessibility plan
to move from a “back door approach” of offering accommodation, to an
explicit and welcoming approach, where Universal Design is considered in
the design or renovation of Town-owned public spaces. Staff advised that
they would bring a draft compilation of the recommendations to the next
meeting for the committee’s review.
