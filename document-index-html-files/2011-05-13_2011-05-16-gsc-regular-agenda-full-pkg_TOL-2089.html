PURPOSE: To obtain Counci) authorization to tender the construction of projects that are required as part of the Town’s Operating Permit for the water supply system, without funding assistance from a Government Grant. NTRODUCTION/BACKGROUND: In order to ensure a safe and adequate water supply for residents in the Town of Ladysmith for now and the future, the Town embarked on a plan to upgrade its water system based on a report prepared by Earth Tech (Canada) Inc. in 2003. Part of the Town’s water supply strategy included the reduction of peak water demands by implementing water conservation strategies, the first of which was the installation of water meters throughout the entire Town. This strategy effectively reduced the Town’s water demand from 1,720,171 cubic metres in 2002 to 1,313,950 cubic metres in 2010. This 23.62% decrease in water consumption was achieved despite a population increase of 1398 or 20.42%. In 2008, the Town completed the first of the improvements recommended by the report with the construction of the enclosed Arbutus Reservoir. The new higher reservoir location now allows the Town to supply the south end from one location. This in turn allows the Town to eliminate the chlorination station in the south end and combine under a centralized treatment plant at Arbutus. Combining the water supply distribution point at one location will eliminate the need for a second reservoir at the south end, will reduce future treatment costs and will | help to reduce the Town's carbon footprint. The elimination of the south end chlorination station will provide the ideal location for the Town’s first in-line turbine which will assist with reducing the line pressure while creating electricity that can be | sold back to B.C. Hydro. A separate report regarding alternative power was received : by Council in October, 2010. f2 4 | i i i | The Town has also received notification from the Vancouver Island Health Authority i (VIHA) of a new 4-3-2-1 regulation which identifies the level of treatment required for surface water providers. VIHA also requires that all municipalities put into effect a compliance program for implementation, including timelines. In December 2008, Council adopted a 5 year Capita! Plan which would bring the Town into compliance with the regulations. The Town has received a new operating permit for operating a water system from VIHA with a number of conditions attached to bring the Town’s system into compliance with the new 4-3-2-1 regulations. Under the new conditions | set out in the permit, the Town must meet some standards immediately and others | must be achieved by set dates as identified in the 5 year Capital Plan. In January 2009, an application for a ‘Towns for Tomorrow’ grant for the construction ' of a centralized treatment facility to a maximum of $375,000 was submitted to the Province, but not approved. In March of 2009, a second intake of the Canada/British Columbia Building Canada Fund was announced. An application was submitted for the construction of a dual pipeline from the south end to Arbutus, a centralised treatment facility, a new power supply and a pipeline from Holland Lake to Stocking . Lake. In January, 2010, we were officially notified that we were unsuccessful in obtaining the grant. Numerous discussions have taken place between the Town and senior government in . an effort to obtain funding. A new application has been submitted as a “shovel ready” project for the dual pipeline betweén the south end and Arbutus reservoir, the centralized chlorination and the new power supply. The total estimated cost for this : : portion of the work is $2,478,000. Council authorized s to complete the detailed design for all three projects at the regular Council meeting on March 8, 2010. The detailed design is now completed. The Town did not receive early approval for the above grant, but is still under consideration for funding. . A letter from VIHA (attached) requires a revised 5 year capital plan for our drinking water supply as the completion dates in our Operating Permit have not been met. A new Operating Permit was issued on February 14, 2011 with alternative implementation dates for the construction to be completed. SCOPE OF WORK: . Go to tender for the construction of the pipeline from the South end to the Arbutus Reservoir, phase | of the centralized treatment facility, and the new power supply for the treatment facility.
