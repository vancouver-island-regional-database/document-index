Enhancing the Aesthetics of the Ladysmith Skatepark
K. Janzen presented the project proposal to enhance the aesthetics of the

Ladysmith Skatepark, including:
e Paint the Skatepark to promote a more vibrant space conductive to
gathering and active outdoor activity;
e Showcase artistic talent for both skatepark users and community
members; and
e Engage skatepark users in enhancing the space.

Next steps are to submit the proposal in phases to School District 68 and the
Public Arts Committee for review and feedback.

2024-12:

That the Parks Recreation & Culture Advisory Committee support enhancing
the aesthetics of the Ladysmith Skatepark project in principle.

Motion Carried
