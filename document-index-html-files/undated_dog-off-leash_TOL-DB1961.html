Py, Park Rules

+ Dogs must have current licence displayed on collar
+ Dogs must be leashed while entering and exiting the dog off leash area (no spike, prong or
pinch collars)
Dog waste must be immediately picked up, bagged and disposed of in provided container
Owners are personally liable for damages or injuries inflicted by their dogs
Dogs must be in control by owner, within sight and be under voice command at all times
Owners must have leash readily available
Children under the age of 13 must be accompanied by an adult and supervised at all times
Agressive dogs, dogs in heat, sick dogs and puppies under 4 months old are not permitted
