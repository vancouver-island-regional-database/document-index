MINUTES OF THE ACCESSIBILITY ADVISORY COMMITTEE MEETING

COUNCIL CHAMBERS, CITY HALL

Sue Bouma

CALL TO ORDER AND Ms. Bouma called the Accessibility Advisory Committee meeting to order at
ACKNOWLEDGEMENT 7:00 p.m., recognizing with gratitude that it was taking place on the unceded
territory of the Stz'uminus First Nation.

AGENDA APPROVAL Moved and seconded:
That the agenda for the January 24, 2024 meeting of the Accessibility
Advisory Committee be approved as presented.
Motion carried.

INTRODUCTIONS Members of the Committee introduced themselves and communicated
their connection to accessibility.

PRESENTATION AND Staff made a presentation outlining accessibility legislation, the role of the

TERMS OF REFERENCE Committee, and best practices for orderly meetings. The Accessibility

REVIEW Advisory Committee Terms of Reference were also reviewed and
discussed.

ELECTION OF CHAIR Moved and seconded:

That the Committee appoint Diane Hobelaid as Chair of the Accessibility
Advisory Committee.

Motion carried.
DISCUSSION AND Staff provided copies of the Official Community Plan, the Accessibility
QUESTIONS Advisory Committee Terms of Reference, and accessibility reviews of the

Frank Jameson Community Centre and Transfer Beach.

The Committee discussed potential alternate meeting locations. Staff will
review the options and advise the committee.

NEXT MEETING Next meeting date and location to be provided to the Committee via email.
