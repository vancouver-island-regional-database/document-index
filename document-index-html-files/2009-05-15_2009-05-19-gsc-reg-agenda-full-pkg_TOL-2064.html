INTRODUCTION/BACKGROUND: On February 2, 2009 Council gave 1st, 24 and 34 readings to “Town of Ladysmith Cemetery Management Bylaw 2009, No.1668”. As part of the bylaw review process, staff also examined the fees and charges associated with the bylaw. The current rates have been in place since 2001. The cemetery has 3055 full burial plots and 2630 of those are occupied. There are 244 cremation plots of which 99 are occupied. Over the past five years, the Town has averaged 34 burials annually, consisting of 12 fuil burials and 22 cremations. One of the changes in the Cemetery Management Bylaw increases the capacity of each plot in the cemetery from one full burial and up to four cremated remains to one full burial and up to eight cremated remains. Essentially, each plot can now accommodate up to nine individuals. The bylaw also introduces columbaria to the cemetery. An increase in cremation inurnments is anticipated in light of the changes to the bylaw. | SCOPE OF WORK: N/A ALTERNATIVES: Council can choose to maintain fees at current levels. When the wood from the two original poles was found to be too rotten to use, the third donated log was.cut up to make the decking for the burling pond. When crews attempted to rebuild the decking with the new lumber, it was discovered that the base and cross members were also starting to show signs of rot. Herold Engineering was engaged to assess and design a new base for the burling pond cover. The estimate for the material for the base and the cost to construct is $7,900. | There is a total of $8,593 left in the Amphitheatre project from the original construction.
