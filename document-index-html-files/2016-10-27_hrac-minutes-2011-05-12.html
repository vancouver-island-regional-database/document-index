MINUTES Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) Thursday, May 12, 2011 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall PRESENT: Chair – Ann Rogers; Members – Brian McLaurin, Allen McDermid, Tamara Hutchinson, John Hinde, and Peter Blumel; Alternate Council Liaison – Lori Evans; Planning Technician & Recorder – Stephanie Blumensaat. ABSENT: Council Liaison – Mayor Rob Hutchins, Member – Jennifer Robinson The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:08 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of May 12, 2011 be approved. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of April 14, 2011 be approved. 3. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES a. Friends of Morden Mine S. Blumensaat confirmed that a letter was sent to Friends of Morden Mine in follow- up to their presentation to the HRAC. A. Rogers will inform them that HRAC Member, A. McDermid, will act as the HRAC liaison. 4. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES a. Ladysmith & District Historical Society (LDHS) Liaison Amendment - “Add the following section 2.2(d): 1 non-voting member from the Ladysmith and District Historical Society to act as liaison between the two groups.” (Bylaw #1760) L. Evans reported that the above amendment to Bylaw 1760 had gone before Council. She will report back confirming whether a letter was sent to the Ladysmith and District Historical Society. b. Ladysmith Museum It was moved, seconded and carried that the matter of the proposed Ladysmith Museum as outlined in the correspondence from the Ladysmith and District Historical Society dated April 5, 2011, be referred to the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission and to the Parks, Recreation and Culture Commission. Members reviewed the letter from Ladysmith and District Historical Society (LDHS) and discussed the idea of a Ladysmith mu seum. L. Evans stated that a community museum is on Council’s list of priorities in terms of strategic direction. Staff noted that the establishment of a museum is in alignment with 7.4 of the Heritage Strategic Plan. Members agreed the Town should play the central role in the establishment of a museum and that key partners should include LDHS, Ladysmith Maritime Society, the Stz’uminus First Nation, local businesses and other Town commissions/committees. Members emphasized the importance of inviting the Stz’uminus First Nation to be involved.

Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission May 12, 2011 2 It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission provide Council its enthusiastic support for the idea of establishing a museum in Ladysmith. 5.
