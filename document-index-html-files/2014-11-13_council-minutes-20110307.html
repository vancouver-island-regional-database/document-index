                        – 11 & 17 Warren Street
     2011-115           It was moved, seconded and carried that staff be directed to

                        Bylaw.

     2011-116           It was moved, seconded and carried that staff be directed to

                        01 Pabla) through density bonusing for amenities, as follows:
                        • To construct an energy efficient building that meets the
                            ASHRAE 90.1 standard;
                        • To secure the December 2010 Ellins Architect Inc. site plan
