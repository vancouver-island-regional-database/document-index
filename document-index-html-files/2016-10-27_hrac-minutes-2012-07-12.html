Heritage Building Condition Inventory Members reviewed the recommendations made in the Heritag e Building Condition Inventory. The inventory is divided into “Phase 1: Current Heritage Buildings” - those included in the Ladysmith Heritage Inventory (R.Goodacre, 1990), and “ Phase 2: Future Heritage Buildings” - downtown heritage buildings not included i n the Ladysmith Heritage Inventory. The Heritage Building Condition I nventory recommend s that all buildings contained in Phase 1 (all buildings in the Ladysmith Heritage Inventory) be accepted into the Ladysmith Community Heritage Register. Members discussed the benefit of having more buildings on the Community Heritage Register, including ease of determining eligibility for heritage grants. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission support an update to the Ladysmith Community Heritage Register by adding all existing buildings listed in the Ladysmith Heritage Inventory.

Members noted that the muted heritage shades are nice but static, and that brighter colo urs add to the character of the town. Members decided to further discuss the use of heritage col ours at a later date. d. Heritage Façade Grant Staff provided information from the Town of St. Mary’s Planner in regards to their Heritage Façade Painting Grant. Highlights included the need for advertising to existing and new building owners to publicize the program. Members discussed the painting quotes provided by Ladysmith and Nanaimo companies, which indicated a broad range of costs involved in façade painting. The companies were asked to provide quotes for three buildings in Ladysmith representing a range of building styles and sizes. Quotes were provided for the buildings housing the In the Beantime Café, the Ladysmith Hotel, and the Traveller’s Hotel. The companies indicated that costs in Ladysmith were higher due to increased insurance requirements. Based on the quotes provided, members agreed upon the proposed 50% grant to a maximum municipal contribution of $2,500 for eligible façade painting. HRAC makes the following recommendations: Create a heritage façade grant program focused on painting facades Grant amount is the maximum of 50% of the eligible actual projects costs (or the lowest estimate, whichever is the lesser) to a maximum municipal contribution of $2,500. The grant will be matched by an equal contribution by the property owner/applicant. Eligibility determined by building inclusion on the Ladys mith Community Heritage Register and Ladysmith Heritage Inventory. Projects will be eligible to receive one façade painting grant within a 10 - year period. Approval process similar to downtown si gn and heritage system, with reviews provided by HRAC. Potential exists in the future to make amendments to the grant program.
