OFFICIAL COMMUNITY PLAN (OCP)/ZONING BYLAW PROPOSED AMENDMENTS a. OCP Amendment and Rezoning Application 3360-14-02 Subject Properties: Lots 9, 10, 11, 12 & 13, District Lot 139, Oyster District, Plan 7217 Trans Canada Highway – Kevin Goldsbury (KD Gold Enterprises Ltd.) The APC members discussed the proposed application for the subject five properties. Applicant, K. Goldsbury, provided information regarding the application. Planner L. Brinkman responded to APC questions regarding the application. The APC members made the following comments and discussed the following matters in relation to the application:  Highway traffic and safety with regard to the access points for the subject properties.  Safety concerns regarding the Highway and Strathcona Road intersection point.  The proposal could do more to align with the Sustainability Visioning Report.  Comments regarding the site contamination and questions regarding the status of the site clean-up and required environmental approvals.  Questions regarding how lots 9 and 10 would be graded, and that buffering should be provided for adjacent residential properties.  Livability of a suite within the same building as a service station.  The suite should meet the requirements of the B.C. Building Code.  Will this application set a precedent, such that other owners of C-3 zoned parcels will apply to amend the zoning bylaw to permit a suite?  The subject properties are part of the gateway to Ladysmith and should be landscaped and designed with extra care for this reason.  It may be wise to take advantage of the opportunity to improve commercial land at the gateway to Ladysmith.  Would like to avoid creating an unattractive commercial strip along the Trans Canada Highway in Ladysmith. Would like to ensure the character of Ladysmith is maintained including along the Highway.

It was moved and seconded. The Advisory Planning Commission (APC) recommends support for the proposal to amend the Zoning Bylaw to permit one residential suite on the property legally described as lots 11, 12, and 13 District Lot 139, Oyster District, Plan 7217 subject to the suite meeting all the requirements of the B.C Building Code , and subject to environmental approvals being in place prior to residential use of the property . Motion carried. 3 in favour, 2 opposed. It was moved and seconded. The Advisory Planning Commission (APC ) recommends support for the proposal to amend the Official Community Plan and rezone lots 9 and 10 , District Lot 139, Oyster District, Plan 7217 from residential use to commercial use, and that auto sales be permitted as a primary use. Defeated. 2 in favour, 3 opposed. It was moved and seconded. The Advisory Planning Commission (APC) recommends support for the proposal to amend the Official Community Plan and rezone lots 9 and 10, District Lot 139, Oyster District, Plan 7217 from residential use to commercial use. Defeated. 2 in favour, 3 opposed. It was moved and seconded. The Advisory Planning Commission (APC) recommends that lots 9 and 10, District Lot 139, Oyster District, Plan 7217 remain zoned for residential use. Motion carried. 3 in favour, 2 opposed. 5.
