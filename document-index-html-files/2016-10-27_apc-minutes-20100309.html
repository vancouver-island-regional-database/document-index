MINUTES Advisory Planning Commission (APC) Tuesday, March 9, 2010 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall LADYSMITH PRESENT: Chair – Cyndi Beaulieu; Members – Douglas Blake, Katherine Turner, Evelyn Kaatz, John Wilson, Marsh Stevens; Council Liaison – Scott Bastian; Staff Liaison – Lisa Brinkman, Planner and Recorder. ABSENT/REGRETS: Member - Pamela Fraser GUESTS: Guy Bezeau - Seven Links Marketing and Development Ltd., applicant The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda of March 9, 2010 be approved. 2. ADOPTION OF MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the APC minutes of December 15, 2009 be adopted. 3. COUNCIL REFERRALS See No. 4 a. 4. OFFICIAL COMMUNITY PLAN/ZONING BYLAW APPLICATIONS a. OCP Amendment & Rezoning Application 3360-09-07 Fourth Avenue– Seven Links Marketing and Development Ltd. (Proposed Lot 6 of Lot 1, DL 108, Oyster District, Plan 47290) Planner, L. Brinkman presented information regarding the proposed Official Community Plan amendment and rezoning to permit 24 single family lots on proposed Lot 6 of Lot 1, DL 108, Oyster District, Plan 47290. G. Bezeau presented images of a similar styled development ‘Heron’s Wood’ in the Municipality of North Cowichan and answered questions. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Planning Commission recommends support for Bylaw 1708 and 1709, with a recommended amendment to the ‘small lot residential development design guidelines’ as follows: “The front building elevation should not be dominated by the garage and where possible the entrance and veranda should be prominent and the garage recessed.” b. OCP Amendment & Rezoning Proposal 3360-10-01 Christie/Grouhel – Town of Ladysmith (Lot 7 and Lot 8, District Lot 147, Oyster District, Plan VIP85271) Planner, L. Brinkman presented the propos al to amend the Official Community Plan and Zoning Bylaw to permit multi-family development on two town-owned lots at Christie Road and Grouhel Road. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Planning Commission is reluctant to support the proposal to designate and zone Lot 7 and Lot 8, Plan VIP85271 for multi-family use without first seeing the potential form and character of a particular project. The Advisory Planning Commission questions whether or not multi-family use is the best use for the subject properties.
