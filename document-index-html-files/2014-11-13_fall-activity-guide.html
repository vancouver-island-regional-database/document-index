                                                                                                                                                                       Parks
Gourlay-Janes Park
A short hike through a forest leads to a beautiful open space bordering the Ladysmith harbour. This beauti-
ful dog off-leash park is located on Chemainus Road. You will find a great place to fish, swim and take in
the beautiful ocean view, birds, flora & fauna. Recent improvements include a new and improved seawall
with wooden railings. Further work to be completed includes reinstalling the benches and planting grass in
September. As well as the recent construction, local Girl Guides and Parks staff planted 150 native trees and
plants last spring.


Holland Creek Trail
This trail winds 5.8 kilometers along both sides of Holland Creek and
appeals to outdoor enthusiasts of all ages. Highlights include Crystal
Falls and the Colliery Dams. The trail is wheelchair accessible with
assistance from the Methuen / 6th Avenue entrance west to the Upper
Colliery Dam. Enter from Dogwood Drive, Davis Road Park, Mackie
Road, 6th Avenue & Methuen
Public washrooms are found at 6th and Methuen where you will also
find a beautiful interpretive display featuring native flora and fauna
created by local resident, Shirley Blackstaff.
Salmon spawning can be viewed at various spots on the trail each fall.
A new foot bridge constructed Sep 2014 to replace the current bridge,
shown at right.
