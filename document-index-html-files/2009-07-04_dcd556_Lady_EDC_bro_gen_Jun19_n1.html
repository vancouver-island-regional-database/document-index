MAKE IT HAPPEN HERE Ladysmith Economic Development Commission

The business of balance. There is so much more to life than just work. It is a rare thing to find a business community that balances passion and play. Until you find yourself in Ladysmith. This is where spirited, innovative and creative entrepreneurial minds grow roots and sprout wings. Which makes our job a whole lot easier. We are the Economic Development Commission, and we are in charge of creating the best environment for businesses of all kinds. We do things like support businesses to expand, leverage community assets to attract business investments and encourage new sectors to flourish. Ladysmith: Make it happen here.

HH iiss ttoo rryy The Ladysmith area has been home to the Chemainus First Nation for at least 5,000 years. European settlement took hold in earnest when the town of Ladysmith was established to service the nearby Extension coal mine; the town was incorporated in 1904. Coal mining dominated the economy until demand dwindled in the early 1930s. Logging soon took over as the major industry, and it continues to play an integral role in our area’s economy. GG ee oo gg rraa pp hh yy Ladysmith is located right on the 49th parallel, on the east coast of beautiful Vancouver Island. The town is built next to the ocean, just off tranquil Stuart Channel. It boasts a 5-mile natural harbour, and provides excellent boating access to the beautiful Gulf Islands. The mountains which surround it not only offer spectacular views, but also protect it from much of the rain that the rest of Vancouver Island experiences. Our moderate coastal climate is characterized by slight seasonal variations in temperature, and typically receives over 1900 hours of bright sunshine each year – only slightly less than Rio de Janeiro gets!
