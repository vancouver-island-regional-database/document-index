MINUTES Advisory Design Panel (ADP) Tuesday, May 22, 2012 at 1:30 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall LADYSMITH PRESENT: Chair – Ron Kinney; Members - Brian Bancroft; Kaien Shimizu; HRAC Member – Peter Blumel ; Council Liaison – Steve Arnett; Alternate Council Liaison – Gord Horth; Planner & Recorder – Lisa Brinkman ABSENT: Brian Childs GUESTS: Luke Kolk, Applicant The meeting was called to order at 1:35 p.m. The Chair noted that Brian Childs would not attend tonight’s meeting due to a potential conflict of interest with the OCP Amendment and Rezoning application 3360 -12-01 (340-2nd Ave.). 1. AGENDA It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of May 22, 2012 be approved. 2. MINUTES It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of February 13, 2012 be adopted. 3. COUNCIL REFERRALS See 4 a. 4. DEVELOPMENT APPLICATION REFERRALS a. OCP Amendment and Rezoning Application #3360-12-01 Lot B, District Lot 56, Oyster District, Plan VIP65504 (340-2nd Avenue) Luke Kolk (DAWNLEE HOLDINGS) Planner, L. Brinkman gave a brief overview of the proposed OCP amendment and rezoning proposal for 340 2nd Avenue. The ADP asked questions and discussed the proposal. The applicant, Luke Kolk answered member’s questions. General comments from the ADP members included: Support for the proposed siteplan; Concern regarding the building form, ma ssing and elevations which do not offer a neighborhood scale; Concern regarding the combination of vertical and horizontal siding; Building is too tight and should be softened and reworked; That perhaps the third floor balcony is not necessary but that a large window may be better;

Advisory Design Panel May 22, 2012 2 Questions regarding the hard and soft landscaping (parking, sidewalks) onsite and on town boulevard areas; That isometric sketches could assist with portraying the building’s character and relationship to the street. It was mov ed, seconded, and carried that the Advisory Design Panel recommends that t he presentation as received today is accepted in principle subject to reworking certain design elements such as massing, landscaping and materials, and that the design be brought again before the Advisory Design Panel with landscape plans (3360-12-01 - 340 2 nd Ave – Dawnlee Holdings). 5. NEW BUSINESS Member, Brian Bancroft suggested that the ADP members tour the new Ladysmith Maritime Society Visitor Centre on Monday, May 28th. 6. MONTHLY BRIEFING None. 7.
