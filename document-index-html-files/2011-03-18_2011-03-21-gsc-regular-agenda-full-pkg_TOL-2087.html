Ph: (250) 754-9222, Fax: (250) 754-5318, svipmp@sryraillink.com Southern Railway of Vancouver Island Limited (SVI) has prepared a draft Pest Management Plan to control vegetation along the former E&N Railway, using an integrated pest management approach. Vegetation control is required to safely operate and maintain the railway. While mechanical and manual control methods will be primarily used, including mechanical brush cutting and mowing, manual brush cutting and pulling, and ballast cleaning and surfacing, use of the following non-residual herbicides is also proposed: Vantage Plus® (glyphosate) or Roundup® (glyphosate), and Garlon 4° (triclopyr). The proposed application methods for these herbicides include: hi-rail truck with a shrouded spray boom (glyphosate only), backpack sprayer (glyphosate only), stem injection (hack and squirt), basal-bark application, and Sprout-less™ cutter head technology. The pest management activities are to be carried out in the railway right-of- way owned by the Island Corridor Foundation and operated by SVI, including the 225 km-long Victoria Subdivision (from Victoria to Courtenay), the 5 kra-long Wellcox Spur (Nanaimo-area), and associated yards, shops and other facilities. The proposed term of this PMP is for the five year period from June 1, 2011 to May 31, 2016.
