Ladysmith Community Gardens Society (LCGS) Delegation

T. Reynen of the Ladysmith Community Gardens Society provided
background information about the society and discussed how the garden
community functions, some of the challenges it faces, some potential
solutions, and issues to consider for the future. Ms. Reynen completed
her presentation by highlighting the important, multi-generational role
the LCGS plays within the community, as it teaches food awareness, how
to grow food and how to preserve it. She noted that there is a growing
interest within the community and that there are 17 names on the
waiting list for garden plots.
