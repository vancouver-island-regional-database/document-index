REGIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL POLICY Kate Miller, CVRD Manager, Re gional Environmental Policy, presented John Wilson with a gift in recognition of his contributions to the Regional Environmental Commission. Ms. Miller outlined the background of the establishment of the CVRD Environmental Commission three years ago – which was formulated around four broad goals and strategic policy direction. She explained how the Ladysmith Environment Commission was formed to support and enhance the regional directions. The CVRD has goals and a framework and is currently working on an in ventory of the regional environmental status: “State of the Region Environmental Report”. She also outlined that the CVRD is undertaking work on regional water baseline information including Ladysmith Harbour and Holland Creek. LADYSMITH
