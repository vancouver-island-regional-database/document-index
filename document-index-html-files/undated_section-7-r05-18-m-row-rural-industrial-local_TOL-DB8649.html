NOTES:

1. PAVED SURFACE — 75mm ASPHALT (COMPACTED THICKNESS)

2. BASE — 100mm CRUSHED GRAVEL (20mm MINUS)

3. SUB—-BASE — 250mm PIT RUN GRAVEL (75mm MINUS)

4, MOUNTABLE MONOLITHIC CURB AND GUTTER IN ACCORDANCE
WITH STANDARD DRAWING C1

5. EXTEND COMPACTED BASE MATERIAL 0.6m BEHIND AND TO
THE TOP OF CURB WHERE NO SIDEWALK IS INSTALLED

6. DEPTHS OF SURFACING AND BASE GRAVELS ARE MINIMUM
AND IN SOME CASES WILL HAVE TO BE INCREASED TO MEET
MAXIMUM ALLOWABLE BENKELMAN BEAM DEFLECTION.

7. INSTALL CURB DRAIN ON HIGH SIDE OF ROAD
WHERE REQUIRED BY THE ENGINEER

8. SUBJECT TO ENGINEERS APPROVAL SIDEWALK, STREETLIGHT
AND HYDRANT OFFSETS MAY VARY.
