MINUTES Joint Commissions (ADP, APC, HRAC) Thursday, August 9, 2012 at 7:00 p.m. Council Chambers, City Hall LADYSMITH PRESENT: ADP Chair - Ron Kinney; ADP Member Brian Childs; HRAC Chair - Jennifer Robinson; HRAC Member – Peter Blumel; HRAC Member – Allen McDermid; APC Chair – John Wilson; APC Member - Todd Hancock; Council Liaison s – Gord Horth and Duck Paterson; Director of Development Services – Felicity Adams; Planner/Recorder – Lisa Brinkman ABSENT: Kaien Shimizu, Brian Bancroft, Katherine Turner, Pam Fraser, Tina Donovan, Evelyn Kaatz, Tamara Hutchinson, Brian McLaurin, Maureen Martin. GUESTS: President/Applicant – Bruce Findlay; Architect – Curtis P. Miles; Vice President of Lion Rampant – Andrew Abernathy. The meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m. 1. INTRODUCTIONS ADP Chair Ron Kinney kindly agreed to serve in the role of meeting Chair and asked meeting attendees to introduce themselves. 2. COUNCIL REFERRAL – TEMPORARY USE PERMIT APPLICATION (3340-12-01) 11 High Street (Lion Rampant Ventures) a) APPLICANT PRESENTATION (BRUCE FINLAY AND CURTIS MILES) Director, F. Adams gave an overview of t he temporary use permit application for a neighbourhood pub, including micro-brewery, at 11 High Street in Ladysmith. Architect, C. Miles presented the proposed use , design rationale (Scottish pub architecture) and façade improvements for the existing building at 11 High Street. The members of the HRAC, APC, and ADP, reviewed the application and asked questions. Vice President of Lion R ampant – Andrew Abernathy stated that free shuttle service will be provided for customers on weekends. b) TEMPORARY USE PERMIT CONSIDERATIONS It was moved, seconded and carried that the three Commissions (HRAC, APC, and ADP) recommend to Council that Temporary Use Permit application #3340-12-01 is feasible. 1-opposed (J. Wilson) It was moved, seconded and carried that the three Commissions (HRAC, APC, and ADP) recommend to Council that the neighbourhood pub owner should encourage their staff not to park in front of the building and not to take up valuable parking spaces close to the establishment at 11 High Street. 1-opposed (P. Blumel) It was moved, seconded and carried that the three C ommissions (HRAC, APC, and ADP) are concerned about parking issues in downtown Ladysmith and recommend that Council study the issue and develop long term solutions. 1-opposed (B. Childs)
